Point of Sale

/pos invoice.issue

A counter checkout for issuing receipts and invoices on the spot.

What this page is for

POS is for in-person sales. You search for products and services, build a basket, apply VAT, and issue the document — all from one keypad-driven screen designed for speed at the counter.

Layout & controls

  • Product search — “Search products & services…” to add items to the sale.
  • Numeric keypad — enter quantities/amounts, including a VAT rate button (e.g. 24.00 %).
  • Open shift — start a cash-register shift before transacting.
  • Receipt — choose the document type.
  • ISSUE — finalise the sale and produce the document.

Typical flow

  1. Open shift at the start of the day.
  2. Search and add products/services to the current sale.
  3. Adjust quantities and VAT on the keypad.
  4. Press ISSUE to generate the receipt/invoice (transmitted to ΑΑΔΕ where applicable).

POS is part of the Finance suite and is gated by the invoice-issuing capability. Issued documents flow into the Finance records.
