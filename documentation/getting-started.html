Getting Started

Sign in, understand your workspace and role, learn the navigation, and see how credits power AI features.

Signing in

/auth Public

The authentication screen has two tabs — Sign In and Sign Up. To create an account you provide your full name, email and a password; new accounts get a 30-day free trial. You can also continue with Google. A Forgot Password? link sends a reset email.

  • Sign Up — full name, email, password → confirm to create your account.
  • Sign In — email + password, or the Google button for single-click login.
  • After signing in you land on the Dashboard.

Workspaces

Everything you do lives inside a workspace. The workspace switcher sits in the top-right of the header (e.g. “Default Workspace”) and shows your role and plan as a pill. A workspace groups your materials, moodboards, quotes, finance documents, CRM records and team members. You may belong to more than one workspace and switch between them at any time.

Roles & access

What you can see and do depends on your role and the capabilities granted to your workspace. Some areas only appear when you have the matching permission:

AreaTypical requirement
Dashboard, Agent Hub, MoodBoards, DiscoverAvailable to all signed-in users
Quotes, Projects, CRM, Finance, InboxCapability / module entitlement on the workspace
Sales PortalSales-rep capability (otherwise shows “Access Restricted”)
Factory AnalyticsFactory accounts

If a menu item or page isn't visible to you, it's almost always a role/capability gate — ask a workspace administrator to enable the relevant module or grant the capability.

Navigating the app

A horizontal top navigation bar runs across every page. The logo is on the left, the main links are in the centre, and the workspace switcher + your profile menu are on the right. The primary destinations are:

Dashboard Agent Hub Inbox Projects MoodBoards Discover Quotes CRM Finance

Credits & plans

AI-heavy actions (image generation, some scans, presentation sheets, 3D/VR, etc.) consume credits. Your remaining balance and plan are shown in your profile, and you can top up at any time:

  • Account & Billing → Credits tab shows your balance and history.
  • /billing/credits — buy credit packs.
  • /billing/subscriptions — upgrade your plan for higher quotas.

Reading, searching and browsing are free; only the explicit AI/generation actions cost credits, and each such control tells you the cost before you confirm.
