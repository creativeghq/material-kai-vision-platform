Dashboard

/

Your home screen and launch pad — a personalised overview of activity across the platform.

What this page is for

The Dashboard is the first screen after signing in. It orients you with a welcome hero, surfaces AI-generated insights, and gives one-click access to the freshest materials, knowledge, profiles and factories on the platform. It's designed to get you exploring quickly rather than starting from a blank page.

Sections on the page

  • Hero — “Material Intelligence Agent Platform” with a Start Exploring button that takes you into discovery.
  • AI Insights — automatically generated highlights based on recent platform activity.
  • Latest Materials — recently added materials; each chip opens the material. View all jumps to the full library.
  • Latest Knowledge — newest knowledge-base documents.
  • Latest Profiles — recently active community members (designers, consultants, suppliers).
  • Latest Factories — manufacturers and their material counts/categories.
  • Recommended for You — personalised suggestions.

Things you can do

  • Click any material, profile or factory card to open its detail.
  • Use the View all links to jump to the full Knowledge Base or Discover lists.
  • Press Start Exploring to begin a guided discovery flow.
  • Switch workspace or open your profile from the header at any time.

Tip: the fastest way to act on anything you see here is to ask the Agent Hub — e.g. “find tiles like this one” or “add this material to a moodboard”.
