Discover & Network

/discover /network

Explore profiles, factories, products and the surplus marketplace from across the community.

Discover

Discover is the community directory. A single search box (“Search by name, service, location…”) works across four tabs:

  • Profiles — designers, consultants and other members; open a profile to see their services and work.
  • Factory — manufacturers, with their material ranges and categories.
  • Products — browse materials/products across the platform.
  • Marketplace — the surplus / last-stock marketplace (see below).

Surplus Marketplace

The Marketplace tab lists last-stock and surplus items offered across workspaces at 0% commission. You can browse listings and send an inquiry to the seller; the conversation is handled through the Inbox. It's the one place where listings are intentionally visible across workspaces.

Network

/network network.manage

The Network page is where you manage your business relationships and connections on the platform — the people and organisations you've linked with. It's capability-gated (network.manage).

Tip: public profiles you find here are also reachable directly at /u/:userId Public and can be shared with anyone.
