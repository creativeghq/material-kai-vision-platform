Finance & Invoicing

/finance /finance/invoices/:id finance.manage

A full back-office: revenue and profit, receivables and payables, the complete set of financial documents, and Greek e-invoicing.

What this page is for

Finance is the workspace's accounting and billing centre. The Dashboard tab summarises the financial health of the business — revenue, profit, outstanding receivables/payables and a follow-up queue — and the remaining tabs let you create and manage every kind of financial document.

Finance is organised into a wide set of tabs, grouped by purpose:

Receivables & Payables

Track what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers. Both views support search (by supplier/number/reference) and filters such as category and age bucket, so you can chase overdue amounts efficiently. The Follow-ups tab queues items that need action.

Documents

Create and manage the full document set — invoices, receipts, credit notes, recorded payments, expenses, delivery notes and cheques. Documents support editable income/expense categories and inline assignment. Each invoice has its own detail page (/finance/invoices/:id).

Greek e-invoicing (myDATA / ΑΑΔΕ)

Invoices can be transmitted to the Greek tax authority (ΑΑΔΕ / myDATA). This is multi-tenant: each workspace issues under its own VAT identity and authorisation. VAT numbers are validated (via ΑΑΔΕ / VIES) before issuing, reducing rejected submissions.

Reports & planning

Reports gives you financial summaries and exports; Planning and Time & billing support forward planning and billable-time tracking. Customers & Suppliers centralises the parties on your documents.

Point of Sale

For counter sales, the platform includes a dedicated Point of Sale screen that issues receipts and invoices on the spot. The Marketplace tab connects finance to the surplus/last-stock marketplace.

Finance is gated by the finance.manage capability and module entitlement. /settings redirects here — workspace settings live under Finance.
