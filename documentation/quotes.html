Quotes & Requests

/quotes /quotes/:id /quotes/requests

Create, send and manage material quotes — and triage incoming requests from customers.

My Quotes

The main tab lists every quote you've created with a KPI strip across the top — Total Quotes, Pending, Accepted and Drafts. Use New Quote to start one, and Filter to narrow the list by status.

Building a quote

  1. Add line items — catalogue products or custom items — each with quantity and price.
  2. Capture FF&E specifications per item where relevant: room, dimensions, installation requirements and delivery date.
  3. Preview the branded PDF (/quotes/:id/preview) — the items table includes a Room column and a “Specifications & Delivery” section.
  4. Send it to the customer; they open it on a public, token-protected page.

Shortcut: generate a quote directly from a moodboard using its Generate Quote button — the board's products become the line items.

Requests

The Requests tab collects quote requests that come in from customers. From here you review a request and turn it into a quote. Use Filter to focus on what needs attention.

The public quote page

When you send a quote, the recipient opens it at /q/:token Public — no login required. They can review the items, specifications and totals, and accept. See Public & Shared Pages.

Quotes is capability/module-gated. Administrators have additional configuration (status tags, upsells, timeline steps, PDF templates and company/VAT settings) under the admin area, which is outside the scope of this end-user guide.
