Material Compare

/compare

Place materials side by side to evaluate them on the attributes that matter.

What this page is for

The comparison tool helps you make a decision between candidates. Add two or more materials and the page lines them up so you can scan their imagery and specifications together instead of flipping between detail pages.

Using it

  1. Click Add Material and pick an item from the catalogue.
  2. Repeat to add more — they appear as columns side by side.
  3. Compare imagery and attributes; remove any you've ruled out.
  4. Use Back to return to where you came from.

Tip: you can send a shortlist straight into comparison from search results, the Knowledge Base, or a moodboard.
