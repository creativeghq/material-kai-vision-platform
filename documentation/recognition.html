Material Recognition

/recognition

Upload one or more photos and let hybrid AI identify the material and its properties.

What this page is for

Material Recognition turns a photograph into structured knowledge. Point it at a tile, a fabric swatch, a stone sample or a finished surface and it returns the likely material type, colours, textures, finish and other descriptive attributes — and can match the image against the catalogue to surface similar real products.

Using it

  1. Click Choose Files / Select Images (or drag and drop) to add one or more photos.
  2. The platform analyses each image with its vision models.
  3. Review the recognised attributes and any matching catalogue materials.
  4. Act on the results — open a match, add it to a moodboard, or refine in the Agent Hub.

What you get back

  • Material identification — type/category and sub-category.
  • Visual attributes — colours, textures, finish and surface pattern.
  • Similar products — visually-matched items from the catalogue.

“Hybrid AI” means several models work together — visual encoders plus a vision-language analysis — to produce a richer, more reliable result than any single model alone.
