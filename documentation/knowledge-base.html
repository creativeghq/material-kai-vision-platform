Knowledge Base

The searchable public library of materials and documents extracted from supplier catalogues.

What this page is for

The Knowledge Base is the front door to everything the platform knows about materials. Supplier PDFs and catalogues are processed by the platform's extraction pipeline into structured, searchable entries — each with imagery, specifications and descriptive metadata. The page is public, so it doubles as a shareable catalogue for customers.

How search works

Behind the scenes, search blends several signals — the text of a material, its visual appearance, an AI “understanding” of it, and colour/texture/style/material characteristics — so you can find items by what they are and what they look like, not just by keyword. You can search in natural language and filter by the available facets (category, colour, material, finish, and more).

Things you can do

  • Search for materials by name, attribute or description.
  • Filter using facets such as category, colour, material and finish.
  • Open a material to view its imagery, specifications and source.
  • Act on results — add to a moodboard, include in a quote, or compare several at once.

Tip: for conversational or visual queries (“something like travertine but darker”), the Agent Hub runs the same search engine and explains why each result matched.
