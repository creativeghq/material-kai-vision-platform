Free Tools

/tools Public

A set of free, no-login utilities — useful on their own and a friendly on-ramp to the platform.

Heat-pump sizer

/tools/heat-pump Public

Estimate the heat-pump capacity a space needs based on its characteristics — a quick sizing aid for specifiers and homeowners.

Heating cost comparison

/tools/heating-cost Public

Compare the running cost of different heating options so you can see which is most economical for a given scenario.

Price scan

/tools/price-scan Public

Type a product or brand name and get back real retailer prices — minimum, median and maximum across the shops that carry it. A free taste of the platform's full price monitoring.

Mention scan

/tools/mention-scan Public

Enter a brand or product and see recent press mentions and the top outlets talking about it — a preview of the platform's mention monitoring.

Fair-use: the public scans are protected by a daily limit per visitor and a bot check, and identical recent queries are served from cache. Signing up unlocks tracked, ongoing monitoring with alerts via the Agent Hub.
