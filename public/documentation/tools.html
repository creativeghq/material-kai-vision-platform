Free Tools

/tools Public

A small set of free, no-login utilities for sourcing, pricing and specifying. Each one is useful on its own — and a friendly on-ramp to the rest of the platform. Every tool here is also available inside the Agent Hub assistant, so you can run the same calculations and scans by just asking in plain language.

What's here

The hub groups four tools into two kinds:

  • Instant calculators — the Heat-pump sizer and Heating cost comparison. These run entirely in your browser, update live as you type, and have no daily limit and no bot check.
  • Live web scans — Price scan and Mention scan. These call real sources across the web, so they're protected by a small daily allowance, a quick bot check, and short-term caching of identical queries (see Fair use below).

You don't need an account to use any of them. The header shows your remaining free scans for the day; signing in raises the limit and unlocks ongoing, tracked monitoring with alerts.

Heat-pump sizer

/tools/heat-pump Public

A first-pass estimate of the heat-pump capacity (in kW) a space needs, so specifiers and homeowners can sanity-check a quote before committing. You describe the space and the result updates instantly — there's no quota and nothing is sent to a server.

You provide:

  • Heated area (m²) and ceiling height (m).
  • Insulation / building era — from no insulation through to passive-house / nZEB.
  • Climate zone (warmest to coldest), or an advanced override with the exact winter design temperature.
  • Glazing / exposure — how much window area and exposed wall the space has.
  • Heat-emission system — underfloor, fan coils, low-temp radiators or existing radiators, which sets the required flow temperature.
  • Optionally, domestic hot water for a chosen number of occupants.

The result shows a recommended capacity range, the space-heating and (if included) hot-water loads, the effective specific load (W/m²), the flow temperature your chosen emitter needs, and notes on how that choice affects efficiency. It also lists the assumptions behind the figure.

This is a coarse planning estimate, not a substitute for a full thermal-loss study (EN 12831 / TOTEE 20701). Always have a professional confirm the load before ordering — and size close to it, since oversizing a heat pump hurts efficiency.

Heating cost comparison

/tools/heating-cost Public

Compares the annual running cost of several heating options for the same home, so you can see which is most economical for a given scenario. Like the sizer, it runs live in your browser with no daily limit.

You enter the home's area and its energy intensity (kWh/m²·yr, taken from an energy performance certificate), then adjust the unit prices for electricity, gas, oil and wood, plus a heat-pump efficiency figure and the type of air-conditioning. The tool then ranks the methods by yearly cost, highlights the cheapest, and shows how much more each other option would cost.

The comparison covers running cost only — it excludes the price of the equipment, installation and maintenance. Use it to weigh ongoing fuel/energy spend, not total cost of ownership.

Price scan

/tools/price-scan Public

Type any product or brand name and get back real retailer prices from across the web. The scan searches multiple shopping sources and reads product pages directly to verify the price, then summarises the result as lowest, median and highest.

Each row shows the retailer, the live price (with any was/now discount), stock availability, a "verified" mark where the price was confirmed by a direct page read, and a link straight to the listing. A scan typically takes a few seconds and returns several retailers.

This is a free taste of the platform's full price monitoring: once you sign in you can keep tracking a product over time and be alerted to price drops, new retailers and promotions, rather than running a one-off scan. See Tools & Capabilities for what the assistant can do with tracked pricing.

Mention scan

/tools/mention-scan Public

Enter a brand or product and see recent press mentions — news, blogs and editorial coverage from the last 30 days — together with the top outlets talking about it. Each result shows the headline, a short excerpt, the outlet, the publish date and a link to the article.

It's a preview of the platform's mention monitoring: signing in lets you track a subject continuously and get alerted to spikes in coverage, negative sentiment from reputable outlets, and brand-new outlets picking up the story — instead of a single snapshot. The same capability is available to the assistant in Tools & Capabilities.

Fair use & limits

The two web scans (price and mention) call real, paid sources on every run, so they're kept fair with a few light guards:

  • Daily allowance — a small number of free scans per visitor each day, shared across both scan types. The header badge shows how many you have left, and the count resets daily.
  • Bot check — a quick, mostly invisible challenge before a scan runs, to keep automated abuse out.
  • Caching — if someone recently ran the exact same query, you get the cached result instantly, and it doesn't count against your allowance.

The instant calculators (heat-pump sizer, heating cost) have no limit or bot check — they don't touch a server.

Want more than a snapshot? When your free scans run out you'll be offered a free account (which raises the daily quota and lets you save and track scans over time, with alerts) or pay-as-you-go credits. Tracked, ongoing monitoring lives inside the platform — start in the Agent Hub, where the assistant can set up and manage it for you.
