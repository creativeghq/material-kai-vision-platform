Customers & Suppliers is a consolidated view of the parties on your documents, focused on the financial relationship: who you invoice and who you pay. The CRM holds the relationship; this tab is where those same parties surface inside Finance so you can see balances and chase money. It's the screen for whoever runs collections — one list showing what every customer owes and what you owe every supplier.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → the Customers & Suppliers tab.

What's on the screen

One list of every CRM party with finance activity, plus the controls to narrow it and the headline numbers:

  • Stats — two figures at the top keep the big picture in view: Total customers owe (€) and We owe suppliers (€).
  • Filters — narrow the list to All customers/suppliers, and All segments.
  • Search — find a party by Name or email.
  • The party list — every CRM party with finance activity, one row each. Click a row to open the full breakdown for that party.

How to email a customer statement — step by step

  1. Open Finance → Customers & Suppliers — every CRM party with finance activity appears in one list.
  2. Narrow the list with the filters (All customers/suppliers, and All segments) or search by Name or email.
  3. Click a row to open the full breakdown for that party.
  4. From there, email a statement — the running total of what they owe.

Worked example — chasing an overdue customer. Open Finance → Customers & Suppliers → search the customer by name or email → click their row to see the full breakdown of what they owe → email a statement. The statement arrives branded by your Statement PDF settings, so the running total goes out looking like the rest of your paperwork.

What it affects elsewhere

The statement that gets emailed is branded by the Statement PDF settings (cover/footer images). This tab complements the CRM rather than replacing it — the party records live there; their money side lives here.
