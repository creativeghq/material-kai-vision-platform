Receivables & Payables

/finance finance.manage

Track what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers, chase overdue amounts, and record the money moving in and out.

How to open it: top navigation → Finance → the Receivables, Payables, Follow-ups, Payments or Cheques tab.

Receivables

Purpose: open invoices plus un-invoiced receivables (deposits, advances, fees). The tab label shows a live count. For each row you can see the customer, the originating document, the amount and how overdue it is. Tools to work the list:

  • Toolbar actions: Add receivable, New invoice, and an All categories filter.
  • Search: "Search customer / number…".
  • Aging buckets (each shows € total + invoice count): Current, 0–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days, 90+ days — so you can focus on the most overdue first.
  • Open a row to view the source document and take action (record a payment, follow up).

To invoice an accepted quote, use Issue invoice on the quote page — that's what creates the invoice that then appears here.

Payables

Purpose: open supplier bills plus un-invoiced payables (rent, utilities, advances). Mirrors Receivables for amounts you owe — so you can plan outgoings and avoid late payments.

  • Toolbar actions: Add payable, Supplier credit note, New supplier bill, and an All categories filter.
  • Search: "Search supplier / bill #…".
  • Aging buckets (each shows € total + bill count): Current, 0–30 days, 31–60 days, 61–90 days, 90+ days.

Suppliers must be marked is_supplier in CRM before they appear here — see CRM.

Payments

Purpose: records money received and paid. Recording a payment against a receivable/payable updates its outstanding balance and feeds the Dashboard totals. Payments tie back to the customer/supplier and the related document.

  • Toolbar actions: Record payment, and an All categories filter.
  • Search: "Search reference / counterparty…".

Follow-ups

Purpose: quotes needing follow-up — your action queue for keeping cash flow healthy. The tab shows a count so you always know how much is outstanding.

  • Toolbar actions: Open filter.

Tip: use the age-bucket filter on Receivables to build your weekly collections call list, then record each settlement under Payments so the Dashboard and Follow-ups stay accurate.

These tabs read from the same data as the Dashboard — the receivables/payables totals you see on the overview are the sum of these lists.
