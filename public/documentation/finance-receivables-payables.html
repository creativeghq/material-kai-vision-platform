Receivables & Payables

/finance finance.manage

Track what customers owe you and what you owe suppliers, chase overdue amounts, and record the money moving in and out.

Receivables

The Receivables tab lists outstanding amounts owed to you. The tab label shows a live count. For each row you can see the customer, the originating document, the amount and how overdue it is. Tools to work the list:

  • Search by supplier/customer, document number or reference.
  • Filters — by category and by age bucket (e.g. current, 30/60/90+ days) so you can focus on the most overdue first.
  • Open a row to view the source document and take action (record a payment, follow up).

Payables

The Payables tab mirrors Receivables for amounts you owe suppliers — same search, category and age-bucket filtering — so you can plan outgoings and avoid late payments.

Follow-ups

The Follow-ups tab is your action queue: items the system flags as needing attention (overdue receivables, documents awaiting a next step). The tab shows a count so you always know how much is outstanding. Working this queue keeps cash flow healthy.

Payments

The Payments tab records money received and paid. Recording a payment against a receivable/payable updates its outstanding balance and feeds the Dashboard totals. Payments tie back to the customer/supplier and the related document.

Cheques

The Cheques tab tracks post-dated and received cheques through their lifecycle, so cheque-based settlements are reflected in your balances alongside other payments.

Tip: use the age-bucket filter on Receivables to build your weekly collections call list, then record each settlement under Payments so the Dashboard and Follow-ups stay accurate.

These tabs read from the same data as the Dashboard — the receivables/payables totals you see on the overview are the sum of these lists.
