Discover · Products

/discover

The Products tab is a single list of all materials and products published across the platform, rather than searching workspace by workspace. It's the fast way to see what's available before you commit to a specific manufacturer or listing — a flat catalogue you can scan, filter and page through.

Where it is

Top navigation → Discover → the Products tab. Products is one of the four tabs in Discover (Profiles, Factory, Products, Marketplace), and they all share the one search box at the top — so you can switch between them without retyping what you're looking for.

What's on the screen

Products gives you the wider catalogue — not just your own workspace's items — as a flat list you can scan, filter, switch views on and page through. The controls on the tab:

  • All categories — a category filter to restrict the list to a single material category, or leave it on All categories to browse everything.
  • View toggle — switch between a grid view (visual cards) and a list view (compact rows), whichever suits how you're scanning the catalogue.
  • Pagination — results are paged (1, 2, 3 …); step through the pages to work down a large catalogue.
  • Search — a search box (“Search materials by name or factory…”) that matches on the product name or the factory that makes it.

How to use it — step by step

  1. Open Discover from the top navigation and select the Products tab.
  2. Use the All categories filter to restrict the list to a single material category, or leave it on All categories to browse everything.
  3. Choose how you scan with the View toggle — a grid of visual cards, or a compact list of rows.
  4. Type into the Search box (“Search materials by name or factory…”) to match on the product name or the factory that makes it.
  5. Step through the results with the Pagination controls (1, 2, 3 …) to work down a large catalogue.

Worked example — scanning what's available. Open Discover → Products → set All categories to the material category you're after → flip the View toggle to grid for a visual scan → type a product name or factory into “Search materials by name or factory…” → page through the results, then act on a product you like — add it to a comparison, a moodboard or a quote.

Everyone signed in sees the whole catalogue

The Products list is the cross-workspace catalogue — every supplier's published products, not just your own workspace's. This matters most for a signed-up architect or specifier: you browse the full market here, then act on what you find. (Prices are still shown per viewer — your price versus retail — and cost and margin are never listed.)

Filtering by material properties

Beyond category and brand, the Filter modal (“Filter materials”) carries a Properties group — the actual technical attributes the products in the list carry (finish, format, slip rating, and so on). The list is built from the catalogue itself: it offers only registry-known, public fields whose value appears on more than one product, so you're always filtering on something real rather than a fixed menu that may not apply. Pick one or more values to narrow the list.

Smart search & saved searches

The Products tab has two search modes. Plain Browse matches on name or factory (above); Smart search is the platform's multi-vector search — it reads intent, not just keywords, and is the same engine the old /search page used before it was folded in here.

  • Save a search you'll run again. With a smart query active, Save this search keeps it as a chip above the box. Click a saved chip any time to re-run it; the trash icon on a chip removes it. Your saved searches are yours, scoped to the workspace you're in, most-recently-used first.
  • If they can't be loaded the page says so — “Saved searches could not be loaded — this is not a statement that you have none” — rather than silently showing an empty rail.

Opening a product — certificates, datasheets & the original file

Clicking a product opens its detail view; the Knowledge tab is where its compliance record and documents live. For a product in your own workspace you can also manage them here.

Certificates — a record, not a chip

A certificate is a proper record with a Standard (e.g. EN 13501-1), a Result (e.g. A1), a certificate number, an issuer, a scope, and Valid from / Valid until dates. The Certificates table shows each with a Status badge the platform derives from the expiry — Valid, Expiring soon, Expired, or No expiry stated — so a lapsing certificate warns you before it bites, and products can be filtered on what they're certified for.

  • Add certificate opens a form for those fields; each row has Edit and Remove. Editing needs it to be your product plus a finance/warehouse/pricing capability.
  • Read off the documents for you. Two suggestion rails offer standards the extractor already found — “From documents” (read out of the product's uploaded files) and “From product text” — as one-click drafts. A date it couldn't parse is left blank rather than guessed; you check each against the source before recording it.

The branded datasheet, and the original file

  • Datasheet PDF (on your own product) builds a branded one-product datasheet — your letterhead, the product's image, its specs and its certificates — and opens it to download.
  • Original PDF on a knowledge-base article hands over the source file the specs/certificates were read from, not just the text we extracted — so you can pass on the real document.

Getting your catalogue in and out

What it is — a bulk import/export for your own products, in the Data Import Hub (/admin/data-import, workspace admins). Why it matters — you can seed your catalogue from a spreadsheet and push it to a sales channel without re-keying.

  • Import from a spreadsheet (CSV) — it reads name, sku, description, category, barcode, country of origin, warranty and a product URL; anything else is ignored and price and cost are never taken from a file. Rows upsert by SKU, so re-importing updates products rather than duplicating them, and it reports how many were created, updated and skipped.
  • Export to a channel — as CSV, JSON or XML, using your own column names or a saved channel mapping that renames them to a partner's vocabulary. Only active products are exported, and cost, margin and supplier links are never included.

What it affects elsewhere

Products you find here are the same items you specify on quotes and documents in Finance and search conversationally in the Agent Hub — Discover is the browse surface, the others are where you act on what you found. Suppliers can see what people searched their catalogue for, and the same product catalogue can be embedded on your own site as a searchable widget.
