Material Recognition

/agent-hub?capability=visual-search Signed in

Show the platform a photo of a surface — a tile, a stone, a wood finish, a fabric, or a room where it is used — and it tells you what the material is and finds the closest real products in the catalogue, ready to open and add to a quote. Material recognition runs in the Agent Hub as the Identify a Material starter.

How to open it: Tools → Material Recognition, the App Launcher, or the Identify a Material starter on the assistant's Core tab. The old /recognition address opens the same starter.

1. Identify a material

  1. Open Identify a Material. A short form opens.
  2. Add the Photo (required) — a photo of the material itself, or of a room where it is used.
  3. Optionally fill in Which Part of the Photo? to point at one surface, e.g. “the wall panelling above the kitchen counter”.
  4. Press Identify. The photo is attached and the assistant searches the catalogue visually.

You can do the same from any conversation: attach a photo with the 📎 button and ask “what is this material and where can I buy it?”

2. What you get back

  • Matching products — the closest catalogue items as product cards showing the tile or swatch.
  • The product window — click a card to open the full product record.
  • Next steps — add a product to a quote or a moodboard, or keep refining in the same conversation (“warmer tones”, “only large format”).

Getting a good match: use a well-lit photo taken square-on, and if the picture shows a whole room, name the surface you mean in Which Part of the Photo?.
