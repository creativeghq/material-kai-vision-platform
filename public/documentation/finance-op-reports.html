Operations · Reports

/finance finance.manage

Reports are financial summaries and exports across your data. This is the bridge to your accountant: the journals and the VAT summary are the standard views an accountant expects, and the CSV export hands them the raw numbers without you re-keying anything. Owners use the same reports for their own analysis.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → Reports tab.

What's on the screen

A report builder with three controls and the on-screen result:

  • Report type — choose one of Sales journal, Purchases journal, VAT summary, or Sales per day.
  • Period selector — This month, Last month, or a custom range.
  • Export CSV — downloads the current report for your accountant or spreadsheet.
  • On-screen summary — the report itself, rendered for the type and period you picked.

The report types

Report typeWhat it shows
Sales journalYour sales documents over the chosen period.
Purchases journalYour purchase documents over the chosen period.
VAT summaryThe standard VAT view an accountant expects for the period.
Sales per daySales broken down day by day across the period.

How to run a VAT summary and export CSV — step by step

  1. Open Finance → Reports.
  2. Choose the report type: VAT summary (the other types are Sales journal, Purchases journal and Sales per day).
  3. Set the period with the selector — This month, Last month, or a custom range.
  4. Read the on-screen summary, then click Export CSV to download it for your accountant or spreadsheet.

Worked example — last month's VAT for the accountant. Open Reports → set the report type to VAT summary → set the period to Last month → read the on-screen figures to sanity-check them → click Export CSV and send the file to your accountant. No re-keying — they get the raw numbers straight from your documents.

What it affects elsewhere

The breakdowns here lean on your Categories (income/expense buckets) — set those up in Settings so revenue and cost aren't reported as one undifferentiated lump.
