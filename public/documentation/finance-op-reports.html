Reports are financial summaries and exports across your data. This is the bridge to your accountant: the journals and the VAT summary are the standard files an accountant expects, and every on-screen report exports to CSV without you re-keying anything. Owners use the same reports for their own analysis — including what each partner was paid.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → Reports (under Tools in the section rail). The VAT return itself has its own section — see VAT Return.

What's on the screen

  • Accounting export — download the Sales journal, Purchases journal or VAT summary for a period, ready for your accountant.
  • Report — a picker of on-screen reports, grouped (see the table below).
  • Period — This month, Last month, Last 3 months, Year to date, Last year or Custom range. Snapshot reports (outstanding balances, open tasks) have no period. Refresh re-runs the report.
  • The result — the report table with its totals, and Export CSV to download the raw rows.

Paid to owners (per partner)

What it is — what each partner was paid in the period, per owner category, from the payments where that partner is the payee. Why it matters — money paid to a partner counts as a business expense whatever your legal form, so the P&L shows it inside Expenses; this report is where you see it split by person. It reads the three built-in categories Owner & partner pay, Profit split and Partner withdrawals (see Settings → Categories) — so set the partner as the payee when you record the payment.

VAT analysis (by myDATA code)

Both sides of AADE's classification table: your sales lines and the received expense documents, with a Side column. Where an expense document carries several classifications, its VAT is split by each one's share of the net.

How to export last month's journals — step by step

  1. Open Finance → Reports.
  2. In Accounting export, pick last month and click Sales journal, Purchases journal and VAT summary in turn.
  3. For a breakdown, pick a report (for example P&L by category), set the Period to Last month, read the result, then click Export CSV.

Worked example — what did each partner take this year? Pick Paid to owners (per partner), period Year to date. Each partner appears with their total per category — pay, profit split, withdrawals. The same total is the Paid to owners figure on the Dashboard P&L.

What it affects elsewhere

The category breakdowns lean on your Categories and on filing each supplier under one (Expenses → By Supplier). Expenses still sitting in the inlet category read as one undifferentiated lump.
