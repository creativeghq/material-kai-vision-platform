Settings · Accounts & Payments

/finance?tab=settings&section=banks finance.manage

Accounts & Payments is one settings section for everything about where your money sits and how it moves: your bank and cash accounts, paying people out, how customers pay you, account statements and the finance digest email. It replaced three separate sections (Accounts, Statements & Digest and Payments), and shows one pane at a time instead of a long stack.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → Settings → Accounts & Payments. A strip of underline tabs picks the pane: Bank accounts · Money out · Payment providers · Statements · Digest. The pane you are on is kept in the address (&pane=…), so a copied link reopens the same pane, and old links to the former Digest or Payments sections land on their new pane.

Worked example — let customers pay by transfer. Open Accounts & Payments → Bank accounts → edit your EUR account → fill IBAN and BIC / SWIFT → tick Show on invoice → save. Your invoices now print the account, and a customer opening their pay link sees a bank-transfer option with beneficiary, IBAN, BIC, reference and amount, each with a copy button, and can upload their transfer receipt for you to review on the invoice.

What it affects

Bank accounts feed Cash by account on the Dashboard's Flow view and the Paid from choice when you mark an expense paid. The Revolut bank feed itself is its own Finance section — see Bank feed & reconciliation.
