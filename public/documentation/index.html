MaterialsHub Documentation

A page-by-page guide to the platform: what every screen does, how it works, and what you can accomplish from it.

Get started → Explore the AI assistant

MaterialsHub is an AI-powered platform for the materials, design, and construction industry. It brings together a searchable material knowledge base, an AI assistant, moodboards, quotes, a full finance & invoicing suite, CRM, a discovery marketplace, and a set of free tools — all inside a single workspace.

How to read this guide. The left sidebar mirrors the app's top navigation. Each page documents one area: a screenshot of the live screen, what it's for, the controls available, and the actions you can take. Use the “On this page” list on the right to jump within a page.

On your phone. MaterialsHub adapts to mobile with a native-app feel: a bottom tab bar for the main areas, swipeable tab strips (flick left/right between tabs instead of hunting for them), and proper safe-area spacing around notches and home indicators. Everything in this guide works on mobile — the navigation just moves to the bottom of the screen.
