SEO & Content Pro add-on

/agent-hub?capability=seo-research /profile?tab=websites

SEO & Content is the platform's search-marketing workbench. It has two halves that work together: you connect your website once (the platform crawls your sitemap and, optionally, pulls your real Google Search Console performance), and then you drive the SEO assistant — Edith — to research keywords, audit pages and competitors, and write full SEO articles that automatically suggest and insert internal links back to your own pages. This page explains how the two halves fit, where each lives, and how it's gated and metered; the sub-pages go deep on each.

Where: the SEO & Content tile is in the App Launcher's Marketing hub (Pro add-on). Clicking it opens the AI studio with the SEO toolkit primed at /agent-hub?capability=seo-research. The Connected Websites half lives in your Profile at /profile?tab=websites and is available to any signed-in user.

The two halves

🌐

Connected Websites & Search Console

Add your site, crawl its sitemap into a semantic index, and see a per-site dashboard — Articles, Keyword research, Toolkit runs, Domain audits, and full Google Search Console performance.
📈

The SEO Toolkit (Edith)

The AI studio for keyword research, SERP & competitor audits, backlink and traffic analysis, and writing SEO articles with automatic internal-link suggestions inserted into the copy.

How the halves connect

The point of connecting a website is that it makes the toolkit website-aware. Once you have a connected (crawled) site:

  • Everything files under your site. Keyword research, toolkit runs and articles are stamped with the website, so the per-site dashboard becomes the running record of your SEO work.
  • Articles link back to you. When Edith writes an article, it semantically matches your own indexed pages and suggests internal links — which you can insert straight into the draft. That's the site-aware inter-linking the crawl exists for.
  • First-party search data feeds the tools. If you connect Google Search Console, tools like striking-distance and top-movers read your real clicks/impressions/position, not just third-party estimates.

Metering — what costs credits

Reading your dashboards is free; the work that calls paid data providers or the LLM is metered against your workspace credit pool:

  • Crawling a site — the crawler fetches each page through Firecrawl and embeds it, so it's charged per page. That's why adding a site runs a 5-page preview first (see Connected Websites) before you commit to the full crawl.
  • Research & audits — the DataForSEO-backed tools (SERP, keywords, backlinks, traffic, audits) each debit credits per run.
  • Writing an article — the article pipeline runs on Claude Opus and debits accordingly.
  • Google Search Console — connecting and reading GSC is free (it's your own data from Google); only the platform-side sync runs, no per-call charge.

Recommended path: connect your website (Profile → Websites) and let the preview run → optionally connect Google Search Console for real performance data → open SEO & Content and ask Edith to research a keyword or write an article → use the Inter-linking tab to drop internal links to your own pages into the draft.
