/agent-hub?capability=seo-research /profile?tab=websites /websites/:id

SEO & Content is the platform's search-marketing workbench. It has two halves that work together: you connect your website once (the platform crawls your sitemap and, optionally, pulls your real Google Search Console and Google Analytics data), and then you drive the SEO assistant — Edith — to research keywords, audit pages and competitors, and write full SEO articles that work from your own pages, link back to them, and publish to your WordPress site. This page explains how the two halves fit, where each lives, and how it's gated and metered; the sub-pages go deep on each.

Where: the SEO & Content tile is in the App Launcher's Marketing hub (Pro add-on). Clicking it opens the AI studio with the SEO toolkit primed at /agent-hub?capability=seo-research. The Connected Websites half lives in your Profile at /profile?tab=websites and is available to any signed-in user. Each site opens on its own page at /websites/:id.

How the halves connect

The point of connecting a website is that it makes the toolkit website-aware. Once you have a connected (crawled) site:

  • Everything files under your site. Keyword research, toolkit runs and articles are stamped with the website, so the per-site dashboard becomes the running record of your SEO work.
  • Articles are written from your pages. Before writing, the article writer reads your own pages on the topic, works from their facts and real photos, and links only pages that exist. The Inter-Linking tab suggests more links to your indexed pages, which you can insert into the draft.
  • Each site has a voice. A website can have its own Brand Profile (tone, author, first-hand experience), and every article for that site follows it.
  • First-party search data feeds the tools. If you connect Google Search Console, tools like striking-distance, top-movers and the What to do next list read your real clicks, impressions and positions, not just third-party estimates.

Metering — what costs credits

Reading your dashboards is free; the work that calls paid data providers or the LLM is metered against your workspace credit pool:

  • Crawling a site — the crawler fetches and indexes each page, and is charged per page read. That's why adding a site runs a 5-page preview first (see Connected Websites) before you commit to the full crawl.
  • Research & audits — the DataForSEO-backed tools (SERP, keywords, backlinks, traffic, audits) each debit credits per run.
  • Writing an article — each stage bills what it actually uses, typically about 90 credits for an article, and the total is shown on the article. Suggestions, refresh comparisons and generated pictures are charged separately when you ask for them. The writer refuses to start, before charging anything, if the balance can't cover it.
  • AI Citations — the questions put to the assistants cost money per answer. The section shows what was spent.
  • Google Search Console — connecting and reading GSC is free (it's your own data from Google); only the platform-side sync runs, no per-call charge.

Recommended path: connect your website (Profile → Websites) and let the preview run → optionally connect Google Search Console and Google Analytics for real data → open the site's page and start from What to do next on Overview → Write it (or Articles → New article) for a new page → refine the result in the article viewer and publish it to WordPress.
