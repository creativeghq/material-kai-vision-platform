HR · Recruitment & careers owner / admin

/hr public careers page

Recruitment runs the whole hiring funnel from inside HR: you post jobs, they appear on a public careers page where candidates apply without an account, applications land in your candidate pipeline, and an AI screening step reads each CV and helps you rank fit — so you spend time on the right people. When you hire someone, they flow straight into onboarding and become an employee. This page covers each stage.

Where: the admin side is HR → Recruitment (owner/admin). The candidate side is a public careers page — no login — that you can share or link from your site.

Job postings

What it is — the open roles you're hiring for. Why it matters — a posting is both the advert candidates see and the bucket applications collect in. How — “Post a job to start tracking candidates.” Create a posting for a role; it becomes an open position and is published to the careers page. The Overview shows your count of open positions.

The public careers page & apply flow

What it is — a public, no-login page listing your open roles, with an apply form on each. Why it matters — candidates apply directly to you — no job-board middleman — and everything lands in your pipeline automatically. How it works for the candidate: they open the careers page, pick a role, fill in their details and upload a CV, and submit. That creates a candidate (name, email, phone, a headline, and the source) and an application against the posting. The CV is stored securely so you can screen it.

The candidate pipeline

Every application is tracked as it moves through your process. A candidate record carries their contact details, a headline, where they came from (source), and their uploaded CV; an application links a candidate to a posting with its stage. (Empty state: “No applications yet.”) You work applications from HR → Recruitment, moving each forward or setting it aside.

AI CV screening

What it is — an AI step that reads each candidate's CV and assesses it against the role, so you get a fast, consistent first read instead of eyeballing every PDF. Why it matters — it surfaces the strongest applications quickly and gives you a starting rank, while you keep the final judgement. How — run screening on an application; the AI parses the CV and returns its assessment for you to review alongside the candidate.

Credit-metered: AI CV screening is an AI operation, so it consumes workspace credits (logged per screen). You only spend on the candidates you actually screen.

Automate it: recruitment events can drive Flows — for example, notify the hiring manager when a new application arrives — so nothing sits unseen.

From candidate to employee — onboarding

When you decide to hire, the candidate becomes an employee and picks up an onboarding checklist — the onboarding tasks that get a new starter set up. The new employee sees their onboarding to-dos in the self-service portal, and you track completion from HR. This closes the loop: a public application becomes a tracked candidate, a screened shortlist, a hire, and an onboarded employee — all in one place.

Flow of a hire: post a job → it lists on the careers page → candidates apply (CV upload) → AI screens the CVs → you shortlist and interview → hire → the new employee onboards via their portal.
