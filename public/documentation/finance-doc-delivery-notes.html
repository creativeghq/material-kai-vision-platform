Documents · Delivery notes

/finance finance.manage

A delivery note is a record of goods dispatched — the document that says stock left your premises. It's one of the document tabs on the Finance page; this guide explains what it is, where to find it, how the list is laid out, how to create one step by step, and how it ties into the dispatch/warehouse flow.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → the Delivery notes tab. Each tab on the Finance page is one document type; Delivery notes is the one for goods dispatched.

When you'd use it — when stock leaves your premises, especially when the goods move before (or separately from) the invoice. Delivery notes are often tied into the dispatch/warehouse flow.

What's on the screen

What it is — a record of goods dispatched. When you'd use it — when stock leaves your premises, especially when the goods move before (or separately from) the invoice. How — click New, pick the delivery note Type, and record the dispatch.

Row actions — PDF and the movement lifecycle

  • Download PDF — the printed delivery note.
  • Movement lifecycle (truck icon) — once the note has left draft, the legs of the movement (start / tranship, return, outcome, recipient rejection, confirmed return) under the digital delivery note rules (ΨΔΑ). Record & file with AADE files each leg you declare straight to myDATA under your own AADE credentials, and every leg shows filed (with its MARK), sending, outcome unknown, refused by AADE or not filed. An unanswered filing is never re-sent blind — Check with AADE settles it first. Details: Compliance → ΨΔΑ Phase Β.

Numbering. Delivery notes have their own range (mnemonic ΔΑ), created automatically on the first one if you haven't set one up — they never share a sequence with invoices or receipts.

How to create a delivery note — step by step

  1. Open Finance from the top navigation, then select the Delivery notes tab.
  2. Click New.
  3. Pick the delivery note Type.
  4. Record the dispatch.

Worked example — dispatching stock ahead of the invoice

Goods are leaving the warehouse before the invoice is raised. Open Finance → Delivery notes → click New → pick the delivery note Type → record the dispatch. The note now appears in the list with its Number, Type, dispatch Date and Status, giving you the record that stock left your premises separately from the billing.

What it affects elsewhere

Delivery notes are often tied into the dispatch/warehouse flow — the record of goods leaving your premises. For how dispatch and warehouse movements fit into the wider picture, see Operations.

Delivery notes you receive from suppliers are a different list: they arrive through myDATA into Expenses and are matched one-to-one to the supplier invoice that bills them — see Received supplier documents.
