Getting Started

Everything you need for your first hour on MaterialsHub: create an account, understand the workspace you're working in, learn what your role lets you do, find your way around the top navigation, and see how credits power the AI features.

Signing in

/auth Public

The sign-in screen is the only page you can reach without an account. It has two tabs — Sign In and Sign Up — and a few helpers around them.

  • Sign Up — enter your full name, email and a password, then confirm to create your account. New accounts start on a 30-day free trial, so you can explore the platform before choosing a plan.
  • Sign In — return with your email and password.
  • Continue with Google — sign in or register in a single click with your Google account; no separate password to remember.
  • Forgot Password? — sends a reset link to your email so you can choose a new password.

After you sign in you land on the Dashboard, the home base for your workspace.

If your email isn't recognised on the Sign In tab, you probably haven't registered yet — switch to Sign Up. If you originally joined with Google, use the Google button rather than a password.

Workspaces

Everything you do on MaterialsHub lives inside a workspace. A workspace is a self-contained area that groups your materials, moodboards, quotes, finance documents, CRM records and team members — what one person sees in a workspace, their teammates in the same workspace see too, while other workspaces stay completely separate.

The workspace switcher sits in the top-right of the header. It shows the name of the workspace you're currently in, along with a pill that tells you your role and the workspace's plan. You can belong to more than one workspace at once — for example one for your own studio and one shared with a partner — and switch between them at any time from this menu. Switching workspaces re-loads the app with that workspace's data; nothing you do in one leaks into another.

Not sure which workspace you're in? Glance at the switcher in the top-right before you create or edit anything important — it confirms both the workspace name and your role.

Roles & capabilities

What you can see and do depends on two things: your role within the workspace and the capabilities / modules that the workspace has enabled. Core, everyday areas are open to everyone signed in; specialised back-office and partner areas are gated and only appear when you have the matching permission.

AreaTypical requirement
Dashboard, Agent Hub, MoodBoards, DiscoverAvailable to every signed-in user
Quotes, Projects, CRM, Finance, InboxA capability or module entitlement on the workspace
Sales PortalSales-rep capability (otherwise shows “Access Restricted”)
Factory AnalyticsFactory / manufacturer accounts

If a menu item or page isn't visible to you, it's almost always a role or capability gate — not a bug. Ask a workspace administrator to enable the relevant module or grant the capability, then refresh.

Navigating the app

A horizontal top navigation bar runs across every page. The logo is on the left, the main destinations are in the centre, and the workspace switcher plus your profile menu are on the right. The primary destinations are:

Dashboard Agent Hub Inbox Projects MoodBoards Discover Quotes CRM Finance

Only the destinations your role and plan unlock will be shown — so your bar may have fewer items than the full list above. The profile menu (top-right) is where you reach your Account & Billing settings and sign out.

Credits & plans

MaterialsHub uses a simple, predictable cost model: reading, searching and browsing are always free. Only explicit AI and generation actions consume credits — for example image generation, virtual staging, presentation sheets, 3D/VR worlds, and some market scans.

  • See your balance & history — Account & Billing → Credits tab shows your current balance and every debit.
  • Buy credit packs — /billing/credits for one-off top-ups.
  • Upgrade your plan — /billing/subscriptions for higher quotas and more capabilities.

You'll never be charged by surprise: every metered control shows you its credit cost before you confirm. If the cost ever exceeds your balance, the action is blocked until you top up — your work isn't lost.

First things to try

Once you're in, a good first session looks like this:

Open the Agent Hub

Ask in plain language — “find grey cement-look tiles” or “stage this empty room”. It's the fastest way to get value from the platform.

Explore the Dashboard

Get the lay of the land for your workspace and jump into any module from one place.

Set up your contacts

Add your customers and suppliers in CRM so quotes, projects and finance documents can reference them later.

When you're ready to go deeper, the back-office areas — Finance & Invoicing and the rest of the modules in the navigation — are documented on their own pages.
