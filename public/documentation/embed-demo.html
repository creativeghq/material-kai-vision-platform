Things worth trying

  1. Drag the model. It is a real 428 KB glTF, uploaded through the platform and served from storage — not a photo.
  2. Switch the fabric. The legs stay walnut, because a choice targets one glTF material, not the whole model.
  3. Watch the price. Every change re-asks the server. The widget never adds the numbers up itself, so it cannot disagree with a quote.
  4. Pick Emerald Velvet and Blackened Oak. A rule the tenant authored fires, and the cart button refuses — the same check blocks it from reaching a quote.
  5. Add to cart on a valid combination. The payload appears below; on a real shop that is what your cart receives.
