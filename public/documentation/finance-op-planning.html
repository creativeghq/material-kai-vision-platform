Operations · Planning

/finance?tab=planning finance.manage

Planning is the forward-looking view of money that hasn't moved yet: scheduled payments and expected receipts, the things that leave on a schedule, and the order lines a customer is waiting on. Invoices and expenses tell you what already happened; Planning tells you what's about to — and lets you pay it, close it from the bank feed, or be reminded the day before.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → Planning (under Tools in the section rail). Three tabs sit at the top:

  • Planning — money on a date: planned payments out and expected receipts in.
  • Recurring — money that leaves on a schedule; each one produces a planned payment when it falls due.
  • What is late — order lines a customer is waiting on. It is a different question from money, so it has its own tab rather than pushing the plan below the fold.

The Planning tab

  • Stats — Expected in, Planned out and Net planned.
  • Filter — search title or note, direction (Incoming / Outgoing), status, category, amount, currency, scheduled date and reminder date. Open plans are grouped Overdue, Due today, Next 7 days, Next 30 days and Later.
  • Add planned payment — direction, category, title, the CRM party to pay or expect from, amount, currency, date, an optional reminder date and notes.

Acting on a plan

Each open (planned or overdue) row has four actions:

ActionWhat it does
Edit (pencil)Change the date, amount, title, party, reminder or notes. Direction and currency are fixed once a plan exists. Moving the date re-arms its reminder.
Pay now (wallet, outgoing only)Opens the one payment form — bank transfer or Revolut payout, or record it. The plan's currency and any bill it settles travel with it, so the payment is allocated against that bill. A Revolut payment that is only a draft leaves the plan open until it is approved.
Mark paid (tick)It was already paid elsewhere: records a real payment and its allocation, and closes the plan in the same step. Pressing it twice records nothing twice.
Cancel (×)Drops the plan.

Closing a plan from the bank feed

Rent, a tax or a payroll run has no supplier bill to match, so an outgoing transfer for one used to sit unmatched. Now, in Finance → Bank feed, an outgoing line whose amount matches an open planned payment (to the cent, within 45 days) offers Close “<plan title>” right on the row — or N plans match when there is more than one. The row menu also has Match to a planned payment…. Closing it records the payment from the bank line and settles the plan, so the payment is never present twice or not at all. See Bank feed & reconciliation.

The Recurring tab

What it is — rent, utilities, an accountant's retainer: anything that leaves on a schedule. Add it once and it shows up in Planning each period instead of being typed again. Nothing is booked and no money moves until you settle the plan it creates.

  1. Open Planning → Recurring and click Add recurring.
  2. Fill What is it (e.g. Office rent), Amount, Currency, Bucket, First one on, How often and Every (e.g. every 1 month).
  3. Save. The next planned payment appears on its date.

Each template can be paused (it stops creating plans) or removed (with a confirmation — plans it already created are kept). If a template falls behind, it skips forward and creates only the current period's plan, not one per missed period.

Plan or bill? A recurring plan (here) is a reminder. A recurring bill — created with Repeat on Expenses → Add expense — writes a supplier bill every period. Use the plan unless you want the cost booked automatically.

Worked example — monthly rent. In Recurring, add Office rent, €1,200, monthly, first one on the 1st. On the 1st a planned payment appears in Planning; the day before, the Planned Payment Due Tomorrow notification reaches the workspace owners and admins. When the transfer leaves your Revolut account, the bank feed line offers Close “Office rent” — one click and the plan is settled with the real payment.

What it affects elsewhere

  • Settling a plan (Mark paid, Pay now or the bank feed) creates a genuine payment + allocation, so it flows into your real cash position.
  • Planned payments appear in the Finance Digest email.
  • The Planned Payment Due Tomorrow flow event fires once per plan (incoming or outgoing): on its reminder date if you set one, otherwise the day before it is due.
