Settings · Payments

/finance → Settings → Payments finance.manage · owner to activate

The Payments section is where you connect the payment providers that let your customers settle invoices online — and where you see your bank connections. Each provider is a separate bring-your-own-account add-on: you connect your own Stripe, Viva or Revolut merchant account, and the methods you enable here are what a customer is offered on the public pay page. Set it up once and every invoice you send gives the recipient a way to pay.

Where: Finance → Settings tab → Payments (in the row of settings sub-tabs, alongside Accounts). Only a workspace owner can activate a paid provider; other finance managers can request one.

Payment providers — how customers pay you

These providers power the public pay page at /pay/:token that a customer reaches from an emailed invoice. Enable the ones you want and connect your own account; the customer never needs a MaterialsHub login.

ProviderWhat it offers
StripeCard checkout for invoices, plus the platform's subscription & credit purchases, with webhook reconciliation. Connect your Stripe account and its methods are offered on the pay page.
Viva.comA per-workspace BYOK merchant account — Smart Checkout card payments and the Greek RF bank-transfer method (a structured reference that auto-reconciles a bank transfer — see below).
Revolut CheckoutOne hosted checkout that accepts cards, Revolut Pay, Apple Pay / Google Pay and Pay by Bank, via your own Revolut Merchant account. (Distinct from Revolut Business below.)

Each provider is enabled/configured in its own dialog — you paste your merchant credentials (and, for Revolut Checkout, activate its webhook). A provider is only offered to customers once it's both connected and its webhook is active, so a half-configured provider never shows a broken pay button.

The Greek RF bank-transfer method

Via Viva, you can offer a bank-transfer with an RF reference — a structured payment code. Its power is automatic reconciliation: because the code is minted per invoice, when the customer's transfer arrives it's recognised and the invoice is settled without anyone matching it by hand. The RF code also prints on the invoice/proforma PDF and email, so a customer who never opens the pay link can still pay by transfer with the right reference — see Invoices for how it appears on the document.

Bank connections

Below the checkout providers, the Bank connections section lists Revolut Business — your own bank account. It uses the same enable/configure mechanics but is labelled “not a checkout method”, because it isn't about customers paying you: it's the bank feed, reconciliation, money-out and team cards documented under Banking. Enabling it here is the same as enabling the Banking add-on.

How to set up online payments — step by step

  1. Go to Finance → Settings → Payments.
  2. Pick a provider (Stripe, Viva or Revolut Checkout), click Configure, and connect your own merchant account (paste the keys; activate the webhook where asked).
  3. For Viva, enable the RF bank-transfer method if you want auto-reconciling transfers and the RF code on your documents.
  4. Email an invoice to a customer, then open its /pay/:token link yourself to confirm the methods appear.

Worked example. Configure Viva with your merchant account and turn on RF bank-transfer → email an invoice. The PDF now carries an RF code next to your IBAN; the customer either pays online at /pay/:token or makes a bank transfer quoting the RF — and either way the invoice reconciles itself.

What it affects

The checkout providers drive the public pay page at /pay/:token and (for Viva RF) the payment block on your documents; Revolut Business under Bank connections drives your bank feed and reconciliation. For the broader set of customer-facing surfaces, see Public Pages.
