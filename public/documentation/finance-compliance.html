Compliance — CBAM, packaging, ΨΔΑ, POS & VAT

/finance?tab=settings&section=compliance finance.manage

A growing set of regulatory obligations — the EU carbon border levy (CBAM), Greek packaging / EPR declarations, the digital delivery note (ΨΔΑ Phase Β), POS interconnection (Law 5155), the pre-filled VAT return reconciliation, and public-sector withholding — now have a home in MaterialsHub. Most live together under Finance → Settings → Compliance; a few surface where the work actually happens (an order, a delivery note, the POS). This page is the map. It's operator / finance-manager territory.

Where: Finance → Settings → the Compliance section (?section=compliance). The cards below live there unless noted. A recurring principle: these tools measure and prepare — an unclassified line is stated as unknown, not defaulted, and where there is no authority to transmit to, the page says so rather than pretending to file.

CBAM — the carbon border adjustment

What it is — the EU's carbon levy on imports of certain goods (iron, steel, aluminium, cement, fertiliser…). The two rules that catch people: the year's liability is measured in mass against a 50-tonne threshold, and once you cross it you're liable for the whole year, not from the crossing date.

  • Per order — on a purchase order's Customs tab, the CBAM card classifies each line by its CN code and origin (Annex I, out of scope, unclassified, or no CN code), shows the running year position against the threshold, and lets you record it in the CBAM register with an entry date and MRN. A line whose net mass isn't weighed is flagged red — mass is the whole basis, so a guess isn't allowed.
  • Per year — the CBAM position card (Settings → Compliance) reconciles the year by sector, goods code and country of origin, and Extracts a CSV. It states plainly: there is no CBAM API — nothing here is transmitted; this prepares your declaration, it doesn't file it.
  • On the product — a product carries its regulatory role read off its origin, plus a Declaration of Performance panel, so the classification isn't re-typed per order.

Packaging & EPR — the ΕΜΠΑ / ΣΣΕΔ declaration

What it is — the Packaging declaration card (ΕΜΠΑ / ΣΣΕΔ) — your extended-producer-responsibility filing for the packaging you put on the market, due 31 March, with the same figures serving both filings.

  • Composition is entered on the product — each product's packaging components (material and weight) feed the declaration; a component with no weight is flagged.
  • Pick a declaration year and it derives the table by material — primary kg, secondary/tertiary kg, and the reusable-asset figures — plus an enterprise-size (ESPR art. 24) selector.
  • Two “obvious” shortcuts it refuses to take: first-placed packaging is not total minus prior (once you buy a second-hand pallet that breaks), so new / used / held are counted separately; and a foreign EPR registration (CONAI, Ecoembes…) does not exempt the Greek liability.
  • A supplier's producer-registration is verified on their CRM record; an unverified supplier can block a sale rather than just warn.

ΨΔΑ Phase Β — the digital delivery note

What it is — Greece's digital consignment note, where a movement of goods is a sequence of legs (dispatch, transhipment, return, outcome), each its own fiscal event. Phase Β1 from 2026-10-12, Β2 from 2027-01-01.

  • Recording the legs — a delivery note that has left draft shows a Movement lifecycle (truck) button: a ledger of legs, not a status field. The legs offered follow AADE's transition table (Start / tranship, Return leg, Declare outcome, Recipient rejects, Confirm the return); each records who declared it, the outcome (full / partial / none), the vehicle and carrier, and shows a filed (MARK) / offline / not filed badge. Offline recording is allowed and marked as such.
  • Readiness — the ΨΔΑ Phase Β readiness card (Settings → Compliance) is where you record the route — through a certified provider, or direct to myDATA as an ERP developer — and see your CN-code coverage.

Legs are recorded, not yet auto-filed. The readiness card exists precisely because a recorded leg looks the same whether or not AADE heard about it. Choosing the transmission route is a configuration decision today; the automated filing is still being finished.

POS interconnection — the terminal as a fiscal device

What it is — Law 5155 requires the card terminal (EFT-POS) to be interconnected with the cash system, so a card payment and its receipt are one signed transaction. The terminal stops being a peripheral.

  • POS interconnection card (Settings → Compliance) — a per-terminal grid: provider/model, the interconnection route (Α.1098 / Α.1155), whether it takes retail and/or wholesale trade, the matching window, and a verdict badge (interconnected / must interconnect). A terminal taking mixed trade must interconnect.
  • Alongside it, the ERP declaration (Α.1054 Δήλωση Συμβατότητας) card records your compatibility declaration.
  • At the register, a card/IRIS payment is now signed → the terminal is charged → the receipt is finalized, capturing the acquirer id, RRN and auth code; and completion is blocked if the document didn't reach myDATA. A wholesale sale settled on a terminal is flagged as needing the correct document, not the retail workaround.

Pre-filled VAT return

What it is — the Pre-filled VAT reconciliation card checks your books against AADE's pre-filled return, where — by AADE's rule — declared income is a floor and declared expenses are a ceiling. Add a period and it gives a verdict; where you legitimately differ, you record a 14.30 deviation (an expense excess) or an 11.4 deviation (an income shortfall). A deviation is not “declared” until an emitted document carries a MARK — until then it reads “not transmitted — still just a difference.”

Public-sector withholding (παρακράτηση)

What it is — when you invoice a public body, it pays most of the invoice and remits the withholding to the state (e.g. 4% on goods). The withheld part is not a debt that never clears — it's a leg of the settlement.

  • Mark the party — on the CRM company, the “Withholds tax (φορέας γενικής κυβέρνησης)” setting tells the platform this counterparty withholds. “Recording it here is what stops the rest reading as debt.”
  • The maths — the withheld amount becomes a settlement leg, so settled + withheld = invoiced; a fully-paid public-sector order no longer shows a permanent outstanding balance, and the withheld figure is what reaches AADE. It's shown as a Withholding row on the invoice.

In short: the Compliance section is where you prepare and reconcile what the law now asks for; the actual documents are issued in Finance and at the POS, and the CBAM/movement/withholding facts are captured on the order and delivery note where the work happens.
