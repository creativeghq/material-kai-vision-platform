Sales Portal

/sales sales.portal

A dedicated workspace for sales representatives.

Access note: the Sales Portal is restricted to accounts with the sales.portal capability. Other roles (including operators without it) see an “Access Restricted” message that states their current access level. For that reason this page is documented without a screenshot.

What this page is for

The Sales Portal gives sales reps a focused view of their pipeline and selling activity — the customers, quotes and opportunities relevant to them — separate from the full back-office. It complements the Quotes and CRM areas, surfacing just what a rep needs to move deals forward.

Getting access

If you should have access but see the restriction, ask a workspace administrator to grant the sales capability to your account. Once enabled, Sales appears in the navigation and opens the rep portal.
