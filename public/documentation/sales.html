Sales Portal

/sales sales.portal

A focused workspace for sales representatives — your own orders, quotes, customers and opportunities, kept separate from the full back-office so you can concentrate on selling.

How to open it: top navigation → Sales (shown only to accounts with the sales capability) — or open /sales.

Access note: the Sales Portal is restricted to accounts that hold the sales.portal capability. Anyone without it — including operators who haven't been granted it — sees an “Access Restricted” card that states their current access level instead of the portal. Because the page is gated this way, this guide is written without a screenshot.

What this area is for

What it is — a streamlined, rep-only view of one person's own selling activity. Instead of navigating the whole platform, a sales rep works from a single screen built around the day-to-day selling loop: see your orders, start a new one for a customer, and hand off to the full quote builder to add products and pricing. Why it matters — it removes the noise. A rep doesn't need Finance, Projects or the rest of the back office to do their job, so the portal gives them exactly the slice they need and keeps everything else out of the way. It complements — and links into — the Quotes and CRM areas without exposing the entire workspace.

Why it's role-gated — the portal is deliberately walled off behind the sales.portal capability for two reasons. First, privacy between reps: you only ever see your own orders, so one rep never sees another rep's pipeline. Second, scope control: gating it means a rep account can be handed out without also granting access to sensitive workspace areas. The capability is the single switch that turns the whole portal on for an account.

What you'll find on the page

Once you have access, the portal is one focused screen made of three parts:

  • Summary stats — quick counts across your orders (total, submitted, accepted and drafts), so you can read your own pipeline at a glance without opening anything.
  • My Orders — a table of every order you've created, showing the order name, customer, status, item count and creation date. Each row opens the order so you can pick up where you left off.
  • New Order — the button that starts a fresh order for a customer (walked through below).

Creating an order

What it is — the rep's core action: turning a customer and a job name into a working draft. Why it matters — it's the fastest path from "a customer wants something" to a real, priced document, and it reuses the same engine as the rest of the platform so nothing is a throwaway. Choosing New Order opens a short dialog:

  1. Pick a customer — search your existing CRM contacts and companies by name or email, or use Quick add to create a new contact on the spot (name required, email optional).
  2. Name the order — give it a clear title, e.g. a project or job name.
  3. Create & add products — this starts the order as a draft quote and takes you straight into the full quote detail screen, where you build the line items from the catalogue.

An order in the Sales Portal is a quote under the hood, so everything the quote builder offers — catalogue products, custom line items, pricing and document output — applies once you continue into it. Customers come from your CRM, and customer names in the orders table link straight back to the matching CRM record.

Getting access

Why you might be blocked — if you open /sales and see the “Access Restricted” card, your account simply doesn't hold the sales.portal capability yet. It isn't a fault; the portal is hidden from anyone without the rep capability by design.

How to get the portal

  1. Ask a workspace administrator to grant your account the sales capability.
  2. Refresh the app once they've enabled it.
  3. Sales now appears in the top navigation and opens the rep portal for you.

What it affects: the moment the capability is granted, you get the full portal — your summary stats, your private My Orders list, and the New Order flow described above.
