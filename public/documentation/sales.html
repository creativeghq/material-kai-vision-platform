Sales Portal

/sales sales.portal

A focused workspace for sales representatives — your own orders, quotes, customers and opportunities, kept separate from the full back-office so you can concentrate on selling.

Access note: the Sales Portal is restricted to accounts that hold the sales.portal capability. Anyone without it — including operators who haven't been granted it — sees an “Access Restricted” card that states their current access level instead of the portal. Because the page is gated this way, this guide is written without a screenshot.

What this area is for

The Sales Portal gives a sales rep a streamlined view of just their own selling activity. Rather than navigating the whole platform, you work from one screen built around the rep workflow: see your orders, start a new one for a customer, and hand off to the full quote builder to add products and pricing. It complements — and links into — the Quotes and CRM areas without exposing the entire workspace.

You only ever see your own orders. Each rep's orders are private to them, so one rep never sees another rep's pipeline.

What you'll find on the page

  • Summary stats — quick counts across your orders: total orders, submitted, accepted and drafts.
  • My Orders — a table of every order you've created, showing the order name, customer, status, item count and the date it was created. Each row opens the order.
  • New Order — start a fresh order for a customer (see below).

Creating an order

Choosing New Order opens a short dialog:

  1. Pick a customer — search your existing CRM contacts and companies by name or email, or use Quick add to create a new contact on the spot (name required, email optional).
  2. Name the order — give it a clear title, e.g. a project or job name.
  3. Create & add products — this starts the order as a draft quote and takes you straight into the full quote detail screen, where you build the line items from the catalogue.

An order in the Sales Portal is a quote under the hood, so everything the quote builder offers — catalogue products, custom line items, pricing and document output — applies once you continue into it. Customers come from your CRM, and customer names in the orders table link straight back to the matching CRM record.

Getting access

If you should have the portal but see the restriction, ask a workspace administrator to grant your account the sales capability. Once it's enabled, Sales appears in the navigation and opens the rep portal for you.
