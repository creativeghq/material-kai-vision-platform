My Quotes at a glance

The default My Quotes tab lists every quote you can see. Above the list sit two strips:

  • Money tiles — what your quotes are worth, in net value (after discount, before VAT):
    • Open Pipeline — quotes sent or submitted and not yet expired, with the value still sitting in drafts noted underneath.
    • Expected To Win — the open pipeline multiplied by your win rate. It reads No verdict until at least 5 quotes have been decided.
    • Profit On Open Quotes — sell price minus buy price over the lines that have a buy price. Lines with no buy price are left out and their value is named, never counted at zero cost.
    • Won — accepted quotes over the last 12 months, with the profit at the buy prices frozen when each was accepted.
    • Win Rate — accepted ÷ (accepted + declined + expired), by value and by count.
    A tile note tells you whether the figures cover your own quotes or the whole workspace. Quotes in another currency than your base currency are counted separately and named.
  • Counts — Total Quotes, Pending, Accepted and Drafts.

The list columns are Quote Name, Status, Items, Value, Margin, Created, Expires and Sent. Value and Margin appear only when you are allowed to see buy prices; a margin marked with an asterisk is computed over part of the quote because some lines have no buy price. Each row has an actions menu: Open, a Move to list of status changes, Add to pipeline and Delete.

Use New quote to start one (see Build & send) and Filter to narrow by status.

Settings

The Settings tab (also the Quote Settings shortcut in the App Launcher; shown to members who manage a supplier network) sets the defaults every new quote starts with — the Default Terms printed on the quote and how many days the offer is valid (Offer valid for, 1–365 days; leave it empty for the platform default) — together with the quote PDF Design.
