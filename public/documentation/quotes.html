Quotes & Requests

/quotes /quotes/:id /quotes/requests

Turn the products and ideas you've gathered into priced, client-ready quotes — capture full FF&E specifications per item, preview a branded PDF, and send a secure link your customer can open and accept without logging in. The same area also collects incoming quote requests so you can triage them and convert them into quotes.

How to open it: top navigation → Quotes. Use the My Quotes and Requests tabs at the top.

The two tabs

Quotes is split into two views you switch between at the top of the page:

  • My Quotes /quotes — everything you've created, with a KPI strip and status filtering.
  • Requests /quotes/requests — quote requests that have come in from customers, waiting to be reviewed and converted.

My Quotes

The main tab lists every quote you've created, newest first. Across the top sits a KPI strip that updates as you work:

MetricWhat it counts
Total QuotesEvery quote in your workspace, regardless of status.
PendingQuotes you've sent that are awaiting the customer's decision.
AcceptedQuotes the customer has accepted.
DraftsQuotes still being prepared and not yet sent.
  • New Quote — start a quote from scratch.
  • Filter by status — narrow the list (e.g. Draft, Pending, Accepted, Rejected, Expired). The filter has a small search box so you can jump straight to a status; the active filter is shown next to the list title.
  • Open a quote — click any row to open its detail page /quotes/:id and continue editing, previewing or sending.

Building a quote

A quote is a list of line items plus the specification detail your customer needs to make a decision. You build it on the quote's detail page.

  1. Add line items. Each item is either a catalogue product (picked from your products) or a custom item (anything you type in yourself). Every item has a quantity and a price, and the quote total is calculated for you.
  2. Capture FF&E specifications per item. Where relevant, add fit-out detail to any item so the quote doubles as a furniture, fixtures & equipment schedule:
    • Room — where the item belongs (e.g. the space it's specified for).
    • Dimensions — the size detail for the item.
    • Installation requirements — what's needed to fit or install it.
    • Delivery date — when it's expected on site.
  3. Review the item detail. Each line can be expanded to show its detail row — notes, installation requirements and delivery — so you can check everything before sending without cluttering the main table.

Shortcut — generate from a moodboard. Open a moodboard and use its Generate Quote button. The products on the board become the line items of a new quote, ready for you to set quantities, prices and FF&E detail.

Line items in detail

A quote is built from line items on the Items tab. The items table has the following columns: #, Product, Room, Qty, Price, Unit, Total and Actions. You can edit Room and Qty inline per row, and remove rows via Actions. Add items with Add Products, which offers two modes: Catalog Search and Custom Product.

Catalog Search

Search the catalogue by name, brand, material or colour; tick the items you want, and the button shows "Add N Products". This is the right mode for materials already in your knowledge base.

Extras & Timeline

A quote has two further tabs:

  • Extras (Extras & Add-ons) — optional add-on lines beyond the main items.
  • Timeline (Project Timeline) — a visual delivery/milestone timeline shown to the customer.

Quote actions

The quote header carries these actions:

  • Submit Quote Request — submit the quote.
  • Share — produce a shareable public link (token-protected, /q/:token).
  • Email Quote — email it to the customer.
  • Preview — preview the branded PDF (also at /quotes/:id/preview).
  • Download PDF — download the generated PDF.

The quote title is editable inline (it defaults to "Untitled Quote").

Previewing the branded PDF

From the quote you can open a branded PDF preview at /quotes/:id/preview. The PDF is laid out for your customer and reflects everything you entered:

  • An items table that includes a Room column, with each item's dimensions appended to the product name.
  • A dedicated “Specifications & Delivery” section that pulls together the per-item notes, installation requirements and delivery dates.
  • Your company branding, totals and any applicable VAT, applied from your workspace settings.

Preview it as often as you like while drafting — it always shows the current state of the quote.

Sending a quote & the public page

When you send a quote, your customer receives a link to a public, token-protected page at /q/:token Public. They can open it with no login required, review the items, specifications and totals, and accept it directly from that page. The link is unique to that quote, so only people you share it with can see it. For more on these shareable surfaces, see Public & Shared Pages.

Once a customer accepts, the quote can flow into your back office — see Finance & Invoicing for turning accepted work into invoices and tracking what you're owed.

Requests

The Requests tab /quotes/requests collects quote requests that come in from customers. Each request is something a customer has asked you to price. From here you:

  • Review a request to see what the customer is after.
  • Convert it into a quote — start a real quote pre-populated from the request, then add line items, prices and FF&E specifications as usual.
  • Filter the list to focus on what still needs attention.

Step by step: build and send a quote

  1. Open Quotes from the top navigation, then click New Quote.
  2. Give the quote a name, and optionally add notes and link it to a project.
  3. Add line items — pick catalogue products or add custom items — and set the quantity and price for each.
  4. For each item, optionally fill in the FF&E specs: room, dimensions, installation requirements and delivery date.
  5. Open Preview /quotes/:id/preview to check the branded PDF.
  6. Use Email Quote or Share to send it to your customer.
  7. The customer opens the token-protected public page /q/:token Public with no login and accepts it. See Public & Shared Pages, and Finance & Invoicing for what happens after acceptance.

Step by step: turn a request into a quote

  1. Open Quotes and switch to the Requests tab /quotes/requests.
  2. Open a request from the list.
  3. Review what the customer is asking for.
  4. Create a quote from it — a real quote pre-populated from the request.
  5. Continue as above: add line items, prices, FF&E specs, preview, then send.

Step by step: quote from a moodboard

  1. Open a moodboard.
  2. Click Generate Quote.
  3. The board's products become the line items of a new quote.
  4. Finish in Quotes: set quantities, prices and FF&E detail, then preview and send.

Quotes is a module-gated area. Administrators have additional configuration that shapes how quotes look and behave — status tags, upsells, timeline steps, PDF templates and company/VAT settings — managed in the admin area. That configuration is outside the scope of this end-user guide.
