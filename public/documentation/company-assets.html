Company Assets

/finance /my-hr Workspace member

The register of everything the company owns, leases or finances and hands to somebody — vehicles, phones, laptops, payment cards and equipment. It records what each asset is, how it was acquired, what it costs each month, and who is holding it right now.

How to open it. The same panel appears in two places, because it answers two different questions. In Finance it sits with the rest of your cost and lease picture. In HR it sits with the people the assets are assigned to. There is deliberately no separate “Assets” page — whichever of the two you already had open is the right one.

Acquisition and cost

Each asset is owned, leased or financed.

  • Leased and financed assets can be linked to a recurring expense. The monthly cost then flows through the normal expense machinery — a supplier bill is raised on schedule, it lands in Payables, and it settles like any other bill. Nothing about car leases is accounted for separately.
  • Owned assets can carry straight-line depreciation: give the acquisition cost, useful life and (optionally) a residual value, and the panel shows the monthly charge and the current book value. Leased assets need none — the lease payment already is the expense — so depreciation is off by default.

Who's holding it

An asset is assigned to an employee or a CRM contact. An asset can have only one active holder at a time — the system enforces this rather than trusting the register to stay tidy, so you can't end up with two people apparently holding the same phone.

Returning an asset closes the assignment instead of erasing it, so the history of who held what, and when, stays intact. That's the record you need when a device goes missing or a car changes driver mid-lease.

Status

StatusMeans
ActiveIn service, normally with a holder.
In repairTemporarily out of service; the assignment stands.
ReturnedBack with the company, waiting to be reassigned.
RetiredDisposed of, sold, or the lease ended. Kept for history.
