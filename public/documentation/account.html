Account & Billing

/profile /billing/subscriptions /billing/credits

Everything about you on MaterialsHub: your public profile and the services you offer, your messages, appointments and reviews, plus the credits, subscription, API keys and billing that power the platform. Each tab is documented in full on its own page below.

How to open it: click your profile menu at the top-right of any page — it shows your photo and your name. The workspace switcher (showing your role and plan) sits next to it.

The side rail

My Profile's sections sit in one side rail, grouped by the question they answer. Every ?tab= link (from a notification, the App Launcher or a bookmark) still lands on the same section. Below a large screen the rail becomes a scrollable strip across the top.

One rail at a time. A section with several panes of its own — Schedule, Social Accounts, Keys — shows them as a tab strip across the top of the section, never as a second sidebar. A website you open from Websites gets its own page at /websites/:id, with breadcrumbs back to the list; old ?website= links redirect there.

Workspaces

Next to the Profile menu is the workspace switcher (showing your current workspace, role and plan). Use it to switch between workspaces, create a new one, or open Manage network. A workspace groups your materials, moodboards, quotes, finance, CRM and team — see Getting Started.

Credits power the AI. Reading, searching and browsing are free; generation actions (images, presentation sheets, scans, 3D/VR) consume credits. Your balance shows in the header and on the Credits tab.
