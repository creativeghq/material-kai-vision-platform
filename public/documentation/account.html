Account & Billing

/profile /billing/subscriptions /billing/credits

Manage your public profile, business details, services, subscription, credits, billing and connected accounts.

Profile tabs

Your profile page is organised into tabs:

  • Profile — public profile, appearance/avatar, personal info, business details and the services you offer. A Copy link button shares your public profile.
  • Inbox — your personal messages.
  • Appointments — scheduled meetings.
  • Reviews — feedback others have left.
  • Subscription — your current plan.
  • Credits — balance and usage history.
  • Billing — payment details and invoices for your subscription.
  • Social Accounts — connect social platforms for publishing.

Your public profile

The Profile tab controls what others see at /u/:userId Public: your name, business, role, and the services you list (add them with Add Service). Business details can be enriched from the ΑΑΔΕ registry for Greek companies.

Credits

Credits power the platform's AI/generation actions. This tab shows your current balance and a history of what consumed credits.

Buying credits

Top up at /billing/credits by choosing a credit pack. Checkout is handled securely.

Plans & subscriptions

Upgrade or change your plan at /billing/subscriptions. Higher plans raise your quotas and unlock additional capabilities across modules.
