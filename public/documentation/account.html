Account & Billing

/profile /billing/subscriptions /billing/credits

Everything about you on MaterialsHub: your public profile and the services you offer, your messages, appointments and reviews, plus the credits, subscription and billing that power AI and generation features across the platform.

How to open it: click your Profile menu at the top-right of any page. The workspace switcher (showing your role and plan) is next to it.

Where to find it

Open your profile from the avatar menu in the top-right of the app, or go straight to /profile. The page is organised into tabs along the top; each is covered below. If you're brand new, start with Getting Started first.

Workspaces

The workspace switcher sits in the top-right of the app and shows your current workspace along with your role and plan within it. From its menu you can switch between workspaces, create a new workspace, or open Manage network to administer the people and connections in your workspace. Each workspace keeps its own data, members and billing, so switching workspaces changes the context for everything you see across the platform.

Profile tab

The Profile tab is where you set up how you appear to the rest of the platform. It's divided into a few sections:

  • Appearance & avatar — upload a profile photo and set how your name and role are presented.
  • Personal information — your name, contact details and role.
  • Business details — company name, address, VAT/tax details and other registration info. For Greek companies these can be enriched from the ΑΑΔΕ business registry: enter your Greek VAT number and pull official details in one step (see Greek e-invoicing for how the same registry data is used for tax documents).
  • Services you offer — list the services you provide using the Add Service button. These appear on your public profile so others know what you do.

Your public profile

What you put in the Profile tab feeds a sharable public page at /u/:userId Public. Use the Copy link button to grab the URL and share it with clients or partners — they can view your name, business, role and listed services without needing an account.

Tip: A complete profile with an avatar and a clear list of services makes a much stronger impression when you share your public link or appear elsewhere on the platform.

Credits

Credits are the platform's currency for AI and generation actions — things like generating images, presentation sheets, scans, 3D/VR worlds, and other metered tools. The Credits tab shows:

  • Balance — how many credits you have available right now.
  • Usage history — a record of what consumed credits, so you can see where they went.

Everyday chatting and searching in the Agent Hub is inexpensive. Only explicit generation actions are metered, and the assistant tells you the cost before it runs one. A full list of which actions cost credits is in Tools & Capabilities.

Buying credits

When you run low, top up at /billing/credits. Choose one of the available credit packs and complete the purchase through secure checkout. Your new balance is reflected in the Credits tab right after payment.

Plans & subscriptions

Your plan is shown in the Subscription tab. To upgrade or change it, go to /billing/subscriptions and pick a plan. Higher plans raise your quotas and unlock additional capabilities across the platform's modules — so if you're hitting limits or want more advanced features, this is where to do it.

Payment details and the invoices for your subscription live in the Billing tab.

Social Accounts

The Social Accounts tab lets you connect social media platforms to your account. Once connected, you can publish content to them directly from MaterialsHub. Connecting and disconnecting accounts is managed entirely from this tab.

Inbox, Appointments & Reviews

  • Inbox — your personal messages and conversations in one place. For the workspace-wide, multi-channel messaging experience, see the dedicated Inbox documentation.
  • Appointments — meetings and bookings that have been scheduled with you, so you can keep track of what's coming up.
  • Reviews — feedback that others have left about you, visible on your profile.

Step by step: complete your profile

  1. Open your Profile from the avatar menu in the top-right, or go to /profile.
  2. Under Personal Information, click Edit and fill in your name, contact details and role.
  3. Under Business, click Edit to add your company details. For a Greek company, enter the ΑΦΜ (VAT number) and pull official details from ΑΑΔΕ in one step — see Greek e-invoicing for how the same registry data is reused.
  4. Add the services you offer with the Add Service button, so they appear on your public profile.
  5. Use Copy link to share your public profile at /u/:userId.

Step by step: top up credits

  1. Open the Credits tab to see your current balance and usage history.
  2. Go to /billing/credits.
  3. Choose a credit pack that suits your usage.
  4. Complete checkout — your new balance appears in the Credits tab right after payment.

Step by step: change your plan

  1. Open the Subscription tab to see your current plan.
  2. Go to /billing/subscriptions.
  3. Pick the plan you want.
  4. Confirm to apply the change. New users may want to start with Getting Started first.

Putting it together: set up your Profile and services first, connect any Social Accounts you publish to, then top up Credits or change your plan as your usage grows.
