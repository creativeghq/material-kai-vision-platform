Tools & Capabilities

Everything the AI assistant can do for you. Ask in plain language, or type / to invoke a tool directly. Results appear as interactive cards in the chat and, where relevant, as real artefacts in their module.

This page is a catalogue. You rarely need to name a tool — describe the outcome you want and the assistant picks the right one. Tools marked with a credit cost only charge when you confirm the action.

Search & discovery

Material & product search

The assistant runs the platform's multi-signal search (text + visual + an AI “understanding” of each item + colour/texture/style/material characteristics) to find materials and products by what they are and what they look like. Ask things like “matte black porcelain tiles, large format” or “something like travertine but darker”.

Explainable search spec

When it searches, the assistant can show a Search Spec card above the results — the colours (with swatches), materials, styles and specifications it used — so you understand why items matched and can refine precisely.

Inspiration from a URL

Paste any web page (a product page, a blog, a Pinterest pin) via the composer's 🌐 button. The assistant scrapes it, extracts the design tokens — colour palette with hex codes, materials, textures, style, room type — and runs a visual search to suggest matching catalogue products. Renders as an Inspiration card with the palette and hero image.

Web & manufacturer search

For questions that go beyond your catalogue, the assistant can search the web and find manufacturers/suppliers, returning summarised, sourced results.

Create & visualise

Image generation & editing

Generate new visuals or restyle existing ones (e.g. re-render a room in a different style). Generated images can be saved to a moodboard or used in deliverables.

Virtual staging (before / after)

Turn an empty or dated room into a staged scene. Results open in a before/after slider so you can compare the source and the staged image, and you can run an “Analyze Quality” pass that scores lighting, perspective, scale, materials and edge-blending.

Presentation sheets

Produce client-ready A3 sheets attached to a moodboard — material boards, colour palettes, concept boards, lighting plans, annotated renders, elevation/render pairs, FF&E schedules, area breakdowns, and full decks. Interactive sheet types open an in-chat canvas so you can place symbols, draw dimensions or position callouts before rendering the PDF.

3D & VR worlds

Generate an explorable 3D world (Gaussian-splat “Marble” world) from an image, which can be embedded into a project's Client View for an immersive walkthrough.

AR & lighting preview

Materials can be previewed in augmented reality (view a swatch in your space, with a QR hand-off from desktop) and under different lighting conditions (daylight, golden hour, showroom spots, evening, night), so you can judge how a finish reads in context.

Monitor & research

Price monitoring

Track a product's price across retailers. The assistant discovers where it's sold, verifies live prices, and keeps watching on an adaptive schedule. You get min/median/max, a price history, and alerts on price drops, new retailers and promotions. (Try a free one-shot version under Free Tools → Price scan.)

Mention monitoring

Track where a brand or product is talked about across news, blogs, RSS and YouTube — with sentiment, top outlets, and spike/negative-sentiment/new-outlet alerts. It also measures LLM visibility: whether (and how favourably) AI assistants recommend the subject, and which competitors get co-mentioned. (Free taster: Free Tools → Mention scan.)

Job research

Set up a recurring job-discovery search across multiple sources with AI keyword expansion and relevance filtering. Findings post back into your conversation and arrive as one consolidated daily (or weekly) digest. Manage cadence, sources and exclusions all from chat.

Content & utilities

Catalogue & document translation

Ask the assistant to translate an uploaded source PDF/catalogue into another language for a branded output.

Content generation

Draft product descriptions, summaries and other copy grounded in your catalogue data.

Save to moodboard

Any image or product surfaced in chat can be promoted into a moodboard (a copy is kept so it survives even if the chat is deleted).

Invoking tools

  • Natural language — just describe the outcome; the assistant chooses the tool.
  • “/” menu — type / in the composer to pick a specific tool or workflow.
  • Composer buttons — attachments, voice and the inspiration-URL analyser are one click away.

Availability varies by role & plan. Core tools are open to everyone; some research, B2B and SEO tools are restricted to higher roles or plans, and module-gated tools (e.g. price/mention/job monitoring) require the relevant module to be enabled for your workspace.
