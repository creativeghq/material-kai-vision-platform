CRM · Leads

/crm?tab=leads /crm/leads/:id crm.view

A lead is an enquiry that is not a customer yet — someone who phoned, wrote or walked in asking about something. Leads live in their own list and never appear in Contacts, so your contact list holds only the people you actually deal with. When an enquiry goes somewhere, you convert the lead and it becomes a contact, with its history and quotes carried over.

Where it is

Top navigation → CRM → the Leads tab, or the App Launcher's CRM Leads chip. Click any lead to open its page.

The Leads list

  • New lead opens the lead form (below).
  • Search leads… and a status filter — Open (New, Contacted and Quoted; the default), All, or one status.
  • List / Board switch. The list's columns are Name, Interest, Source, Status, Est. Value, Quotes, Follow-up, Received and Actions (convert, delete).
  • The Board has a column per status. Drag a card to change its status; dropping it on Converted opens the convert dialog.

The lead page

  • Header — the name, status and source, Email (opens a new email in your Inbox, addressed and linked to the lead — nothing is sent until you press Send) and Call. Above it: Edit, Convert to contact and delete.
  • Details — status, email, phone, company, estimated value, follow-up date and when it was received.
  • Quotes — Create quote starts a quote already filled in from the lead, and Link existing quote attaches one you made earlier. Linking or creating a quote moves a New or Contacted lead to Quoted. In the Quotes list, the lead's name shows as the customer.
  • Request — what they asked for.
  • Activity — a timeline of notes, logged calls and meetings (with reminders), quotes and emails, each its own entry.

Leads from email and WhatsApp

A lead can also be created automatically when an enquiry email or WhatsApp message arrives, by an Inquiry Received automation in Flows (currently set up by the platform operator). Such a lead's page shows a Conversation panel: a summary of the conversation, with its status and follow-up date kept in step with it as new messages come in. A reply from your side moves a New lead to Contacted. Changing the status yourself stops the automatic syncing; Stop syncing and Resume syncing switch it by hand. Syncing never moves a lead backwards and never converts it.

Converting a lead

  1. Press Convert to contact (or drop the card on Converted).
  2. Leave Also open a deal in the pipeline ticked to start a deal too — its stage follows the lead's status, and a follow-up date becomes a task.
  3. Press Convert. You land on the new contact. The lead's request, notes and activity are kept on the contact and its quotes move to it.

No duplicates. If a contact with the same email already exists, it is reused instead of creating a second one. Converting the same lead twice opens the contact it already became.

Leads are workspace data and follow the crm.view capability like the rest of CRM. For people you already deal with, see Contacts.
