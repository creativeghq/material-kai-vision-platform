Operations · Marketplace sales

/commerce /discover

Finance no longer has a separate Marketplace tab. Selling through marketplaces now happens in two places, depending on who you sell to — and in both cases the sale lands in Finance as an ordinary order, so it is already part of your revenue, profit and reports.

Selling to the public on Skroutz, Shopify or WooCommerce

What it is — the Sales Channels page (/commerce) connects your Skroutz, Shopify and WooCommerce stores and publishes product feeds for Skroutz, BestPrice.gr and Google. Why it matters for Finance — each channel order arrives as a sales order with the cost of every line and the eCommerce income category on it, the fiscal document can be issued and sent back to the store, and a channel's lump-sum payout settles the orders it covers while booking the commission to eCommerce Fees. A per-channel comparison shows revenue, cost, fees and margin side by side.

How: enable a channel module (Shopify, WooCommerce or Skroutz Marketplace) from the App Launcher, then open Sales Channels. Full guide: Sales Channels.

Selling surplus to other trade members

What it is — the surplus / last-stock Marketplace on Discover, where you list leftover warehouse stock for other businesses on the platform. How: see Discover → Marketplace.

What it affects elsewhere

  • Orders — channel orders appear here; their invoice row offers Send to channel, and the Shipping tab records carrier and tracking.
  • Settings → Documents — a numbering range can apply to one sales channel only.
  • Settings → Online Store — product feeds link to your storefront, so it must be open and its products published.
