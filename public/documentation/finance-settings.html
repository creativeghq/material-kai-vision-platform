Finance Settings

/finance finance.manage

Finance Settings is where you configure the whole money side of your business before issuing your first document. It's one tab on the Finance page that opens into thirteen sub-sections — most documented click-by-click on their own page. Use the cards below (or the sidebar, where they appear nested under Settings).

How to open it: top navigation → Finance → the Settings tab. A second row of sub-tabs appears — one per section below.

Recommended setup order (each step builds on the last): Business Identity → Documents → e-Invoicing → Categories → Pricing → Services → General defaults → Accounts & Payments (bank accounts) → Team. Online Store, statements and the digest can wait until you're live.

The sections

⚙️

1. General

Payment terms, VAT %, VAT return (Φ.2) filing frequency, catalogue markup, buyer risk checks, order rules and expense cards.
🏢

2. Business Identity

Your legal issuer details for invoicing & myDATA — bilingual, with Fetch-from-ΑΑΔΕ autofill.
🧾

3. Documents

Establishments, numbering series, invoice template & colours, POS terminals.
🏛️

4. e-Invoicing

Provider registration, the myDATA connection, the e-invoicing mandate and self-billing.
🛡️

5. Compliance

CBAM, recalls, accessibility, POS interconnection, packaging/EPR and ΨΔΑ Phase Β.
⚖️

6. Approvals

Credit holds, margin authority and till variances: what is allowed, who may approve it, and who signed.

🏷️

7. Pricing

Markup rules that turn catalogue cost into sell price.
🗂️

8. Categories

Income/expense buckets, including the built-in owner categories.
📏

9. Cost Codes

How the workspace classifies what a job costs — set on a supplier bill, an expense or a timesheet; a project's Cost by code report groups by it.

🛠️

10. Services

Billable non-stock lines (labour, delivery, installation) for quotes, invoices and POS.
👥

11. Team

See members and invite colleagues by role with a generated sign-up link.
🛒

12. Online Store

Your public storefront's headline, subheadline and product search.
🏦

13. Accounts & Payments

Bank accounts · Money out · Payment providers · Statements (incl. Statement PDF) · Digest — one section, one pane at a time.

Once Business Identity, Documents, e-Invoicing, Categories and Services are done, you can issue compliant invoices and run the POS end-to-end. Everything else here is polish you can add as you grow.
