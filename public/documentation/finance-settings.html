Finance Settings

/finance finance.manage

Finance Settings is where you configure the whole money side of your business before issuing your first document. It's one tab on the Finance page that opens into twelve sub-sections — and each one is documented in full, click-by-click, on its own page. Use the cards below (or the sidebar, where the twelve appear nested under Settings).

How to open it: top navigation → Finance → the Settings tab. A second row of sub-tabs appears — one per section below.

Recommended setup order (each step builds on the last): Business Identity → Documents → e-Invoicing → Categories → Pricing → Services → General defaults → Team. Online Store, Statement PDF and Finance Digest can wait until you're live.

The twelve sections

⚙️

1. General

Document defaults (email, payment terms, VAT, markup) and the issuance/credit-limit safety rules.
🏢

2. Business Identity

Your legal issuer details for invoicing & myDATA — bilingual, with Fetch-from-ΑΑΔΕ autofill.
🧾

3. Documents

Establishments, numbering series, invoice template & colours, POS terminals, myDATA inbox.
🏛️

4. e-Invoicing

Novus → myDATA activation status and notes.
🏷️

5. Pricing

Per-category markup rules that turn catalogue cost into sell price.
🗂️

6. Categories

Income/expense buckets for documents and reports — add your own or import defaults.
🛠️

7. Services

Billable non-stock lines (labour, delivery, installation) for quotes, invoices and POS.
👥

8. Team

See members and invite colleagues by role with a generated sign-up link.
🛒

9. Online Store

Your public storefront's headline, subheadline and product search.
📄

10. Statement PDF

Brand the account-statement PDF with a cover backdrop and footer.
📧

11. Finance Digest

Schedule a recurring finance summary email to chosen recipients.
💳

12. Payments

How customers pay you online from an emailed invoice.

Once Business Identity, Documents, e-Invoicing, Categories and Services are done, you can issue compliant invoices and run the POS end-to-end. Everything else here is polish you can add as you grow.
