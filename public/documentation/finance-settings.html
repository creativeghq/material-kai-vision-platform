Finance Settings

/finance finance.manage

The configuration hub for your whole finance operation — business identity for invoicing, document design & numbering, e-invoicing, pricing, categories, services, team members, your online store, statements and the finance digest. Set these up once during onboarding.

How to open it: top navigation → Finance → Settings tab. Settings is itself divided into the sub-sections below (a second row of tabs).

Shortcut: for a Greek company, enter the VAT number and click Fetch from ΑΑΔΕ to auto-fill the legal name, activity, tax office and address from the official registry. Then Save.

Documents

How documents are numbered, branded and exchanged.

  • Establishments — your business premises/branches (Add establishment).
  • Document Types & Series — the numbering series for each document type.
  • Invoice design template — choose a template (e.g. Classic) and set the brand Colors: Accent, Header background, Header text, Table header, Text, Secondary text, Lines / borders. Save template or Reset to defaults.
  • EFT-POS Terminals — register card terminals (Add terminal).
  • myDATA Inbox (Received Documents) — connect to pull received documents: User ID (sent as aade-user-id) and Subscription Key (Ocp-Apim-Subscription-Key).

e-Invoicing (Novus → myDATA)

Tracks the activation status of electronic invoicing through the Novus connector to myDATA. Set the Status (e.g. Not requested) and an optional Note (contract no., date submitted, who handled it), then Save status. See Greek e-invoicing for how transmission works.

Pricing

Category Markup Rules — set a Markup % per product Category so catalogue prices convert to sell prices automatically. Pick a category, enter the markup, and add the rule.

Categories

Finance Categories classify income and expenses for reporting and filtering across the document lists.

Step by step: add a finance category

  1. Open Finance → Settings → Categories.
  2. Enter a Name (e.g. Materials, Rent, Services).
  3. Choose the Kind — income, expense, or Both.
  4. (Optional) set a default margin for the category.
  5. Add it. Tip: use Import default categories to seed a standard set, then tweak.

These categories are the ones you assign inline on invoices/expenses (see Documents) and filter by in Reports.

Services

Define billable Services (labour, delivery, installation, etc.) with New service, so they can be added to quotes, invoices and the POS alongside material products.

Team

Manage who can work in the workspace. Shows current Members and lets you invite more.

Step by step: invite a team member

  1. Open Finance → Settings → Team.
  2. Under Invite Team Member, choose the Role to grant.
  3. Click Generate invite link (under Invite & referral links).
  4. Send the link to your colleague; they join the workspace with the role you picked.

This is the platform's team-membership surface: the Role you grant determines which areas (CRM, Finance, Sales, etc.) the member can see — see Getting Started → Roles & access.

Online Store

Configure your public storefront (served at /store/:slug): set the Headline, Subheadline and product search, then Save store settings.

Statement PDF

Brand the account-statement PDF: upload a Cover / page backdrop and an optional Footer image.

Finance Digest

Schedule a recurring finance summary email. Set the Frequency (e.g. Weekly), Day of week, Hour (UTC, 0–23) and Recipients (Add one or more). Save digest settings; use Send test to preview, or Send now to recipients to dispatch immediately.

Payments

The Payments settings section configures how customers can pay (payment links / methods surfaced on the public pay-invoice page, /pay/:token).

Onboarding order: Business Identity (Fetch from ΑΑΔΕ) → Documents (series + template) → e-Invoicing status → Categories (Import defaults) → Pricing rules → Services → Team. After that, issuing invoices and running the POS works end-to-end.
