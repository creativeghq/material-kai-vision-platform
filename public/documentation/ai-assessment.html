AI Assessment Pro add-on

/projects/:id?tab=assessment /finance?tab=assessment /properties/:id?tab=assessment

AI Assessment gives you an at-a-glance health verdict of something you're running — a project, your books, or a property listing — plus a written report and a ranked list of what to do next. It's deliberately one system serving three subjects, not three separate features. And it's built on a strict rule: the AI reads the numbers, it doesn't count them — the signals, the scores and the verdict are all worked out by the platform, and the model only writes about them. So the figures are trustworthy and the narrative is what the AI adds.

Where: the Assessment tab on a project (/projects/:id?tab=assessment), the AI Assessment tab in Finance (/finance?tab=assessment), and the AI Assessment tab on a property (/properties/:id?tab=assessment). Each is a separately-purchasable Pro add-on.

The rule: the AI reads, it doesn't count

This is what makes the numbers safe to act on. The platform derives — in the database, from your real data — the signals it checked, the six dimension scores, and the overall verdict. The Claude turn is then handed those results and writes only the headline, the narrative and the ranked actions. It never invents a score or a figure; it explains the ones the platform computed. Opening the tab re-derives the signals live and shows them for free; running the AI write-up is the only part that costs credits.

What you see

  • A verdict — a badge and a score out of 100: On track, At risk, Off track, Stalled, or Not enough data — with a plain-English line and “{n} of 6 areas judged · signals as of {date}”.
  • Six dimensions — always shown, even if one couldn't be judged (it reads Not judged, never 0). They're the same six structural slots across subjects, labelled for each domain:

  • Signals — the individual checks, grouped by dimension. Each is a value or a stated reason there isn't one: needs attention, fine, not recorded (no data) or not applicable. Only needs attention affects the score; “no data” and “not applicable” are excluded rather than scored as zero. The default view is “only what matters”, with a toggle to show them all, and each has an Open deep-link to where you'd fix it.
  • The narrative — the written report card the AI produced, with the model used and the credits it cost.

Running one

Opening the tab shows the current signals and verdict immediately (free — Refresh signals re-derives them any time). To generate the written verdict and the ranked plan, press the run button — its wording matches the subject: Assess this project, Assess the books, or Assess this listing (and Re-assess after). That's one AI turn, charged in credits (typically ~9), and the panel tells you it's a paid step before you press it. Who can run it depends on the subject: a project's owner; in Finance, anyone except an accountant; on a property, anyone who can manage it.

What to do next — the actions

Every assessment ends with a priority-ordered What to do next list. Each action names a real signal the report raised (so it's never a vague suggestion), with an impact and effort badge (Quick / Moderate / Significant), a short rationale, and a Go there link to the exact place to act.

Only a project action can become a task. On a project, an action has an Add as task button that drops it onto the project's task list (and completing that task marks the action done). Finance and real-estate actions have no task list to go to, so instead of pretending, they offer Done / Dismiss (and Reopen) — the same close-out every subject gets. It's an honest asymmetry, not a missing feature.

From the assistant

The assistant can run and read assessments too: ask it to assess this project / the books / this listing (a paid run), or to read the latest assessment and its open actions, and it can mark a project action onto the task list — all under your own permissions.

Gating & cost

  • Three Pro add-ons — project, finance and real-estate assessment are enabled independently per workspace; where one isn't enabled, the tab shows an “not available here” upsell.
  • Free to read, paid to write. Viewing the signals/verdict and acting on an action are free; only pressing Assess spends credits (a ceiling is reserved and the unused part refunded; an unpriced model isn't charged).
