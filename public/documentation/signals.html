Signals

/signals Signals add-ons

Signals watch the market for you. Save a question — who starts hiring for this role, who runs this technology, who is in market for this use case, or something you describe in your own words — and every hour the platform checks it against the companies it reads. New matches land on the Signals page and, if you want, as a notification. Every match shows the evidence behind it.

How to open it: the Marketing hub → Hiring Signals, Technology Signals, Intent Signals or Described Signals. Each kind is its own add-on and appears only once your workspace has it; you also need access to the CRM.

1. Where the evidence comes from

Companies come from the CRM websites of every workspace and are re-read monthly. The platform reads each company's own website and, where it has one, its public job board. Only companies with a readable job board contribute hiring and intent signals. The tiles at the top of the page show the coverage: Companies Tracked, Websites Read, With a readable job board and Open postings.

3. Explore, then save

  1. Open the Explore tab and pick the kind, a window (Last 30 Days, Last 90 Days or Last Year) and, if you like, Only my CRM.
  2. Choose what to look for and press Search for a one-off result.
  3. Press Save as signal to keep watching. You can also start from New signal.

The signal editor asks for the Kind, an optional Name, what to watch and, under Scope and Alerts, whether to look only at companies already in my CRM and whether to notify me when it finds something new.

4. Matches and saved signals

  • Matches lists new companies that match your signals, newest first; filter by signal, and dismiss a match you have dealt with.
  • Saved signals lists each signal with when it was last checked, and lets you mute or turn on alerts, pause or resume, edit or delete it.
  • Saved signals are checked every hour.

5. Described signals

Write what to watch for under What to Watch For and press Draft Rules. AI drafts the Question the AI Answers for Each Item, Keywords (a free first filter) and Exclude Items Containing; adjust them, then press Test on Last 30 Days. The test shows how many items passed the free filter, sample verdicts (Match, No Match or Check Failed) each with the quoted evidence and a source link, and an estimate of what the signal will cost on its first runs and per week. A described signal cannot be saved until it has been tested.

Credits: drafting and testing are free. Once saved, each item the AI checks costs 1 credit; items that fail the free keyword-and-meaning filter are never sent to the AI.

6. On a CRM company

Every CRM company has a Signals tab showing its Open roles (open now, opened and closed in the last 30 days), Technologies and Buying intent, each with its source. When a section is empty it says why — the website has not been read yet, it has no job board the platform can follow, it could not be read, or it did not answer.

7. From the assistant

In the Agent Hub, ask Pepper or JARVIS — “who is hiring a BIM coordinator in Athens?”, “which companies run SAP?”, “show this company's signals”, “save that as a signal”. Deleting a signal from chat asks for your approval first. See What you can do with the assistant.
