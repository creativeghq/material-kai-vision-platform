Received supplier documents — ΔΑ matching & supplier PDFs

/finance?tab=doc_expenses finance.manage

The Expenses tab is the inbox of documents your suppliers filed against your VAT number in myDATA. Two things now make those documents complete: a supplier's delivery note (ΔΑ) is matched — one to one — to the invoice that bills it, and the supplier's own PDF can be attached so a value-only myDATA record gets its real lines and the supplier's own invoice number.

Where: Finance → Expenses. The table's columns are Date, Number, Issuer, Type, Category, Net, VAT, Gross, Detail, Handled and the row menu. The same matching appears in By Supplier.

The Detail column — what goes with what

What it is — one cell that says how this document relates to the other half of the purchase. A supplier often sends a delivery note when the goods move and an invoice later; the ΔΑ names the items, the invoice names the money.

  • Needs detail — the document has only value lines; nothing on it can be received into the warehouse or turned into a product until someone says what was on it.
  • Detail on ΔΑ … / Invoiced by … — a settled link: either stated by the issuer on myDATA, or one somebody already accepted.
  • ΔΑ …? (with a question mark) — a suggested match. Click it to review; ✗ dismisses it and it is never suggested again.

Review a suggested match side by side

How to — click the suggestion. The review shows This document and Suggested match next to each other — issue date, dispatch date, vehicle, total (a ΔΑ “prices nothing”), and their own lines — then a checklist:

  • whether the delivery note is already the detail for another invoice (and which, when, and by whom);
  • whether the dispatch dates are the same, and how many days after the ΔΑ the invoice was issued;
  • any rival candidates on either side;
  • the items on the delivery note — what the invoice would be billing, beside its total.

Then Link them, Not a match, or Close. Accepting only happens here, with both documents on screen.

One ΔΑ, one invoice. A delivery note can be the detail for only one invoice. If it is already linked elsewhere, the button reads Already on … and is disabled — unlink it there first. Suggestions whose ΔΑ is already invoiced are no longer offered.

Linked invoices expand to show their delivery

An invoice with a linked ΔΑ gets a chevron. Expand it to see the delivery note nested under it and a panel with the dispatch date, vehicle, how it was linked (Stated by the issuer on myDATA, Matched by us, Linked by an operator) and when, the items, and the billed total — with Open … and Unlink (not offered for a link the issuer stated on myDATA). A ΔΑ whose invoice is in the same list folds inside it.

The supplier's own invoice — attach, upload, or forward it

Why it matters — a myDATA record often carries a single value-only line and no supplier invoice number. The supplier's PDF has both. The platform reads its header and lines (each file read spends credits) and stores it with the document.

  • Attach supplier PDF (row menu; Replace supplier PDF once one is there) — attaches the file to that document. Its lines are written only if they add up to the myDATA net; otherwise the file is attached and you're told the lines don't foot. Supplier's document in the same menu opens it.
  • Upload supplier invoice (above the Expenses table) — PDF or image. If it matches an existing myDATA document by supplier VAT, date and net, it is attached there (“Matched to a document you already have”) — never filed twice. Otherwise it is filed as a new document To confirm.
  • File in supplier documents — the same, from an attachment's menu in the Inbox.
  • File emailed supplier invoices automatically — an opt-in switch on the myDATA Inbox (Received Documents) card (Profile → Keys). An invoice or credit note arriving at your platform address is read and filed to confirm, or attached to the document it matches. Nothing is booked or paid on its own.

To confirm — a document read from a file, not filed with AADE, shows To confirm in the Handled column and is never turned into an expense automatically. Check it, then Confirm document in the row menu to move it into the normal queue. Booking it as a bill uses the supplier's own invoice number.

Foreign-currency documents

A received document in a currency other than EUR is stamped with the ECB reference rate for its date (the latest working-day rate on or before it) and that rate is carried to the supplier bill. If no rate exists, it is left missing and flagged — never assumed to be 1.

Worked example — a supplier's ΔΑ arrived on the 3rd and their invoice on the 10th. In Expenses, the invoice's Detail cell reads ΔΑ 2944?. Click it: same dispatch date, issued 7 days later, the ΔΑ isn't on any other invoice, and its 12 items are what the invoice bills. Link them. The invoice now has a chevron; expand it to see the delivery underneath. Then Attach supplier PDF from the row menu so the invoice carries the supplier's own lines and number.

What it affects

A linked ΔΑ gives the invoice its items, which is what lets goods be received into the warehouse and costs reach products. Supplier credit notes received through myDATA are on Credit notes → Received from suppliers. AADE's monthly totals for these documents are compared with the ledger on the myDATA Book.
