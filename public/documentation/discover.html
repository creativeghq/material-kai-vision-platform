Discover & Network

/discover /network

Discover is the community directory — one search box across people, manufacturers, products and the surplus marketplace. Network is where you keep track of the business relationships you build along the way.

How to open it: top navigation → Discover. Switch the Profiles, Factory, Products and Marketplace tabs; reach the network at /network (or the workspace switcher → Manage network).

What Discover is for

Discover is the public-facing side of MaterialsHub: the place to see who and what is on the platform beyond your own workspace. Whether you are looking for a designer to collaborate with, a manufacturer whose ranges fit a project, a specific material, or a one-off bargain on surplus stock, you start in the same place. A single search field at the top — “Search by name, service, location…” — filters whichever tab you are on, so you can switch contexts without retyping what you are looking for.

The unified search and its four tabs

The search box works across four tabs. Pick the tab that matches what you want to find, then type a name, a service, a location or a keyword to narrow the results.

Profiles

The Profiles tab lists members of the community — designers, consultants and other users who have a public presence. Opening a profile shows the services they offer and examples of their work, so you can decide whether to reach out or connect.

Factory

The Factory tab is the directory of manufacturers. Each entry leads to that manufacturer's ranges and the product categories they cover, which is the quickest way to discover who makes the kind of material you need.

Products

The Products tab lets you browse materials and products from across the platform in one list, rather than searching workspace by workspace. It's a fast way to see what's available before you go deeper into a specific manufacturer or listing. For richer, conversational product search you can also use the Agent Hub.

Surplus Marketplace

The Marketplace tab is the platform's surplus and last-stock marketplace. Workspaces list items they want to move — overstock, discontinued ranges, end-of-line pieces — and these are offered to the whole community at 0% commission. You browse the listings just like any other tab, and when something fits you send an inquiry directly to the seller.

The inquiry doesn't open a separate messaging system: the conversation is handled through your Inbox, alongside your other platform messages, so you can negotiate, ask for details and arrange the deal in one place.

The Marketplace is deliberately the one place where listings are visible across workspaces. Everywhere else on the platform, your data stays inside your own workspace — surplus listings are the intentional exception so that stock can find a buyer beyond your own organisation.

Step by step: inquire about a marketplace listing

  1. Open Discover from the top navigation and switch to the Marketplace tab.
  2. Search or browse the surplus and last-stock listings shared across workspaces.
  3. Open a listing to see its details.
  4. Send an inquiry to the seller.
  5. Continue the conversation in your Inbox, where the inquiry and the seller's replies are handled alongside your other platform messages. See also Public pages.

Network

/network network.manage

While Discover is about finding people and businesses, Network is about keeping the relationships you form. It's where you manage your business connections on the platform — the members and organisations you've linked with — so you can return to them, keep them organised and work with them over time.

Network is capability-gated by network.manage. If you don't see it, your role or plan may not include connection management; an administrator can adjust this.
