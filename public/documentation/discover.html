Discover & Network

/discover /network

Discover is the community side of the platform — find people, factories and products, and trade surplus stock — plus your reseller Network. Each tab is its own detailed page below.

How to open it: top navigation → Discover, then the Profiles, Factory, Products or Marketplace tab. The reseller Network is at /network (or the workspace switcher → Manage network).

The tabs

👤

Profiles

The community directory — find designers, consultants and members by name/service/location.
🏭

Factory

Manufacturers and their material ranges/categories.
🧱

Products

Browse all materials/products across the platform.
🏪

Marketplace

Surplus / last-stock materials at 0% commission — list, find, inquire.
🌐

Network

Your dealers/architects and their commission, catalog access and e-invoicing.
