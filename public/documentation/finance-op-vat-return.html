Operations · VAT Return

/finance?tab=vat_return finance.manage

VAT Return prepares your periodic VAT declaration (Φ.2): the VAT you charged minus the VAT you paid. It works the figure out from your own documents, checks it against what ΑΑΔΕ already holds in myDATA for the same months, lists what explains any gap, and records that the period was filed. You still submit the Φ.2 in myAADE — yourself or through your accountant. This page never sends the declaration.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → VAT Return (under Tools in the section rail). The P&L card on the Dashboard also has an Open the VAT return link. A collapsible How this works panel on the page repeats the steps below.

Monthly or quarterly — set it once

What it is — how often your business files the Φ.2. How — Finance → Settings → General → VAT return (Φ.2) filing: Monthly (the default) or Quarterly, then Save settings. What it affects — the VAT Return page opens on your last finished period of that kind (last month, or last quarter), and the VAT return ready notification is sent once per month or per quarter accordingly.

What's on the screen

  • Period — a picker listing your filing periods first (e.g. September (last month), the two months before it, this month (so far)), then the other kind (the last quarters and this quarter (so far)), plus Custom with From/To dates and Reload. A year is not a filing period and is not offered. ΑΑΔΕ keeps its book by month, so a part-month cannot be compared.
  • Three figures — Your figure (VAT you charged customers minus VAT you paid suppliers, from the invoices, bills and credit notes in this workspace), ΑΑΔΕ's figure (myDATA) (the same sum from what myDATA holds), and the Difference (yours minus ΑΑΔΕ's — zero means they agree). Each names its own direction: payable, or refundable/credit.
  • Your return, line by line — Sales @ rate, Less: customer credit notes, Purchases (supplier bills) and so on, with Net, VAT and document count. An empty period is a nil return, and the page says so.
  • Compared with ΑΑΔΕ — income and expenses, yours against ΑΑΔΕ's, side by side. The two are never merged.
  • Why they differ — each cause with its document count and amount: Received documents nobody has booked, Issued invoices with no MARK, Supplier bills entered by hand, Deviations recorded for this period. Below them, whatever is left unexplained on each side is stated rather than rounded away.
  • Filing — where the period stands: Open → Reconciled → Filed.

No figure is a zero in disguise. If the return cannot be worked out, or ΑΑΔΕ has not been read for a month, the page says so — "That is not a statement that nothing is due." A partial period states no direction, because the difference between two incomplete figures has no reliable sign.

How to prepare and record a return — step by step

  1. Open Finance → VAT Return. It opens on your last finished period.
  2. Read the three figures. If the Difference is not zero, work through Why they differ: book the received documents (Expenses → By Supplier), transmit the invoices with no MARK, and reload.
  3. Click Open this period. This saves ΑΑΔΕ's figures next to yours and checks ΑΑΔΕ's pre-fill rules: the sales you declare may not be lower than ΑΑΔΕ holds, and the purchases you deduct may not be higher. The verdict reads Within the pre-fill rules, Within tolerance, Breaches the pre-fill rules, or that there are no ΑΑΔΕ figures to check against.
  4. If a rule is breached, the verdict offers Record a 14.30 deviation (purchases above ΑΑΔΕ) or Record an 11.4 deviation (sales below ΑΑΔΕ) for the exact amount. A deviation only counts once it is emitted to myDATA and carries a MARK — until then the list shows it as not transmitted.
  5. Press Mark reconciled when the figures are right. Re-read the figures refreshes both sides at any time.
  6. Submit the Φ.2 in myAADE, then press Mark as filed. The filing time is recorded; pressing it again keeps the original stamp. Reopen takes a filed period back to reconciled.

Opening a period, recording deviations and marking it filed are owner/admin actions; other finance users can read the page.

Worked example — September's return. On 5 October the VAT Return Ready notification arrives. Open VAT Return: it shows September (last month), your figure €2,140 payable against ΑΑΔΕ's €2,310. Why they differ lists 3 received documents nobody has booked — book them from By Supplier, reload, and the difference is zero. Open this period → Within the pre-fill rules → Mark reconciled. File the Φ.2 in myAADE, come back and press Mark as filed.

The “VAT return ready” notification

Sent once per filing period (month or quarter), as soon as ΑΑΔΕ's book has both sides for every month in it. Its amount is ΑΑΔΕ's figure, so it can differ from yours until the differences are cleared. It goes to the workspace owners and admins through the VAT Return Ready flow event. Because a filing deadline is a legal obligation, this default notification cannot be switched off by a workspace.

What it affects elsewhere

  • The VAT shown on the Dashboard Profit & loss card is the same derivation, for the dashboard period.
  • Reports → VAT analysis (by rate) and VAT analysis (by myDATA code) break the same figures down for your accountant.
  • Costs marked settled outside the books on Expenses are kept out of the return.
