Account · Reviews

/profile?tab=reviews

The Reviews tab keeps your reputation in one place: the feedback people who've worked with you have left, which you can reply to. It appears on your public profile, so how you respond is part of how clients size you up.

Looking for appointments? Meetings and bookings scheduled with you have moved to the Schedule tab (its Bookings section), alongside your availability and logged meetings. Old ?tab=appointments links now redirect there.

Where it is

Open your Profile menu at the top-right of any page, or go straight to /profile?tab=reviews.

Reviews

What it is — reviews left by people who have worked with you. You can reply to each one. Why it matters — they are visible on your profile as your reputation.

How to reply to a review

  1. Open the Reviews tab to see feedback others have left.
  2. Find the review you want to respond to.
  3. Use the reply action to post your response — it appears alongside the review on your profile.

What it affects: your replies are part of your public reputation.

Related: the profile Inbox tab holds your personal messages and conversations in one place.
