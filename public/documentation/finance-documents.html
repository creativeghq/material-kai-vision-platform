Financial Documents

/finance /finance/invoices/:id finance.manage

Create and manage the full set of financial documents — and keep them organised with editable income and expense categories.

How to open it: top navigation → Finance → choose the Invoices (or Receipts / Credit notes / Expenses / Delivery notes) tab.

Document types

TabWhat it's for
InvoicesSales invoices to customers. Each has its own detail page; can be transmitted to ΑΑΔΕ (see e-invoicing) and shared/paid online.
ReceiptsProof-of-payment documents, including those issued from the POS.
Credit notesCredits/refunds that offset an invoice.

Delivery notesGoods dispatched, often linked to the dispatch/warehouse flow.

The screenshots below show each document tab, followed by the toolbar actions and column layout of each one.

Two shared toolbar concepts you'll see on several tabs:

  • Sync from myDATA — pulls the documents the tax authority already has on file for your business (so issued invoices and incoming expenses can be reconciled without re-keying them).
  • Income category / Expense category — opens the manager for the income/expense category list used across the document lists, filters and reports. These categories are also editable inline on a document header. See Finance settings.

The invoice detail page

/finance/invoices/:id

Opening an invoice shows its full detail: the customer, line items, taxes and totals, status, and actions. From here you can issue/transmit it, generate the PDF, and share a payment link.

Creating & editing documents

  1. Open the relevant tab (e.g. Invoices) and start a new document.
  2. Pick the customer/supplier and add line items (products/services with quantity, price and VAT).
  3. Assign an income/expense category — categories are editable and can be assigned inline on the document header.
  4. Save; issue/transmit when ready. Issued documents flow into the relevant balances and reports.

The invoice editor in detail

From Finance → Invoices, click New to open the editor. Other document types — Sales invoice, Service invoice, Receipt, Delivery note — use the same editor; pick the type at the top.

Document & delivery options

  • Submit to myDATA on issue — transmit electronically when issued.
  • Print online code / QR.
  • Include in MYF report.
  • Move stock on issue — decrement warehouse.
  • Print terms & comments.
  • Send by email on create / Send by SMS on create.
  • Info box (printed, ≤250 chars) — extra printed note (warranty/delivery terms).
  • Issue now — assigns the legal number + date (otherwise saved as draft).

Click Preview to check the PDF, then Create invoice.

Note: "Issue now" assigns the official myDATA number — drafts can be edited freely; issued documents are legal records.

See also: e-invoicing and Finance settings.

Categories

Internal income and expense categories can be added and managed directly from a document's header, and used as filters across the document lists and reports. This keeps your books consistent without leaving the document you're working on.

Finding documents

Every document list supports text search (by supplier/customer, document number or reference) and category filtering, so large histories stay manageable.

Invoices and receipts you issue here feed Receivables and the Dashboard automatically; expenses feed Payables and reporting.

Step by step: create an invoice

  1. Open Finance from the top navigation, then select the Invoices tab.
  2. Start a new invoice.
  3. Pick the customer. The VAT number is validated; for Greek customers the details can be enriched automatically from ΑΑΔΕ.
  4. Add line items — choose catalogue products or add custom lines — each with a quantity, price and VAT rate.
  5. Assign an income category from the document header (categories are editable and can be managed inline).
  6. Review the totals (net, VAT and gross).
  7. Issue the invoice. On issue it is transmitted to ΑΑΔΕ / myDATA and appears in Receivables.
  8. Generate the PDF and share a payment link with the customer.

For the transmission details and statuses, see e-invoicing.

Step by step: record an expense

  1. Open Finance from the top navigation, then select the Expenses tab.
  2. Start a new expense.
  3. Pick the supplier.
  4. Enter the amount and VAT.
  5. Assign an expense category from the document header.
  6. Save. The expense feeds Payables and the finance Reports.
