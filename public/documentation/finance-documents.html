Financial Documents

The Documents side of Finance is where you create and manage every kind of financial document. Each document type is its own tab — and its own detailed page below, covering the list view (columns, filters, toolbar), how to create one step-by-step, and what it affects. Payments live under Receivables & Payables.

How to open it: top navigation → Finance → choose the Invoices, Receipts, Credit notes, Expenses or Delivery notes tab.

The document types

🧾

Invoices

Sales invoices — the full myDATA editor, list columns, Sync from myDATA, the mD status.
🧾

Receipts

Proof-of-payment documents, including those issued from the POS.
↩️

Credit notes

Credits/refunds that offset an invoice.
💸

Expenses

Business costs, categorised — Sync from myDATA, supplier/VAT search.
📦

Delivery notes

Goods dispatched, linked to the dispatch/warehouse flow.

All document types share the same editor and the same myDATA behaviour — the Invoices page documents that editor in full; the other pages focus on what's specific to each type.

The totals breakdown (every final document)

Every customer-facing final document — quotes, invoices and their PDFs/exports — now foots with the same explicit five-line totals block, so VAT and any discount are never hidden inside one number. The five lines are:

LineWhat it shows
PriceThe net subtotal, after any per-line / per-customer discounts.
DiscountThe order-level paid-upfront (cash) discount only (per-line discounts already show on each row).
Price after DiscountPrice − Discount = the net taxable base.
VATVAT calculated on the post-discount net.
FinalPrice after Discount + VAT = the grand total the customer pays.

The block is the same on the in-app preview and the generated PDF, and it foots (the lines add up), so a customer can always see exactly how the total was reached. The paid-upfront discount line is driven by the resale pricing engine's cash-discount rule and flows from an accepted quote through to its invoice.
