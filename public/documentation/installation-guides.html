Installation Guides

/guides /guides/:id /guides/:id/play /g/:token shared link /tools/tile-installation public tool

An installation guide turns a supplier's assembly manual into an interactive, step-by-step 3D build: each step puts its parts and hardware in place, with the manual's own page beside it. Customers and installers can watch it from a link without an account. For floors, the Tile Installation Guide builds a tiled floor step by step and gives a setting-out drawing with a cut schedule.

Where: Apps menu → Projects Hub → Installation Guides (/guides). It is its own module — if it is not shown, Installation Guides is not enabled for your workspace (see Modules).

Making a guide from a manual

  1. On Installation Guides, press New guide, choose the supplier's PDF and press Read the manual. The PDF is uploaded, its pages turned into images, and it is read; the list shows each guide as Not read, Reading, To Review, Published or Failed, with its pages and steps. Reading a manual is charged per page.
  2. Open the guide. Its Steps tab lists each step with its Title, Instruction, an optional Warning, how many People it takes and the Parts this step puts in. The Parts tab is The Kit — names, printed part numbers, kind and counts as read from the manual. Correct anything it misread and Save changes.
  3. Press Publish. A published guide is read-only; Unpublish it to correct a step or a part.

If a read fails or stops part-way, the guide says so and offers Try again or Read the manual again.

The Look — real finishes and your space

Until a product photo is read, the 3D shows plain laminate. On the Look tab:

  • Find photos of the product, Upload your photo, or paste a product page or photo address. Pick the photo that matches and press Read the finishes (one credit).
  • Finishes lists what was read; correct a colour or a material if the photo misled the reader. Which part has which finish assigns a finish to each part — or plain, or its own look (wood, black plastic, steel) for hardware.
  • Your Space — when the photo shows a room, the 3D straightens the wall behind the product into a level backdrop and builds the room around it. Drag the four points onto the back wall's corners (the bottom two where it meets the floor, the top two where it meets the ceiling or the top of the photo).
  • Press Save the look to apply it, or Clear the finishes.

Watching the build

Open the guide to play it (/guides/:id/play). Each step brings its panels and hardware in one piece at a time, doors swing and drawers slide, and drilled holes and part numbers are shown. Beside the build is the manual page for the step (Open the manual at this page) and the step's Parts in and Hardware in, with a Hardware schedule.

  • Controls — previous / next step ([ ]), Play the build / Pause (space), and an Assembly timeline to scrub.
  • Views — Your Space (the build in the photographed room), Exploded (⇧X), show or hide part names (⇧N), and the camera following each piece or holding the step view (F). The manual opens with ⇧B.
  • The player works on a phone as well as a desktop.

Sharing a guide

Press Share in the player and choose who can open the build:

  • Your workspace — copy the workspace link; members of this workspace can open it.
  • Public link — turn it on to get a link (/g/:token) a customer or installer can open without an account, to watch the build and read the manual. Turn it off and the old link stops working.

Tile Installation Guide

What it is — a free public tool (/tools/tile-installation, also in the Tools hub) that lays a tiled floor step by step in 3D and tells you what to buy. Why it matters — you see the pattern, the cuts and the materials before the first tile goes down.

  1. Under Your Floor, enter the width and depth (m), the tile length, width and thickness (mm), the Pattern and rotation, and optionally a photo of the tile.
  2. Under Setting, set the joint and wall gap (mm), the Cutting Allowance (usually 5–10%, more for diagonal and herringbone), the Trowel Notch, the Grout Colour, whether to Prime the floor first and to Back-Butter Tiles.
  3. Optionally add Your Room — a photo of the real room; mark the floor's four corners to see the tiles in it.
  4. Read What you need: the floor area, the tiles covering the floor (edge cuts counted whole), the tiles to order once a cutting allowance is set, the pieces on the floor, spacers, and approximate kilos of tile adhesive and grout (typical coverage — the figures on your product's bag take precedence).

Setting-out drawing and cut schedule

When the assistant plans a tile installation, its result card has a Setting-out drawing per room: the start lines, every tile, cut pieces shaded, cuts under a third of a tile hatched red, a Cut Schedule of the cut pieces grouped by size, and the Checks before laying — no cut under a third of a tile, edge cuts that match on opposite walls, and room sizes given by a person. An angled pattern is reported as not measured (its cuts are marked out on site), never as passing. Download PDF saves the sheet; failed checks are listed on the card as Before laying: ….
