Settings · e-Invoicing

/finance?tab=settings finance.manage

This section answers two questions: is this VAT number registered with the e-invoicing provider (Novus) so it may issue legal documents at all, and is the connection set up to transmit them to myDATA. Registration is now a tracked, step-by-step ladder driven by Novus's own answers — not a status you type in.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → Settings → e-Invoicing. The same registration card is also the e-Invoicing step of Start Here (for workspace managers with finance.manage), placed first because the signature and Novus's review take time.

Why it is urgent — from 1 October 2026, a B2B invoice issued straight out of an ERP does not legally count as issued. Novus files a provider declaration with ΑΑΔΕ against your VAT number, and until that clears, this business cannot issue a legal invoice through the platform.

Card 1 — Register for e-invoicing

What it is — the application to Novus and every step after it, each with a verdict badge. The overall badge at the top reads Not started, In progress, Needs attention, or Active — this VAT can issue. A refresh button re-checks with Novus.

The application

  • Administrator — full name and Administrator — ΑΦΜ — the person who signs (their own ΑΦΜ, not the company's).
  • What this business issues — Invoices to businesses (B2B) and/or Receipts to consumers (B2C). B2G is a separate arrangement and is not handled here. Choosing B2C asks for your Internet provider, their contract number and contract date.
  • Save keeps the draft; Send to Novus submits it. Sending is disabled while Business Identity details are missing — the card lists what is still needed with a link to Business Identity.

Send it once. Sending creates a real contract at Novus with its own number. If a reply goes missing, the platform replays the same request rather than opening a second contract.

  • Step states — Done, Your turn, In progress, Waiting on Novus, Marked done, Earlier step first, Not yet, Not needed, Needs attention.
  • “Mark done” is a note, not a verdict. It records who clicked and when. Where Novus can confirm a step itself, the step reads Waiting on Novus until it does; a self-reported step says “Marked done here on … — self-reported, not confirmed by Novus.” Not done after all undoes it.
  • What has happened so far — expands Novus's history of the request (who did what, when).
  • Cancel the application — available until the registration is active.
  • Sandbox — a sandbox registration is labelled as such: it proves the flow works but does not let this VAT issue a real document.
  • If Novus can't be reached, the card says so and shows the steps as of the last successful check.

Card 2 — e-Invoicing (Novus → myDATA)

  • Provider connection — Connected · Live / Sandbox, or Master key not configured. Documents transmit through the platform's single Novus provider key; there is no per-business key to enter here.
  • Transmit invoices to myDATA — on by default. When on, issuing an invoice can transmit it to AADE via Novus.
  • Issuer identity — lists any mandatory issuer field still missing (company name, VAT, activity, ΔΟΥ, street, number, postal code, city, country); myDATA rejects transmission until they are filled. Go to Business Identity jumps there.
  • Provider registration — points back to Card 1 (whether this VAT may transmit is Novus's answer), with an Open Novus portal link.

Card 3 — B2B e-invoicing mandate

What it is — whether you are actually meeting the mandate: how many B2B invoices since the mandate date went through the provider, through timologio, as an ERP fallback, or with no recorded channel. It records the Δήλωση Έναρξης Ηλεκτρονικής Έκδοσης Στοιχείων (Filed on, Declared start date, Record the filing), shows any open loss of connection (while open, B2B invoices may lawfully go out on the ERP channel — re-transmit them once it closes), and the inbound side: documents received, structured, pulled from myDATA.

Card 4 — Self-billing (αυτοτιμολόγηση)

Invoices you raise in a supplier's name: the supplier is the issuer and you are the counterpart, so each is booked as a purchase with a supplier bill. Each supplier gets its own series that never mixes with your own sales numbering.

Worked example — open Finance → Settings → e-Invoicing → fill the administrator's name and ΑΦΜ, tick B2B → Send to Novus → Download the contract, give it to the administrator, Mark done → when it comes back signed, Upload the signed PDF → wait for Novus to approve and activate → accept the declaration in myAADE and Mark done. When the top badge reads Active — this VAT can issue, you are ready to transmit.

What it affects

Registration decides whether issued documents can legally go out through the provider; the connection card decides whether they are transmitted. For how issuing and transmitting work day to day, see Greek e-invoicing (myDATA).
