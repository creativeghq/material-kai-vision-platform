Documents · Expenses

/finance?tab=doc_expenses /finance?tab=expense_suppliers /finance?tab=expense_kinds finance.manage

Expenses is where every cost of the business lands — supplier invoices filed against your ΑΦΜ in myDATA, rent, contracts, payroll and your own year-end entries, purchases from outside Greece, and the bills you record yourself. Three sections in the Finance rail read the same inbox three ways: Expenses (document by document), By Supplier (file and book a whole supplier at once) and By Kind & VAT (what the year's costs are made of). This guide covers all three, how a document becomes an expense, how to pay or settle it, and how to record and file an expense document with ΑΑΔΕ yourself.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → under Documents in the section rail: Expenses, By Supplier and By Kind & VAT.

The one rule to remember — a received document is not an expense until it is booked. Until then it is in no report, no P&L and no VAT return. Your own entries (payroll, depreciation, year-end regularisations) are the exception: they count from the document, because they can never become a supplier bill.

Expenses — the inbox

What it is — every received document, one row each, with its supplier, kind, VAT treatment, origin and whether it is handled. When you'd use it — to pull in what suppliers filed against you, to book or pay a single document, or to find one.

Toolbar

  • Every expense, by kind — opens the By Kind & VAT section.
  • Record an expense document — create a 13.x–17.x document here and, optionally, file it with ΑΑΔΕ (see below).
  • Sync from myDATA — pulls the documents ΑΑΔΕ already holds for your business. Pick a period (a preset or your own From/To) and Sync; documents already in the inbox are left untouched, so re-running an overlapping period is safe. It needs your myDATA inbox credentials in Profile → Keys.
  • By supplier — jumps to the By Supplier section.
  • Expense category — add an expense category without leaving the list (the full list is in Settings → Categories).
  • Add expense — record a cost directly: category, supplier/payee, net, VAT and total, date, currency, due date and Repeat. With Paid now on (the default) it books the cost and the money going out in one step; set Repeat to also create a recurring bill template.
  • Upload supplier invoice — drop a supplier's PDF and the platform reads its header and lines. If it matches a myDATA document you already have (same supplier, date and net total) the file is attached there, never filed twice. Otherwise it is filed for confirmation and is never booked on its own — check it and press Confirm document in the row menu. More on delivery-note matching, supplier PDFs and foreign-currency documents: Received supplier documents.
  • Filter — three groups: General (search, handled, detail), Kind & VAT (expense kind, VAT treatment, where it came from, ΑΑΔΕ document type) and Supplier (issuer, category, source), plus amount and issue date. The issue-date filter queries the database, so older documents beyond the first 2,000 shown are reachable by narrowing it.

Mark as paid — in the books, or outside them

What it is — one action with two outcomes, and the Paid from account decides which. Why it matters — purchases paid before the platform held your books should not be counted a second time.

  • Pick an account — the purchase is booked as an expense and the payment leaves that account in the same step (Book and pay, or just Pay when it is already booked). It counts in the P&L, the VAT return and that balance.
  • No account — settled outside the books — nothing is booked and no money moves. The cost is kept out of the P&L, expenses and the VAT return, and reported separately beside the P&L (and on the supplier's CRM record) so it is never hidden. Add a note saying why. This option is disabled on a document that is already an expense — reverse those with a supplier credit note instead.

Recurring expenses

Bills that repeat (created with Repeat on Add expense) are listed under the inbox, where you can pause, resume or remove each template. A bill template creates a categorised supplier bill every period. For a reminder that books nothing until you settle it, use a recurring plan in Planning → Recurring instead.

Record an expense document and file it with ΑΑΔΕ

What it is — a form for the expense-side documents that are yours to file — rent, a foreign purchase, a contract, your own payroll entry. These go straight to myDATA under your workspace's own ΑΑΔΕ credentials, not through an e-invoicing provider. Why it matters — without it, every one of these had to be typed into myAADE by hand.

  1. On the Expenses tab, click Record an expense document.
  2. Choose the Document type, then fill Series, Number, Issue date and Net amount.
  3. Where the type needs one, enter the supplier: Supplier ΑΦΜ / VAT number, Supplier name, Country.
  4. Set the VAT category and VAT amount (and the exemption reason on a 0% line), plus the Expense classification type and category.
  5. Click Record only to keep it in Expenses unfiled, or Record and file with ΑΑΔΕ. A filed document shows its MARK; if ΑΑΔΕ refuses it, the reasons are shown and the document stays so you can correct it.

Not sure your myDATA subscription may file as well as read? Check my ΑΑΔΕ rights in the same dialog asks ΑΑΔΕ without creating anything.

A MARK cannot be withdrawn. Check the figures before you press Record and file with ΑΑΔΕ.

By Supplier — categorise and book a whole supplier

What it is — the same inbox grouped by issuer. Why it matters — documents arrive under the inlet they came through (the built-in myAADE category), which says nothing about what the money bought. Filing is done once per supplier, and it sticks: everything that arrives from them later is filed the same way automatically.

Categorise the inlet

Click Suggest categories. The platform proposes one category per supplier, each with its reason, and writes nothing until you apply it. The decision comes from, in order:

  1. Document type — a 17.1 is payroll, decided without anything else being consulted.
  2. Registered activity (ΚΑΔ) — what the supplier told ΑΑΔΕ it does, where the CRM holds their ΚΑΔ.
  3. Classifier — a model reading the trade name, only where neither of the above answers.

Low-confidence proposals arrive unticked, and a supplier that could not be read comes back undecided. Change any category before applying (it then reads Your decision), tick the rows you agree with and press Apply. Applying only touches documents still in the inlet, so a category somebody chose by hand is never overwritten; correcting a supplier's category does move the documents that rule filed.

Waiting to become expenses

The bookable documents per supplier, with the total that will book. Book N files and books a supplier's documents together, because a document books into the category its supplier is filed to. Documents that cannot book are listed under Not bookable, and why (cancelled at ΑΑΔΕ, payroll, credit notes, delivery notes with no money…).

Expenses by supplier

Every supplier with Docs, To file, Net and the date span. Pick a category in File as and press File N. Filing a supplier is permanent — the next invoice from them files itself.

By Kind & VAT — what the year's costs are made of

A full-width breakdown for a chosen year. Four totals — Received, In the P&L, Waiting on you, Not an expense — then By where it came from (Greece, EU, outside the EU, not stated, with All expenses summed), By VAT treatment, and Every segment: one row per kind, VAT treatment and origin, with what reached the books and what is still waiting.

Paying an expense from an order

On a purchase or sales order, each attached expense with something still due has a pay button (wallet icon) on its row. It opens the same payment form with that bill's supplier, currency and amount due — bank transfer, Revolut, or just record it. Rows with nothing due have no button. See Orders.

What it affects elsewhere

  • The P&L and Spend on the Dashboard — booked documents and your own entries count; settled-outside costs are stated separately.
  • Payables — a booked, unpaid expense is something you owe.
  • The VAT return — received documents nobody booked appear as a cause of difference against ΑΑΔΕ.
  • Reports — category breakdowns are only as good as the supplier filing above.
  • Notifications — Expense Booked, Expense Settled Outside the Books and Supplier Credit Note Received are flow events with a default notification.
