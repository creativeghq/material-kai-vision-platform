Account · Ambassador

/profile?tab=ambassador

The Ambassador tab is where you say which brands you represent — the factories and suppliers you actually work with, and the material categories you promote each one in. It's a showcase for your public profile: pick a brand from the platform's supplier list, describe your relationship, and it goes live on your profile straight away. This is not a referral or affiliate scheme — there's no link to share, no sign-ups to track and nothing to earn. It's about credibility: showing visitors the brands behind your work.

Where: your Profile → the Ambassador tab (/profile?tab=ambassador). Open to everyone — no plan, no module, no approval step.

What's on the tab

  • Four stat tiles — Brands (you represent), Categories (covered), On our list (how many of your brands are suppliers on the platform), and Featured (your lead brands).
  • Brands you represent — the card that lists them, with Add brand and a View profile link to see how they appear publicly.
  • Each brand row shows its relationship label, an “On our supplier list” badge where applicable, a “Featured” badge, and the year you've worked with them — with reorder arrows and Edit / Feature / Remove actions.

Your profile has to be public. If your profile is private, the tab warns you that none of this is visible, with a Make profile public button. Brands only appear to visitors once your public profile is public.

Adding a brand

Click Add brand and fill in:

FieldNotes
BrandSearch the platform's supplier list and pick a factory/supplier. (A brand that isn't on the list can still be added by hand.)
Your relationshipHow you're connected — see the types below.
Categories you promote it inThe material categories you represent this brand in.
What you say about themA short headline (up to 280 characters) shown on your profile.
Working with them sinceThe year the relationship started.
Brand linkA link to the brand (https only).
Showcase moodboardOptionally feature a moodboard that shows your work with them.
Feature this brandA switch to mark it a lead brand, surfaced more prominently.

It's live the moment you save — nobody has to approve it.

Relationship types

The relationship you choose sets how the brand reads on your public profile:

TypeReads on your profile as…
Ambassador“Ambassador for …” — you represent and actively promote the brand in your work.
Authorized dealer“Authorized dealer of …”
Certified installer“Certified installer for …”
Specifier“Specifies …”

What it affects

  • Your public profile — the brands, relationships and categories appear at /u/:userId Public (once your profile is public).
  • The brand can see you. If the brand you picked matches a company on the platform's supplier list and that company has an account, that supplier sees who represents it in its Supplier Portal. Nothing else is shared, and there's nothing for them to approve.

In short: add the real brands behind your work, say how you're connected and in which categories, and — with your profile public — they become part of how clients and partners size you up. It's a credibility signal, not a rewards programme.
