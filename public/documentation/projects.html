Projects

/projects /projects/:id

Organise work by client or job, invite collaborators, and deliver a polished online “Client View”.

What this page is for

Projects are the top-level container for a piece of work. A project ties together the moodboards, sheets, quotes and people involved, and gives you a single place to assemble and share deliverables with a client.

The list page

  • New Project — create a project (name it for the client or job).
  • Show archived — reveal projects you've archived.
  • Each project card opens its detail page.

Inside a project

A project's detail page is organised into tabs covering the work and its deliverables. The headline capability is the Client View.

Client Views

A Client View is a project-level deliverable that bundles selected presentation sheets — drawn from any of the project's boards — into one client-ready package: a PDF plus a revocable online page. The online page can carry things a PDF can't:

  • 3D walkthrough — an explorable Marble world embedded in the page.
  • Lighting moods — switch the hero render between lighting presets.
  • Live FF&E table — pulled from a linked quote.
  • Inline approve / comment — the client can sign off or leave feedback.

You choose and order the sheets, toggle which embeds to include, generate the PDF, and copy or disable the share link. Client feedback comes back into the project.

Invitations

You can invite collaborators or clients to a project. Invitees receive a link (/projects/invite/:token) that lets them land on a public invite page and then accept once signed in.

Projects are module-gated — if you don't see Projects in the navigation, the module isn't enabled for your workspace.
