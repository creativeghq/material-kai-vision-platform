Projects

/projects /projects/:id

Projects are the top-level container for a piece of work — they tie together the moodboards, sheets, quotes and people involved, and let you deliver a polished online Client View. The detailed areas are on their own pages below.

How to open it: top navigation → Projects (module-gated — if it isn't shown, the Projects module isn't enabled for your workspace). Use New Project to create one; Show archived reveals archived projects.
