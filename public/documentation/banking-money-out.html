Money-out & payouts

/finance → Settings → Accounts finance.manage

Money-out is how you pay people from your connected Revolut account without leaving the platform — a supplier, a CRM counterparty, a whole run of due bills, or someone who has no IBAN at all. Every instruction is audited before Revolut is even called, and — because paying the wrong account is unrecoverable — the platform puts a Verification-of-Payee (VoP) name check in front of it. By default it prepares a draft that a human approves in the Revolut app, so nothing moves money unattended.

Where: Finance → Settings → Accounts → the Money-out card (below the bank feed; shown only when the connection is live). Each instruction you send is listed there with its state.

Verification of Payee — the gate in front of every payment

What it is — before you can pay a CRM bank account, it must be registered with Revolut as a counterparty, and creating one runs a name check: Revolut compares the holder name you have on file against the name the receiving bank has for that IBAN. Why it matters — a bank transfer to the wrong account is gone; VoP catches a typo'd IBAN or a mismatched name before the money leaves. The verdict is one of:

VerdictWhat happens
MatchesGood to pay.
Close matchAllowed, but the UI warns and shows the bank's actual name so you can eyeball it.
Does not matchHard-blocked — you can only proceed by explicitly ticking a “the holder name does NOT match this IBAN — proceed anyway” confirmation.
Couldn't be checkedThe receiving bank doesn't support the check; proceed with your own judgement.

You can run this check ahead of time from a CRM bank account's Verify holder button, so a supplier is pre-verified before you ever pay them.

Pay a CRM counterparty

Pick the recipient (a saved CRM bank account), the source pocket, an amount and a reference. Then choose how it goes out:

  • Payment draft (default) — prepared here, but a person approves it in the Revolut app before any money moves. This is the safe default: the platform never moves money unattended.
  • Send immediately — tick this to make it a direct payment that executes straight away (still VoP-gated).

Supplier bill run — pay everything due at once

Draft all due bills prepares a single multi-payment run covering every supplier bill that's due today or earlier and whose supplier has a VoP-verified counterparty. One approval in the Revolut app pays the whole run. Bills whose supplier isn't linked to a verified bank account are skipped and listed — never guessed at — so you can go fix those and re-run. When the transfers complete, they reconcile back to the bills automatically and mark them paid.

Payout links — pay someone without an IBAN

Need to refund a customer or pay someone whose bank details you don't have? A payout link takes just a name and an amount; the platform creates a claim link (auto-copied to your clipboard) that the recipient opens to receive the money into their own account. The link's lifecycle — created → claimed / expired / cancelled — is kept up to date automatically from Revolut, and every payout link is recorded in the money-out audit.

Moving money between your own pockets (FX)

The connection can also fetch a live FX rate and exchange between your business's own currency pockets — useful treasury plumbing when you invoice in one currency and pay in another. (These run under the connection; they aren't customer-facing.)

Everything is audited first. Each instruction — draft, direct payment, bill run, payout link, exchange — is written to the money-out log with a unique request id before Revolut is called, so a network hiccup can never create a duplicate payment, and you always have a record of what was instructed and its current state.

Worked example — paying a supplier safely. In CRM, open the supplier's bank account and click Verify holder → it comes back Matches. Later, in Finance → Settings → Accounts → Money-out, choose that counterparty, enter the amount and the bill reference, leave it as a draft, and send. Approve the one draft in your Revolut app; when it clears, the platform reconciles it against the bill and marks it paid.
