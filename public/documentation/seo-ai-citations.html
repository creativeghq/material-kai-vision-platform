AI Citations

/websites/:id?section=ai &view=overview · asked · discover · pages · beyond

More buyers now start with an AI assistant than with a search box. AI Citations asks ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini the questions your customers ask, and records two different things for each answer: whether it named you, and whether it cited you, i.e. linked your site as a source. It shows who was cited instead of you, which page of yours should have won and what that page is missing, where the questions worth asking come from, and the visits the assistants actually send you. Figures cover the last 90 days.

Where: open a website (Profile → Websites → click the site) → Visibility → AI Citations. The section is five tabs in reading order. Each tab opens with a one-line explanation and a button to the next step.

TabWhat it answers
1. Where you standHow often the assistants name you, link you, and who they pick instead.
2. What we askThe questions every number in step 1 comes from, and who answers them.
3. Find questionsReal questions from your market, your Google searches, People Also Ask and your own enquiries.
4. Put them on pagesWhich page answers which question, and why a page lost.
5. Beyond the chatAI answers about you in the wider market, visitors sent by assistants, and your presence on YouTube, Reddit, Pinterest and Trustpilot.

Getting started

If nothing is being asked yet, the section says so and offers Track this site's brand. After that the questions run on the AI Visibility schedule you set under Reports → Schedules, which shows the cost per run first; nothing is asked on a schedule you didn't switch on. Run probes now asks them immediately. If monitoring has stalled, a banner names the fix: Track this site's brand, Switch {brand} back on or Run probes now.

2. What we ask

Question by question lists every question as one row: the prompt, a verdict pip per assistant and a one-line summary (cited by / named by / left out by N of those that answered). Open a row for each assistant's verdict (Cited you, Named you · #N, Left you out, Failed or Not run) and Show answer for the full reply. Expand all / Collapse all sit beside Edit questions.

Edit questions opens What we ask the assistants:

  • Questions — your own prompts, one per line. A question you tracked from step 3 keeps a note of where it came from.
  • Also known as — other names for the brand.
  • Languages and Countries. Set a country: without one, the DataForSEO and scraped answers are searched from the United States, and a warning says so.
  • Who answers — Cheap tier · vendor APIs, Frontier tier, DataForSEO · 4 engines, your country (the default when you track a brand from this section), or Scraped · as a buyer sees it.
  • A checkbox to also ask the stock questions.

Click Save questions. The probe list holds up to 12 questions.

3. Find questions

No assistant tells you what someone asked before it sent them to you, and the ChatGPT question database covers the United States in English only, so these are the nearest real questions. Each list has Track (adds it to the probe list, asked in your market's language) and Put on a page (see step 4).

TabWhere the questions come from
Asked in your marketReal searches in your country and language that Google answered with an AI Overview and that contain one of your topics (short head terms), with AI search volume and who the answer cites. Topics default to your shortest tracked keywords; Edit topics changes them. Each topic is one paid search on every market read. A failed search reads as unknown, never as "no questions".
AI cites youQuestions where an AI answer already cites your site, and which page it cites.
You rank in GoogleQuestion-shaped or five-word-plus searches from your Search Console, with impressions, clicks and position.
People also askThe questions Google showed beside keywords you researched for this site.
From your enquiriesEach new enquiry from your website form or page card is turned into the general question a buyer would ask an AI assistant, with nothing personal kept: no name, company, email or phone. They appear within six hours of the enquiry, repeats are merged with a count, and Dismiss hides one. None is asked of an assistant until you track it.

4. Put them on pages

Put on a page asks Which page should answer this? and ranks your pages for the question: the page Search Console already shows for that exact search first, then pages whose title or heading match. Pick one and Add to this page, or Write an article for it if no page fits. The question is then listed here (Waiting) and on the page in Site Health → Pages. Answer it on the site, under the question as a heading, and the next crawl marks it On the page. If the page already carries the question, it's marked done straight away.

Why you were not cited — fixing the page that lost

Below sits every question an assistant answered without linking you, the page of yours that should have won it, and what that page is missing. Click Find the page on a question; the platform reads your best-matching page and scores it from 0 to 100 on eight things answer engines favour:

  • Answers in the opening
  • Headings ask the question
  • FAQ or Q&A block
  • Concrete facts and figures
  • Comparison or spec table
  • Cites its sources
  • Named author or company credentials
  • Visibly current

It lists up to three fixes. If the page is already well shaped, it says so: the gap is authority or awareness, not the page. Re-read page checks it again after you've changed it. On a page that was cited instead of you, Compare with yours scores that rival page the same way and lists the dimensions where it beats yours.

5. Beyond the chat

  • In the wider corpus — DataForSEO's index of real AI answers to other people's questions, kept separate from your own probes and never merged with them. It counts only answers that actually name your brand, and shows Mentions in AI answers, AI search volume, a month-by-month trend, what's cited alongside you, and the most-mentioned domains, brands and pages in your market, separately for Google AI Overview and ChatGPT. (ChatGPT mention data covers the United States only, so a Greek market can legitimately read empty there.)
  • Visitors sent by AI assistants — from your connected Google Analytics: AI-referred sessions, Share of traffic, Engaged Rate against the site as a whole, and Key Events, with a row per assistant (ChatGPT, Perplexity, Copilot, Gemini, Claude and others). Without Analytics it says Not connected; a failed read is unknown, not zero.
  • Presence beyond Google — Build the plan searches your top non-branded Search Console queries (or tracked keywords) on YouTube, Reddit and Pinterest, and your brand on Trustpilot. Each platform reads present, absent or unknown (a failed lookup is never "absent"), with concrete actions: threads to answer, videos to make, pins to add, a profile to claim or reviews to ask for. The credit ceiling is stated before anything is spent. Rebuild the plan runs it again.

Failed is not absent

A probe that got no answer is excluded from every rate, never counted as a miss. An assistant whose calls all failed reads Unknown, never 0%. When 10% or more of the latest checks failed, a warning says so. If no answer carried a source, Cited instead of you says that is unknown, not "nobody beat you".

AI search volume — how often a term is asked of an assistant rather than typed into Google — also appears as an Asked of AI tile in Keywords → Research. Click Check AI volume there to fetch it. It's fetched only on request, because each lookup is a paid call.
