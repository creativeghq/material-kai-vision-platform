AI Citations

/websites/:id?section=ai

More buyers now start with an AI assistant than with a search box. AI Citations asks ChatGPT, Claude, Perplexity and Gemini the questions your customers ask, and records two different things for each answer: whether it named you, and whether it cited you, i.e. linked your site as a source. It then shows who was cited instead of you, and for each question you lost, which page of yours should have won and what that page is missing. Figures cover the last 90 days.

Where: open a website (Profile → Websites → click the site) → Visibility → AI Citations. This section used to be called AI Visibility and led with share of voice. It now leads with citation. Share of voice is now the Named rate.

Getting started

If nothing is being asked yet, the section says Nothing is being asked yet and offers Track this site's brand. Once the brand is tracked, the questions run on a schedule. Run probes now (top right) runs them immediately. If monitoring has stalled, a banner names the fix: Track this site's brand, Switch {brand} back on or Run probes now.

Choosing the questions and who answers

Edit questions (on Question by question) opens What we ask the assistants. In it you set:

  • Questions — your own prompts, one per line.
  • Also known as — other names for the brand.
  • Languages and Countries. Set a country: without one, the DataForSEO and scraped answers are searched from the United States, and a warning says so.
  • Who answers — Cheap tier · vendor APIs, Frontier tier, DataForSEO · 4 engines, your country (the default when you track a brand from this section), or Scraped · as a buyer sees it.
  • A checkbox to also ask the stock questions.

Click Save questions. The probe list holds up to 12 questions.

Why you were not cited — fixing the page that lost

What it is — every question an assistant answered without linking you, the page of yours that should have won it, and what that page is missing. How to use it — click Find the page on a question; the platform reads your best-matching page and scores it from 0 to 100 on eight things answer engines favour:

  • Answers in the opening
  • Headings ask the question
  • FAQ or Q&A block
  • Concrete facts and figures
  • Comparison or spec table
  • Cites its sources
  • Named author or company credentials
  • Visibly current

It lists up to three fixes. If the page is already well shaped, it says so: the gap is authority or awareness, not the page. Re-read page checks it again after you've changed it.

Failed is not absent

A probe that got no answer is excluded from every rate, never counted as a miss. An assistant whose calls all failed reads Unknown, never 0%. When 10% or more of the latest checks failed, a warning says so. If no answer carried a source, Cited instead of you says that is unknown, not "nobody beat you".

AI search volume — how often a term is asked of an assistant rather than typed into Google — also appears as an Asked of AI tile in Keyword Research. Click Check AI volume there to fetch it. It's fetched only on request, because each lookup is a paid call.
