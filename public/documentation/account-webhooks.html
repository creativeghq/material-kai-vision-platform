Account · Outbound Webhooks

/profile?tab=webhooks workspace member

Outbound webhooks let your own systems get notified the moment something happens in your workspace — an invoice is paid, an order ships, a quote is approved. You register an HTTPS endpoint, pick which events it should receive, and the platform POSTs a signed JSON payload to it as each one occurs. Every request is HMAC-signed so your receiver can verify it genuinely came from us. This is the integration surface for wiring MaterialsHub into an ERP, a Zapier/Make scenario, a Slack notifier or your own backend.

Where: your Profile → the Webhooks tab (/profile?tab=webhooks). It's workspace-scoped — any member of the active workspace can manage them (not admin-only, no add-on). Pick a workspace first if none is active. See also Keys & Connections and the API.

Adding an endpoint

  1. Click Add endpoint and enter the URL — it must be https and reachable from the public internet.
  2. Optionally add a description.
  3. Tick the events you want it to receive (at least one; there's deliberately no “all events” option — you subscribe to exactly what you need). Create enables once you've set a URL and an event.
  4. The platform shows your signing secret exactly once — “Copy your signing secret now.” Store it; it can't be shown again, and if you lose it you rotate for a new one.

Each endpoint row has an Active toggle, a Delivery log, Rotate signing secret and Delete. A workspace can have up to 10 endpoints.

Verifying a delivery — signature & headers

Every POST carries these headers so your receiver can trust it:

HeaderWhat it is
X-MaterialKai-EventThe event type.
X-MaterialKai-TimestampUnix seconds when it was sent.
X-MaterialKai-Signaturesha256=<hex> — the HMAC.
X-MaterialKai-DeliveryA delivery id, stable across retries — use it to de-duplicate.

The signature is HMAC-SHA256(secret, "<timestamp>.<raw body>"). To verify: recompute it over the exact raw body with your signing secret, compare, and reject a timestamp more than a few minutes old (replay protection). The body envelope is { "type", "workspace_id", "data" }.

Built to be safe. Endpoints are https-only and SSRF-guarded — the URL is checked at registration and again at delivery (in case DNS is re-pointed), and internal addresses are refused. The signing secret (whsec_…) is generated server-side and never readable back — only shown once, and rotating one invalidates the old immediately.

Delivery log, retries & auto-disable

Each endpoint's Delivery log (When · Event · Status · HTTP · Detail) is your observability — there's no separate “test” button; you watch real deliveries here. A delivery is delivered, pending, delivering, failed, or dropped (queued for an endpoint that was disabled or deleted). Retries back off over increasing delays, and after a run of consecutive failures the platform auto-disables the endpoint with a visible reason — re-enabling it clears the counter. Log rows are pruned after 30 days.

Worked example — notify your ERP when a customer pays. Add an endpoint pointing at your ERP's HTTPS receiver, tick invoice_paid and payment_received, and copy the signing secret. On each payment, your endpoint gets a signed POST; verify the X-MaterialKai-Signature against the raw body, de-dupe on X-MaterialKai-Delivery, and post the payment into your books. Watch the Delivery log to confirm 2xx responses.
