Construction · Site — Diary, Inspections & Snags

/projects/:id?tab=site owner

The Site section is what you use on the job: a dated site diary, inspection checklists you walk with, and the snag (defect) list. It has one time-saving shortcut — you can dictate a site walk out loud and it becomes a diary entry plus the snags you described. And it enforces one honest rule throughout: a failed inspection item has to become a snag, so a failure can't quietly disappear.

Where: a project → the Delivery stage → the Site section (?tab=site), with a Snags / Inspections / Site log toggle and a Dictate a walk button above it. Owner-only; site photos are private (signed links).

Dictating a site walk

What it is — talk through a walk of the site and the platform turns it into records. Why it matters — a walk names a dozen defects in two minutes of speech; typing them up later is where they get lost.

  1. Dictate a walk → Start talking (English or Ελληνικά), then Stop. The transcript is editable.
  2. Read it turns the transcript into a proposal: a site-log entry (with an editable date) plus a list of defects it heard (each with a title and severity, and an amber flag where the room you named matched nothing). Anything unclear goes in a “heard but not recorded” box rather than being silently dropped.
  3. Tick what to keep and Create records — one atomic step, so a retry can't double-write. Nothing is saved until you've checked what it heard, because transcription mishears.

Inspections — a checklist where a failure becomes a snag

New inspection builds a checklist: a name (e.g. “Pre-plaster — Plot 4”), a date, and a list of things to check — each with optional “what good looks like” guidance and a trade (a cost code) that rides along. Walking it, each item is Pass / Fail / N/A. A Fail with no snag yet shows a Raise snag button — and the snag is created already coded to that trade. You can Sign off even a failed walk (it says so plainly); the verdict (Passed / Failed / In progress …) is derived, and unanswered items are counted separately, never counted as a pass.

The guard: the section tracks failed items that have no snag raised — shown per inspection and as a project total — so a failure can't be signed off and forgotten.

Snags (the defect list)

The Snags view is the punch list: Raise a snag with a title, description, severity (low / medium / high / critical), room, trade (cost code), due date, photos, and whether to show it to the client. Filter by room, trade or Show closed. Snags marked client-visible are what make the handover list. Raising a snag from a failed inspection item is idempotent — press it twice and you get the one snag, linked back to the item.

Site log (the diary)

The Site log is the dated diary — “internal, never shown to the client.” Log a site visit records the date, notes, who attended, the weather and photos. It's the running record of what happened on site, day by day.

Worked example. Walk the site dictating as you go — “kitchen, cracked tile by the window, medium; hallway, paint runs, low…”. Read it, confirm the two defects and the log entry, Create records. Later run the Pre-plaster inspection; the one item that fails becomes a snag coded to Plastering. Mark the client-visible snags and they roll into the handover list.

From chat: the Site tools are UI-only — the assistant doesn't run inspections or raise snags.
