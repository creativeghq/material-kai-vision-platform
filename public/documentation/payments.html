Payment Providers

/finance → Settings → Payments finance.manage · owner to activate

Payment providers are the third-party integrations that move money into (and, with Revolut, out of) your workspace. Three are supported — Stripe, Viva.com and Revolut — and each connects your own account, so funds settle directly to you, never through the platform. Whatever you connect and enable is what a customer is offered on the public pay page; whichever method they use, the invoice is reconciled through one shared money path, so a card payment and a bank transfer book the same way. This page compares the three and explains how they're gated; each has its own page for the deep setup.

Where you set them up: Finance → Settings → Payments. Each provider is a separate Pro add-on — only a workspace owner can activate one; other finance managers can request it.

On the public pay page the enabled methods are offered in a fixed order — Stripe, then Viva, then Revolut — with the first preselected. A customer never needs a MaterialsHub account; they follow the token link from their invoice email and pay.

Revolut is two things. Under this category, Revolut covers both Revolut Checkout (a customer payment method, above) and Revolut Business — your own bank account, with a transaction feed, reconciliation, money-out and team cards. Stripe and Viva are checkout-only; Revolut also does your banking.

How a provider becomes usable

A provider is only offered to your customers once three things are true — a deliberate ladder so a half-set-up provider never shows a broken pay button:

  1. Published — the operator has the provider enabled platform-wide (a global kill-switch).
  2. Enabled — your workspace owner has activated the add-on for your workspace.
  3. Connected — you've actually completed the provider's setup (Connect onboarding for Stripe; credentials + a verified webhook for Viva and Revolut).

Turn every method off and the provider simply drops from the customer's choices. Each provider re-checks all three gates at the moment money moves, so a client can never trigger a charge through a provider you haven't fully set up.

What they settle

Every provider writes into the same payment ledger through one shared routine — so a Stripe card payment, a Viva RF bank transfer and a Revolut Pay charge all book identically against the invoice, allocate correctly, and never over-collect. Refunds and disputes are flagged to owners/admins, never auto-reversed — you settle them with a credit note, keeping the books deliberate.

Pick by what you need: want the widest card reach with the least setup → Stripe; a Greek merchant who wants card and auto-reconciling bank transfers → Viva; already bank with Revolut and want your account, cards and reconciliation in one place → Revolut. You can enable more than one; the customer chooses at checkout.
