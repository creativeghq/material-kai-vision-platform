MoodBoards

/moodboard /moodboard/:id

Curate your favourite materials, images and finishes into boards, then turn a board into client-ready presentation sheets, a quote, or a shareable public page. MoodBoards are the bridge between browsing the catalogue, chatting with the Agent Hub, and delivering a finished proposal.

How to open it: top navigation → MoodBoards. Click a board to open it, or New MoodBoard to create one.

What a MoodBoard is for

A MoodBoard is a working collection — a place to gather the products and inspiration for one room, project or concept. You add catalogue materials, pull in images (from Pinterest or from the AI chat), and let the platform suggest matching products. When the board is ready you can render polished presentation sheets, generate a quote from its products, or publish it as a public link a client can open. For larger deliverables that combine sheets from several boards, MoodBoards feed into Projects → Client Views.

The list page

This is the home for every board you own. Each board appears as a card showing:

  • Name — what the board is called.
  • Visibility — a Public or Private badge.
  • Item count — how many materials and images are on the board.

Use New MoodBoard to start a fresh board. From a card you can open it, rename it, change its visibility, or delete it. Deleting a board removes it and its generated sheets — it does not delete the underlying catalogue products.

The board detail page

Opening a board gives you two tabs and an action toolbar.

Items tab

The Items tab is the board itself — the materials and images you've collected, arranged as a visual grid. This is where you review what's on the board, open an individual item, or remove items you no longer want.

Tools tab

The Tools tab holds board-level helpers. The key one is Find Products, which looks across the catalogue for materials that match what's already on the board — a quick way to round out a concept with complementary items.

The action toolbar

Saving from the AI chat: when the Agent Hub returns a product or generated image you like, you can save it straight into a moodboard. A copy of the image is kept on the board, so it survives even if you later delete the conversation it came from.

Presentation sheets

From a board you can produce presentation sheets — polished, client-ready A3 pages. Sheets are generated through the Agent Hub: you describe the sheet you want, the assistant assembles it, and the finished PDF is attached to the board so you can re-open and re-render it later.

Interactive sheets: some sheet types — Lighting plan, Annotated render and Elevation / render pair — open an in-chat canvas first. You place fixture symbols, draw dimensions, or position callouts directly on the image, and only then is the PDF rendered. The passive sheet types render immediately. The full list of what the assistant can build is in Tools & Capabilities.

About the inputs: on interactive sheets every symbol, callout and dimension is placed relative to the backdrop image, so the canvas and the rendered PDF stay in sync whatever the image size. Every sheet outputs an A3 PDF stored with the moodboard, so you can re-open and re-render it later — and the same sheets can be bundled into a project-level deliverable via Projects → Client Views. Sharing & deliverables

A board you make Public can be opened by anyone with its link at /board/:id — handy for sending a quick visual reference to a client or colleague without giving them an account.

When you need a richer, branded deliverable that bundles sheets across multiple boards — with an embedded 3D walkthrough, lighting moods, a live FF&E table and inline client approval — use a Client View. Client Views live at the project level; see Projects → Client Views.

Tip: build the board first, then ask the Agent Hub to generate the sheets you need, then either Generate Quote for pricing or assemble the sheets into a Client View for the final hand-off.

Step by step: create a board and add materials

  1. From the top navigation open MoodBoards, then click New MoodBoard.
  2. Enter a title for the board (and an optional description), then confirm.
  3. Open the new board to reach its detail page with the Items and Tools tabs.
  4. Use Add Products to search the catalogue and add materials to the board.
  5. Optionally use Import from Pinterest to bring in images — they are auto-matched to catalogue products — or open the Tools tab and run Find Products to surface complementary materials.
  6. Arrange the items on the Items tab until the board reflects your concept.

Step by step: produce a presentation sheet

  1. From the board, choose a sheet type — for example a material board, colour palette, lighting plan, FF&E schedule or full deck.
  2. The Agent Hub drives the sheet: describe what you want and the assistant assembles it.
  3. For an interactive sheet, complete the in-chat canvas — place fixture symbols, draw dimensions, or position callouts directly on the image.
  4. The finished sheet renders as a PDF attached to the board, ready to re-open and re-render later. See Tools & Capabilities for the full list of sheets the assistant can build.

Step by step: share a board

  1. Set the board to Public using the Public / Private toggle.
  2. Copy its share link at /board/:id and send it to your client or colleague.
  3. For a richer, project-level deliverable, bundle sheets across boards into a Projects → Client View, or turn the board's products into a quote.
