MoodBoards

/moodboard /moodboard/:id

Curate and organise your favourite materials, then turn a board into client-ready presentation sheets or a quote.

The list page

This is where all your boards live. Create a new one with New MoodBoard; each card shows the board name, whether it's Public or Private, and how many materials it holds. From a card you can open, rename, change visibility or delete the board.

The board detail page

Opening a board reveals two tabs and an action toolbar:

  • Items tab — the materials and images on the board, arranged as a visual collection.
  • Tools tab — board-level helpers, including Find Products to discover matching catalogue items.
  • Add Products — add catalogue materials directly to the board.
  • Import from Pinterest — paste a pin or connect Pinterest to pull images in; imported images are auto-matched to catalogue products.
  • Generate Quote — turn the board's products into a quote (enabled once the board has products).
  • Public / Private toggle and Delete.

Presentation sheets

From a board you can produce presentation sheets — polished, A3 client deliverables generated via the Agent Hub. Sheet types include:

SheetWhat it is
Material boardA grid of selected products with optional descriptions.
Color paletteSwatches with names, optionally pulled from board images.
Concept boardFree-form image layout with captions.
Lighting planFixture symbols placed on a backdrop, with a legend.
Annotated renderCallouts pointing to products on a render.
Elevation / render pairDimensions and tile callouts across an elevation + render.
FF&E scheduleA furniture/fixtures/equipment table, often from a quote.
Area breakdownA one-page room spec (hero render + plan + finishes + palette).
Full deckSeveral sheets combined with a cover into one PDF.

Some sheets are interactive — the platform opens an in-chat canvas so you can place symbols, draw dimensions or position callouts before rendering the PDF.

Sharing

A board can be made Public and shared via a link (/board/:id). For richer, project-level deliverables that bundle sheets across multiple boards, see Projects → Client Views.
