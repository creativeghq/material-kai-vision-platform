Real Estate Pro add-on

/real-estate

Real Estate turns MaterialsHub into a full property agency platform: list, manage and publish properties, enriched with the platform's AI (listing copy, photo tagging, instant valuations); run the sales funnel end to end — leads, buyer matching, viewings, offers and completions; and syndicate your listings to external portals. Two sibling add-ons extend it — Property Management (lettings) and Investments (analysis). It's a purchasable add-on on the module framework, and it plugs into the parts of the platform you already use — CRM, Finance, Contracts, Discovery and the assistant.

Where: enable Real Estate from the App Launcher (Pro add-on), then open it at /real-estate. It's the agent workspace; individual properties, leads and viewings also surface on the relevant CRM records. The two sub-add-ons (Property Management, Investments) appear as extra tabs once enabled.

How it's organised

The Real Estate workspace is a set of tabs — Overview (the agent dashboard), Listings, Leads, Buyers, Viewings, Sales, and the Portal syndication feed; with Lettings and Investments added by the sibling add-ons. There's also a public side: your published properties appear in Discovery and on your agency profile, each with its own public page and inquiry form.

The areas

🏠

Listings & workbench

The multi-step property editor, AI listing copy & photo tagging, brochure PDF, and publishing to Discovery.
🎯

Leads, buyers & matching

Inquiries → CRM leads, buyer requirements + auto-match, AI lead scoring, and GDPR-gated new-listing alerts.
🤝

Viewings, offers & sales

Viewing scheduler (→ calendar), the per-property offer ledger, and sale completion → a Finance commission invoice. Plus instant AVM valuations.
🔑

Property Management (lettings) · add-on

Tenancies, a rent ledger, maintenance work orders and landlord statements.
📈

Investments · add-on

Yield, cap rate, cash-on-cash and cash-flow analysis, with a portfolio roll-up.
🔗

Portal syndication

Publish to external portals — OpenImmo (DACH), Kyero and a generic XML feed, push and pull.

How the pieces connect

  • Leads live in CRM. A public property inquiry converts to a CRM lead; a person's properties and buyer requirements show on their CRM contact record, and lead scoring is the shared CRM/sales scoring.
  • Sales post to Finance. Completing a sale raises the commission as a Finance invoice, so agency income flows into your books.
  • Contracts do the paperwork. Transaction documents are signed through the Contracts module (public e-sign + audit trail).
  • Viewings hit your calendar. Scheduled viewings sync into the platform calendar.
  • The assistant can run it. A dedicated “Estate” agent persona (a property advisor) and the manage_real_estate toolkit let you list, match and update from chat — see AI Agents.
  • AI is credit-metered. Listing-copy generation, photo tagging and valuations consume workspace credits.

Roles & visibility

There's a dedicated Estate Agent role for the people running listings. Each listing has visibility controls — an “Open for all” toggle and a public-listings switch — so you choose what's shown on your public agency page and Discovery versus kept internal.

Getting started: enable Real Estate → add a listing in the workbench (let the AI draft the copy) → publish it → capture leads and match buyers → schedule viewings, take offers, and complete the sale. Add Property Management if you also let, and syndication to push to portals.
