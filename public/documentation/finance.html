Finance & Invoicing

/finance /finance/invoices/:id finance.manage

A complete back-office: revenue and profit, receivables and payables, the full set of financial documents, Greek e-invoicing, warehouse and dispatch, planning, and reporting — all in one workspace.

What this area is for

Finance is the accounting and billing centre of your workspace. The Dashboard tab gives you the financial health of the business at a glance, and the remaining tabs let you create and manage every kind of document, transmit invoices to the Greek tax authority, run the warehouse/dispatch side, plan ahead and report. Because it's deep, this guide splits Finance into focused sub-pages — use the list below or the sidebar.

Finance at a glance (Dashboard)

The default Dashboard tab summarises:

  • Revenue & profit for the period.
  • Receivables — money customers owe you.
  • Payables — money you owe suppliers.
  • Follow-up queue — items that need action (e.g. overdue documents).
