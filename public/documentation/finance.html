Finance & Invoicing

/finance /finance/invoices/:id finance.manage

Finance is the complete back-office of your workspace: revenue and profit, what you're owed and what you owe, the full set of financial documents, Greek e-invoicing, expenses and VAT, planning and reporting — all in one place. This page is your orientation: it teaches you how to read the Dashboard at a glance, then points you to the focused sub-pages for everything deeper.

How to open it: top navigation → Finance. The Dashboard tab opens first; use the tab bar across the top for everything else.

What it is

Finance is the accounting and billing centre of your workspace. It's where invoices are created and transmitted to the Greek tax authority, where you track every euro coming in and going out — including every expense filed against your ΑΦΜ — and where you plan ahead, prepare the VAT return and report. The Dashboard tab is the front door — a single screen that tells you the financial health of the business — and the rest of the tabs handle the day-to-day work behind those numbers.

Why it matters

Most of your business decisions come down to two questions: how much money is the business actually making, and is cash coming in fast enough to cover what's going out. The Dashboard answers both without you opening a spreadsheet. Once you can read it, you'll spot an overdue customer, a thin-margin month, or a slow-collection problem the moment you log in — instead of finding out at the end of the quarter.

How to read the Dashboard

The Dashboard has three layers: a row of KPI cards that is always visible, one period selector that governs everything below it, and four views — Position, Flow, Spend and Activity — each answering one question. Switch views with the underline tabs under the period selector; only one view is on screen at a time.

The KPI cards

Across the top, five cards give you the headline numbers. Read them left to right as a quick health check:

  • Cash in bank — your actual cash position: all payments in minus all payments out. Where AR/AP show what's promised, this shows what you have. Its subtext flags any cash “not yet invoiced” — see deposits below.
  • AR outstanding — what customers currently owe you, with the overdue part underneath (or "All on schedule"). Customer credit you hold is stated separately and never netted into this figure.
  • AP outstanding — what you currently owe suppliers, with a count of open bills.
  • This month revenue (net) — net revenue for the current month, with its margin and margin %.
  • DSO — Days Sales Outstanding: roughly how many days it takes to collect what you're owed. Lower is better. It also shows how many quotes need follow-up.

The period selector

One control sets the window for every view: This month, Last month, Last 3 months or Year to date. The P&L, the Spend overview and the sales insights all read the same period, so two blocks can never describe different windows.

Position — how am I doing?

  • Nightly checks strip — the integrity checks that run every night on your finance data. When one finds something, the strip says how many things need a look; a clean night says so in one quiet line, and a strip that could not load says that instead of implying all is well.
  • Profit & loss — income less expenses for the period, from your own documents: Income, Expenses, Net, and VAT (collected minus paid, labelled with its own direction — payable or refundable). A second row shows Paid to owners (owner pay, profit split and partner withdrawals — already included in Expenses) and Profit before owner payments (Net with those added back). A third, shaded row shows ΑΑΔΕ's figures for the same months from the myDATA book, with how many months were read. The two sides are never merged — ΑΑΔΕ confirms your books, it is never added to them. Links take you to the ΑΑΔΕ book and the VAT return.
  • What the expenses are made of — inside the P&L card: how VAT was treated (VAT charged, Reverse charge, No VAT charged, Outside VAT) with document counts, and where the money went — Greece, the EU, outside the EU, not stated — with All expenses summed beside them. This is where rent and payroll show up without you needing to know their ΑΑΔΕ codes.
  • Gap and exclusions — when ΑΑΔΕ holds more expense than your books, an amber line states the difference and how many received documents are still not booked, with Book them → (to By Supplier). Costs you marked settled outside the books are stated in their own line as NOT in the figures. Underneath, Billed to us, Paid, Still owed on those and Owed right now — all time show the supplier-bill side.
  • Monthly P&L — income, expenses and net month by month, switchable between Your books and the ΑΑΔΕ book. Its To book column counts received documents that have not become an expense yet.
  • Revenue trend — the revenue line with its period-over-period growth.

A figure is a value or a reason. Wherever a figure cannot be read, or ΑΑΔΕ has not been fetched for a month, the card says why instead of showing a zero. A currency mix is reported as such rather than summed.

Flow — where is the money?

  • Cash by account — the live balance of each bank and cash account, with a link to manage them in Settings → Accounts & Payments.
  • Receivables by age and Payables by age — side-by-side ageing buckets.
  • Received, not yet invoiced — deposits / on-account (shown only when there's a balance) — cash you took before raising a document. It is in the bank but isn't revenue yet. Each row offers Issue document, turning the order the deposit was taken on into a receipt/invoice so the money becomes revenue.
  • Cash flow (next 90 days) — expected In, Out and Net per month from dated invoices and bills.
  • Gross margin (last 12 months) — Revenue, COGS, Margin and % per month: what you made on what you sold. This is not the P&L — it has no operating expenses in it.

Spend — what are the expenses?

An overview of the period's costs: Total spend against the period before it (with the change in %), the VAT on it, the number of documents and suppliers; what share of the money is filed under a real category; Where it goes (by category), What kind of spend (purchases, rent, contracts, your own entries, from outside Greece…), Where it comes from (Greece / EU / outside the EU / not stated); and supplier concentration — what share the five largest suppliers, and the single largest, account for. Documents still sitting in the inlet they arrived through (for example the built-in myAADE category) are not a real category; Suggest categories takes you to By Supplier to file them.

Activity — what happened?

Next follow-ups (with View all), Recent invoices (with New to create one and each row's actions menu), and Sales insights for the period: top customers, best-selling products, recent orders and top outstanding customers.

Finance that reaches you

Besides the scheduled Finance Digest email, five finance events notify the workspace owners and admins through a default flow, so you don't have to go and look:

EventWhen it fires
Expense BookedA received myDATA document became a supplier bill.
Expense Settled Outside the BooksA cost was marked paid outside the platform and kept out of the P&L.
Supplier Credit Note ReceivedCredit notes a supplier issued you were brought in from myDATA.
Planned Payment Due TomorrowA scheduled payment or expected receipt falls due (or reaches its reminder date).
VAT Return ReadyA finished filing period has ΑΑΔΕ figures on both sides and can be filed — see VAT Return. This one cannot be switched off by a workspace, because a filing deadline is a legal obligation.

Access & what it affects

Finance is gated by the finance.manage capability and module entitlement, so only authorised members reach it. Note that /settings redirects here — workspace settings live under Finance, not separately. An Admin sub-view is available to operators for configuration.

Everything you do across the sub-pages feeds back into this Dashboard: an issued invoice raises Revenue and AR, a recorded payment moves cash to In, an expense increases AP. The Dashboard is the live mirror of all the work happening in the tabs below it.
