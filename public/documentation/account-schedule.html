Account · Schedule

/profile?tab=schedule

The Schedule tab is your personal calendar hub — it brings together three things that used to be separate tabs: your Availability (when clients may book you), your Bookings (the requests they've made), and My calendar (the meetings you've logged in CRM). It's organised as a small side-nav so you set your hours, work your incoming bookings and see your meetings all in one place.

Where: your Profile → the Schedule tab (/profile?tab=schedule), with three sections reachable directly: ?tab=schedule&section=availability, …&section=appointments (Bookings, the default) and …&section=calendar. Old ?tab=appointments and ?tab=calendar links now redirect here.

Availability — when clients can book you

What it is — the hours you're open to being booked. Why it matters — it's what turns your public profile into something a client can act on: set your windows and a “Book a time” option appears on your public page.

  • Accept bookings — the master toggle. Turn it off and your public profile shows no booking option and the Bookings section stays empty.
  • Set your windows — a month calendar; click a future date and add one or more time windows (options run 7:00 AM–9:00 PM in 30-minute steps). Clients book 1-hour slots inside those windows.
  • Save Availability to publish it.

No external calendar to connect. Availability is a simple per-day window you set here; there's no Google/Outlook sync, and the “booking link” is just your public profile at /u/:userId.

Bookings — requests from your public profile

What it is — the meetings clients have requested with you, from your public profile. Why it matters — it's the queue you actually work: confirm the ones that suit you, decline the ones that don't, and keep the details in one place. You don't create bookings here — clients do, from your public page; this is where you respond to them.

  • Statuses — each booking is pending, confirmed, cancelled or completed; a badge shows how many are pending your response.
  • Filter — search by client or service and filter by status.
  • The detail drawer — open a booking to Confirm or Decline it, Mark as completed afterwards, Open inbox to message the client, set “What is this about?” to tie it to a project or property, and keep Private notes only you see.

It feeds your automations. Confirming, completing or cancelling a booking raises a Flow event (appointment confirmed / completed / cancelled), so you can send the client a confirmation or reminder without doing it by hand.

My calendar — the meetings you've logged

What it is — the CRM meetings you've recorded against your contacts and companies, split into Upcoming meetings and Past & done. Each row links back to the contact or company and shows its reminder badges. Why it matters — it's your own agenda of logged meetings alongside the client bookings above.

Meetings are logged from a CRM contact's Activity tab (not created here) — this section is the read-across view of all of yours in one list.

Day-to-day: set your Availability once (and keep Accept bookings on), then live in Bookings — confirm what fits, decline what doesn't, mark meetings done, and let the flow events handle the confirmations. Glance at My calendar for the meetings you've logged in CRM.
