Greek e-invoicing (myDATA / ΑΑΔΕ)

/finance finance.manage

Issue compliant invoices and transmit them to the Greek tax authority (ΑΑΔΕ / myDATA) directly from the platform — no separate accounting portal, no manual re-entry. This guide explains what myDATA actually is, why up-front VAT validation saves you money, and walks the issue-to-transmit flow step by step.

How to open it: e-invoicing happens automatically when you issue invoices/receipts. You configure it once under top navigation → Finance → Settings (see the Settings guide, section e-Invoicing).

What it is

myDATA (my Digital Accounting and Tax Application) is the Greek tax authority's electronic bookkeeping system, run by ΑΑΔΕ. Every business invoice and receipt in Greece must be reported to it. The platform connects to myDATA through the Novus connector, so when you issue a document here it is transmitted electronically to ΑΑΔΕ as the official record. There is no second system to copy figures into — the document you create in Finance is the filed document.

In practice this means the invoice you build under Documents → Invoices (or the receipt you ring up at the POS) becomes the tax-authority record the moment you issue it. The manual step of re-keying everything into a separate accounting portal disappears.

Why it matters

myDATA reporting is a legal obligation, not an optional integration — Greek businesses must transmit their documents. Doing it inline has two concrete payoffs:

  • One source of truth. The document you issue is the document that's filed, so your books and the authority's records can't drift apart.
  • No double entry. You stop re-typing invoices into a separate portal, which removes both the work and the transcription errors that come with it.

Multi-tenant by design

What it is — transmission is per-workspace. Each workspace issues under its own VAT identity and tax-authority authorisation, configured once and reused on every submission. Why it matters — many businesses share the platform, and this design guarantees they never cross-file: your documents always go out under your VAT number, never anyone else's. Your issuer details are set up a single time (in Settings → Business Identity) and applied automatically thereafter.

Why VAT validation matters

What it is — before a document is issued, the platform validates the counter-party's VAT number: Greek numbers against the ΑΑΔΕ registry, EU numbers against VIES. This ties into the same Greek business lookup used across CRM.

Why it matters — a wrong or inactive VAT number is one of the most common reasons myDATA rejects a submission. Validating up front catches the problem while it's still cheap to fix — you correct one field before issuing, instead of unwinding a rejected or mis-filed document afterwards. It also keeps your counter-party details accurate, which feeds clean data into CRM and your reports.

Because VAT is validated up front, fixing a wrong customer VAT before issuing is much cheaper than correcting a rejected or mis-filed document later. You can make this enforcement strict in Settings → General — toggles there can block issuance when a buyer VAT is ΑΑΔΕ-inactive or was never validated.

How it works — the issue-to-transmit flow

From your side it's a single Issue click, but several things happen in sequence behind it. Here is the end-to-end flow:

  1. Create the document. Build an invoice under Documents → Invoices, or issue a receipt at the POS.
  2. VAT is validated. The counter-party's VAT number is checked against the ΑΑΔΕ registry (Greek) or VIES (EU). If your General settings are set to block, an invalid number stops you here — before any filing happens.
  3. The document is transmitted. On issue, it is sent to ΑΑΔΕ / myDATA through the Novus connector and marked accordingly, so you can see it's been filed.
  4. It flows into the rest of Finance. The issued document feeds your Receivables, the Dashboard KPIs and your reports — automatically.

What it affects

Once e-invoicing is connected, transmission is woven into normal issuing — there is no separate export step, and every issued invoice or receipt becomes part of your official myDATA record. It also drives the downstream figures: each transmitted document raises your receivables, updates the Dashboard, and appears in your reports, so the same action that satisfies the tax authority keeps your back-office in sync.

Setup: e-invoicing requires your workspace's fiscal details and tax-authority authorisation to be configured (under Finance → Settings / by an administrator). Once connected, transmission happens as part of issuing — there's no separate export step.
