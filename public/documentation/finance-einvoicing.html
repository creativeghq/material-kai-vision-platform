Greek e-invoicing (myDATA) — set it up and use it

/finance finance.manage

If you invoice in Greece, every invoice and receipt has to be reported electronically to the tax authority (ΑΑΔΕ) through myDATA. MaterialsHub does this for you at the moment you issue a document — but only after you've filled in a few details. This page walks the whole thing end to end: what you need before you start, exactly which fields to fill and where to find them, and how issuing an invoice actually transmits it.

The short version: fill in your Business Identity, check your document numbering and paste your myDATA credentials, register your VAT with the provider (a tracked ladder that ends with Novus saying Active), then issue invoices with “Submit to myDATA on issue” ticked. The setup areas are all under Finance → Settings; issuing happens on Finance → Invoices.

Before you start — what you need from ΑΑΔΕ

myDATA reporting uses credentials you get from the tax authority, not your normal TaxisNet login. Have these ready:

  • Your company's tax details — VAT number (ΑΦΜ), tax office (ΔΟΥ) and registered address. (The app can fetch most of these for you — see Step 1.)
  • myDATA REST API credentials — a User ID and a Subscription Key, issued from your ΑΑΔΕ myDATA account when you register for REST API access. You'll paste both into the platform (Step 2). These are what let the platform talk to myDATA on your behalf.

Don't have the myDATA REST credentials yet? You can still set everything else up and create draft invoices; you just won't be able to transmit until the credentials are in. Get them from your ΑΑΔΕ myDATA account's API-access / authorisation section (they appear as a user id and an Ocp-Apim-Subscription-Key).

Step 1 — Your Business Identity (the issuer details)

Where: top nav → Finance → Settings → Business Identity. These details print on every document and are sent to myDATA as the issuer — they're mandatory.

  1. Make sure the language toggle is on GR (Greek details are what myDATA expects).
  2. In the Billing sub-tab, type your VAT number (e.g. EL123456789) and click Fetch from ΑΑΔΕ — it fills your Company name, Activity, Tax office and registered address from the official registry.
  3. Fill anything not returned: GEMI number, Phone, Email, and the bank/IBAN and logo under Bank & Logo.
  4. Click Save.

Full field list: Settings · Business Identity.

Step 2 — Document series & myDATA credentials

Where: Finance → Settings → Documents.

  1. Establishments. Click Add establishment and add your premises. Your headquarters is branch 0; add branches as needed (each reports to myDATA under its own establishment number). Fill Branch number, Name, and the address; Save.
  2. Document Types & Series. Each document type has its own numbering range, so a retail receipt never takes a number out of your invoice sequence. You don't have to create them first: if a type has none, one is created automatically on the first document, numbered from 1, with the Greek mnemonic for the type (e.g. ΤΠ for a sales invoice, ΑΛΠ for a retail receipt, ΔΑ for a delivery note). Add a series yourself when you need to continue from your previous software (last invoice was 1450 → start at 1451) or assign it to a branch. The number printed on the document is the same one transmitted to myDATA.
  3. Optional — a range per sales channel. When adding a series, Applies to lets you give one channel its own numbering: Skroutz, Shopify + WooCommerce, Shopify only, WooCommerce only, Own online store or Register (POS). A document from that channel's order uses that range; everything else keeps the All sales range. Nothing changes until you add one.
  4. myDATA Inbox (Received Documents). Paste the credentials from Step 0: User ID (the field is labelled “sent as aade-user-id”) and Subscription Key (labelled Ocp-Apim-Subscription-Key). These also power the “Sync from myDATA” button that pulls in documents suppliers issued to you.

Full detail: Settings · Documents.

Step 3 — Register your VAT with the provider

Where: Finance → Settings → e-Invoicing → Register for e-invoicing (also the first step of Start Here). Whether your VAT may transmit is the provider's (Novus's) answer, so it is no longer a status you set by hand — it is a seven-step ladder the platform reads back from Novus.

  1. Fill the administrator's full name and ΑΦΜ, tick what you issue (B2B and/or B2C), and click Send to Novus — once.
  2. Download the contract, get it signed, and Upload the signed PDF.
  3. Wait for Novus to check and approve and activate the client.
  4. Accept the provider declaration in myAADE, then click Mark done on that step.

When the badge reads Active — this VAT can issue, you're ready. The same page holds the Transmit invoices to myDATA switch (on by default) and the list of any missing issuer fields. Full walkthrough: Settings · e-Invoicing.

Step 4 — Issue an invoice (and transmit it)

Where: Finance → Invoices → New. The editor has myDATA-specific fields — here's what each one is and what to put:

The issue-time switches

  1. Tick “Submit to myDATA on issue” — this is what actually transmits the document.
  2. Optionally tick Print online code / QR, Include in MYF report, send by email/SMS, etc.
  3. Click Issue now — this assigns the official sequential number and date and (with the toggle on) sends it to myDATA. Until you issue, it stays an editable draft.

Full editor reference: Documents · Invoices.

Selling abroad — intra-community, export and reverse charge

What it is — a sale to a business outside Greece gets its own myDATA document type instead of going out as a domestic 1.1 with Greek VAT. Why it matters — a cross-border sale filed as domestic is a wrong filing, and the buyer's country decides which document is right.

BuyerGoodsServicesVAT
Business in another EU country (intra-community)1.22.20%, with its exemption cause; buyer VAT must be VIES-validated
Business outside the EU (export)1.32.30%, with its exemption cause
A consumer, anywhere11.111.2Stays a retail document (EU distance selling / OSS is not modelled)
  • Proposed for you. In the invoice editor the document type follows the buyer's country, with a note explaining why — e.g. “an intra-community sale is 1.2 at 0% VAT, with its exemption cause (art. 33 for goods), and needs the buyer's VAT validated in VIES. Goods collected in Greece are a domestic sale — pick 1.1.”
  • Refused if inconsistent. Transmission is refused when a cross-border type's buyer country contradicts it, when any line carries Greek VAT on one, or when an intra-community document's buyer VAT is not VIES-validated.
  • Classification and name follow. The income classification is set for the cross-border type, and a foreign buyer's name is always sent.
  • Reverse charge (art. 39a) is a domestic sale that reverses the VAT: it stays 1.1, only the VAT treatment changes.
  • Cross-border services get 2.2 / 2.3, but no automatic exemption cause — where a service is supplied depends on the service (work on a property in Greece is Greek), so the line states its own cause.

Peppol — a foreign buyer's e-invoicing address

On a CRM company, Check Peppol (beside the VAT checks) searches the public Peppol Directory for that VAT number and records the result as a badge: Peppol: receives invoices, Peppol: listed, no invoice type, or Not on Peppol · date. If the directory can't be reached you get an error — never a false “not listed”.

How to tell it worked — the “mD” column

Back on the Invoices list, the mD column shows each document's myDATA transmission status, so you can see at a glance which invoices have been reported and which haven't.

The transmissions log — every attempt, and what to do when one fails

The mD column tells you a document's current myDATA state; the myDATA transmissions tab (/finance?tab=mydata_transmissions, beside the myDATA Book in the Finance rail) is the full audit — “every attempt to file a document with AADE, newest first — one row per attempt, so a document that took three tries shows all three.” A banner counts what hasn't landed (“N attempts have not landed”).

  • One row per attempt, with columns When, Document (links to the invoice, credit note or delivery note), Type, Series / AA, Status, MARK, Credits and Actions.
  • Filter by outcome — All attempts / Accepted / Queued (AADE offline) / Rejected / Errored / Cancelled — with a refresh button.
  • The status distinguishes “refused” from “unknown”. Accepted = registered at AADE with a MARK; Rejected = AADE refused it (no MARK — fix it and send again), and the row shows AADE's error code and message; Errored = the provider couldn't be reached, so it's genuinely unknown whether it was filed — “check before re-sending” rather than assume it failed.
  • Send again appears only on a failed attempt that isn't a dropped-connection case and still has a document to resend. An accepted row gets no retry button, because AADE refuses a duplicate of a document it already registered.

What the customer's copy now carries. Three things AADE requires on the printed/emailed document — which were being transmitted but left off the customer's copy — now print on both the PDF and the on-screen preview: “Transmitted via” (the e-invoicing provider's name/website, beside the MARK), the issue date and time in your own timezone (not the server's UTC), and, for a document settled at a terminal, a CARD / IRIS PAYMENT block (payment identity, amount, terminal and the provider signature).

When the provider can't be reached

A document is never silently sent twice. If the provider doesn't answer (it is given about 75 seconds), the platform first asks the provider what it already holds before any resend — on invoices, credit notes and delivery notes alike — and a background sweep retries what is genuinely missing. A document issued while the provider was unreachable is marked as such to AADE and prints Transmission Failure_1 or _2; it is refused once it falls outside myDATA's one-day window.

The QR code and the customer's filed copy

The QR printed on the PDF, on template-rendered documents and on POS receipts now opens the provider's filed copy of the document (the copy the provider registers with AADE), with AADE's own link only as a fallback. Emails and the in-app “verify” buttons still send people to AADE.

The public invoice page

/pay/:token

What it is — the page a customer reaches from an invoice's payment link. Besides paying, it now shows a Filed with ΑΑΔΕ (myDATA) panel — the number, MARK, UID, authentication code and issue time, a Filed copy link, a Verify at ΑΑΔΕ link and “Transmitted via …” — both while the invoice is payable and once it is paid.

  • Download PDF — every state (payable, paid, closed) offers our own PDF of that exact document. Downloads are recorded in the document's delivery trail.
  • Cancelled or credited documents — an old link no longer errors. It shows Cancelled — number or Credited — number (“reversed by a credit note, so there is nothing to pay on it”), read-only, with its filed facts and the PDF.

Card payments on a terminal, after the fact

A payment terminal can now settle documents outside the till, through one dialog (Sign for the terminal → charge it → enter the transaction id → Terminal approved — file it):

  • Card payment on terminal — in an issued invoice's actions menu, for the amount still due (Card or IRIS).
  • Refund to the card — the card button on a not-yet-transmitted credit note.
  • Card receipt (8.4) — for a wholesale invoice paid on a terminal, the invoice detail offers Issue the 8.4, linked to the invoice (see Compliance → POS interconnection).

All three need an active terminal registered under Finance → Settings → POS terminals.

Checking AADE's side — the myDATA Book

The transmissions log is what we sent; the myDATA Book is what AADE holds, month by month, with a comparison against the platform's ledger.

VAT validation (why it matters)

Before a document is issued, the customer's VAT number is checked — Greek numbers against the ΑΑΔΕ registry, EU numbers against VIES. You can make this stricter in Settings → General (block issuance to an inactive or never-validated VAT). Fixing a wrong VAT before issuing is far cheaper than correcting a rejected myDATA submission afterwards.

Quick checklist: ① Business Identity saved (with Fetch from ΑΑΔΕ) → ② numbering checked and myDATA User ID + Subscription Key pasted → ③ VAT registered with the provider (badge reads Active) → ④ issue an invoice with “Submit to myDATA on issue”. After that, every issue reports automatically and the mD column confirms it.
