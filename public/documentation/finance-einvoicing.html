Greek e-invoicing (myDATA / ΑΑΔΕ)

/finance finance.manage

Issue compliant invoices and transmit them to the Greek tax authority (ΑΑΔΕ / myDATA) directly from the platform.

What it does

When you issue an invoice (or a receipt from the POS), the platform can transmit it electronically to myDATA, the Greek tax authority's e-bookkeeping system. This replaces manual entry into a separate accounting portal — the document you create here becomes the official record.

Multi-tenant by design

Transmission is per-workspace: each workspace issues under its own VAT identity and authorisation, so businesses sharing the platform never cross-file. Your workspace's issuer details are configured once and used on every submission.

VAT validation before issuing

Customer/supplier VAT numbers are validated before a document is issued — Greek numbers against the ΑΑΔΕ registry and EU numbers against VIES — reducing rejected submissions and keeping counter-party details correct. This ties into the Greek business lookup used across CRM.

How it fits the document flow

  1. Create an invoice under Documents → Invoices (or issue a receipt at the POS).
  2. The counter-party's VAT is validated.
  3. On issue, the document is transmitted to ΑΑΔΕ / myDATA and marked accordingly.
  4. The issued document feeds your Receivables, Dashboard and reports.

Setup: e-invoicing requires your workspace's fiscal details and tax-authority authorisation to be configured (under Finance → Settings / by an administrator). Once connected, transmission happens as part of issuing — there's no separate export step.

Because VAT is validated up front, fixing a wrong customer VAT before issuing is much cheaper than correcting a rejected or mis-filed document later.
