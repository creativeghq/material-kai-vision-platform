Settings · Business Identity

/finance finance.manage

Business Identity is where you enter your company's details once so they appear correctly everywhere — on every invoice and receipt, in what you send to the Greek tax authority (myDATA), on your public profile/storefront, and in the emails the platform sends. This page lists every field, where it lives, what to put, and why it matters, so you can fill it in confidently.

Where: top navigation → Finance → Settings tab → Business Identity. The section is split into five sub-tabs across the top: 1. Billing, 2. Customer Contact, 3. Personal, 4. My Company and Bank & Logo. Click Save after editing.

Two things to know before you fill it in

  • Every text field is bilingual. Each field shows an ·en / ·gr marker, and there's an EN / GR toggle at the top. Fill the Greek version for Greek documents/myDATA; add the English version if you also invoice international clients. The right language prints automatically per document.
  • Don't type it all by hand. On the Billing tab, enter your VAT number and click Fetch from ΑΑΔΕ — the platform pulls your official company name, activity, tax office and registered address straight from the tax registry, so they exactly match what myDATA expects. Then just fill the gaps below.

1. Billing — your legal identity (prints on documents & goes to myDATA)

This is the mandatory, official part. Where: the 1. Billing sub-tab. Why it matters: these exact details head every invoice/receipt and are transmitted to myDATA as the issuer — if any are wrong, your documents are legally wrong.

5. Bank & Logo — payment details & branding on documents

Where: the Bank & Logo sub-tab. Why it matters: these are the two things customers most need on an invoice — how to pay you, and your branding.

FieldWhat to enterWhy it's needed

Logo (printed on invoices)Upload your company logo.Brands every generated document PDF.

How to fill it in — recommended order

  1. On Billing, set the toggle to GR, type your VAT number, click Fetch from ΑΑΔΕ, then fill any gaps (GEMI, phone, email).
  2. On Bank & Logo, add your IBAN(s) and upload your logo.
  3. On Customer Contact, add your brand name, website, socials and opening hours.
  4. On Personal, set the responsible person and the notifications email.
  5. On My Company, set the company type and main activity.
  6. (Optional) switch to EN and add English versions if you invoice abroad. Click Save.

What this powers

  • The header/footer, payment details and logo on every document PDF.
  • The issuer block sent to myDATA — a prerequisite for Greek e-invoicing.
  • Your public profile and online store (brand, contact, hours).
  • Where the platform sends you notifications (the notifications email).
