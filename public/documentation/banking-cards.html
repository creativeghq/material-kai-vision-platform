Team cards & expenses

Revolut Business · finance.manage

With Revolut connected, you can issue virtual cards to your team, cap what each can spend, freeze them instantly, and — the part that saves real time — have every card purchase, with its receipt, flow automatically into the right person's expense report. No chasing paper receipts, no manually keying card spend into the books, and no card left live after someone leaves.

Where: the Cards & expenses panel in the Revolut Business module settings (open it, then click Load to fetch your team, their cards, and recent expenses).

Issuing & controlling cards

  • Issue a virtual card — pick a team member and an optional label; the card is created instantly (virtual only).
  • Invite by email — for someone not yet in your Revolut team, send a card invitation to their email.
  • Set a spend limit — an inline limit sets a monthly (or weekly) cap on the card.
  • Freeze / unfreeze — instantly block or restore a card with a toggle.

Card spend → the right person's expense report

What it is — the panel shows each card's expenses (last 90 days) with date, merchant and amount, and each receipt downloads on demand — fetched live from Revolut, never stored by the platform until you import it. Import to expense reports then files each expense against the person who holds the card.

How the attribution is safe — it's a deterministic chain, never name-guessing: the expense → the card holder → that holder's email → the matching HR employee → their user → their monthly “Revolut card” expense report → a line item (marked company card, awaiting approval) with the receipt attached exactly where a manually uploaded one would go. A card whose holder can't be matched to an employee is logged as unmatched, never invented — so spend is never attributed to the wrong person.

Large-spend alerts

Any company-card purchase of €100 or more raises the Large Card Spend event, which you can route through Flows (e.g. notify the owner, post to a channel). It carries the amount, so a flow can filter to a higher threshold if €100 is too noisy. It fires once per payment.

Offboarding — cards freeze themselves

You don't have to remember to kill a leaver's card. A background sweep freezes (never unfreezes) any active card whose holder maps to an employee who has an end-date in the past or is marked terminated. So the moment HR records someone's departure, their card stops working — closing the gap where an ex-employee's card stays live because nobody thought to cancel it.

It also runs on a schedule. The same import and offboard-freeze run automatically on the nightly sync, so card expenses keep flowing into reports and leavers' cards keep getting frozen even if nobody opens the panel.

Worked example — a card for a new hire. Open the Cards & expenses panel → Load → issue a virtual card to the employee with a €500 monthly limit. They spend on it; receipts and lines appear against their monthly expense report automatically (pending approval). If a purchase tops €100 you get a Large Card Spend alert. When they later leave and HR sets their end-date, the card freezes itself on the next sweep.
