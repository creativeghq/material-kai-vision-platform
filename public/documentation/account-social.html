Account · Social Accounts

/profile?tab=social-accounts

Social Accounts is where you connect your social media accounts and — for workspace owners and admins — run WhatsApp: get a number, write templates and forms, send campaigns and watch delivery. Connected accounts become publishing targets, and their DMs, comments and lead-form submissions arrive in the Inbox. This guide covers what each section is, why it matters, how to use it, and what it changes.

My accounts — connecting a platform

What it is — the list of your Connected accounts, then a card per platform you haven't connected yet (Instagram, Facebook, LinkedIn, TikTok, Pinterest, YouTube, X (Twitter), Threads, Google Business and WhatsApp). Why it matters — once connected, you publish to it from MaterialsHub instead of switching apps, and its messages reach your Inbox.

  1. Open Profile → Social Accounts → My accounts.
  2. Click Connect on the platform and approve access on the platform's own page; you're brought back here.
  3. Use the bin icon on a connected account to disconnect it, and Refresh to re-sync the list.
  4. New post (top-right) opens the assistant ready to publish a post.

Google reviews on your profile. A connected Google Business location offers Show these reviews on my public profile — off by default. They appear in their own section, clearly marked as Google reviews, never mixed into the rating clients leave here.

What connecting brings into the Inbox

Connected accounts don't just publish — what people send them reaches a person:

  • DMs and comments under your own posts become Inbox conversations (sources Social DM and Social comments). A reply to a comment is public; you can answer the person by DM instead.
  • Lead forms — a Meta Instant Form submission becomes an Inbox conversation (source Lead form) holding the answers. Nothing is added to your CRM silently; Add to CRM in the side panel runs the duplicate search first.
  • Where they came from — when a DM started from an ad or an ig.me / m.me link, that entry point is kept on the conversation.
  • WhatsApp calls — an ended, missed or failed call shows as an event in that customer's conversation.

Automatic comment replies. In Flows, the Social Comment Received trigger can be followed by Reply to Comment (public) or Reply by DM (private) — add a condition on the comment text for keywords (“price”, “where can I buy”). Each comment is answered at most once. Nothing like this runs unless you build the flow.

Post analytics

What it is — how each published post performs, per network. Figures are kept as a daily history, so you can see a post's numbers change over time rather than only the latest. A post removed on a network after it was published is marked deleted for that network. Why it matters — you can see which content keeps working and which fades.

WhatsApp channel owner / admin

What it is — where you connect a WhatsApp Business number. Connect WhatsApp runs Meta's own sign-up, where you pick the business account and number; I already have Meta credentials is the alternative. Each connected number shows its settings — Send read receipts (the blue tick, per number), default number, quota — and you can sync, check its health, or import earlier conversation history. What it affects: its conversations appear under the Inbox's WhatsApp tab; with more than one number the Inbox gets a number switcher.

Get a number owner / admin

What it is — buy a phone number for WhatsApp without leaving MaterialsHub. Why it matters — connecting WhatsApp used to assume you already owned a suitable number.

  1. Pick a Country, Type (Local / Mobile / National / Toll-free) and optional Area code, then Check availability.
  2. The price is shown as credits a month. Click Buy a … number. The first month is taken from the workspace's credits when the number is issued (and refunded if nothing is issued); WhatsApp is enabled on it automatically.
  3. Some countries need an identity check: use Identity check link / Open KYC form, complete it on Zernio's page, then buy again — such a number is charged when it goes live.
  • Your numbers lists what the workspace holds. Bought numbers are billed monthly in credits and can be released here; a number you connected yourself is not billed by us.
  • Monthly charges shows each month's rental. A charge that failed (not enough credits) is marked and still owed — top up and use Retry the charge to settle it and bring a held number back.

WhatsApp templates

Pre-approved messages Meta requires for anything sent outside the 24-hour reply window — a campaign, a first message, or a follow-up days later. Create template submits one to Meta from here (no trip to WhatsApp Manager); once approved they're offered in the Inbox (Send a template), in follow-ups and in campaigns.

WhatsApp forms

What it is — a form that opens inside WhatsApp, so a customer answers in fields instead of a paragraph. The answers land in the conversation as a message — an order form feeds Inbox order intake exactly like a typed order. Why it matters — structured answers (quantity, size, delivery date) instead of a message you have to decode.

  1. New form: a Title, a Purpose (Order request, Lead capture, Booking request, Support request, Survey, Other), the WhatsApp number, the Message sent with the form, the button texts, and the Fields (each with a type, required or not, options for a dropdown, and an optional hint).
  2. It's saved as a draft and checked by WhatsApp; preview it in WhatsApp's renderer.
  3. Publish it — it becomes sendable and is frozen. Change a published form with New version; retire one so customers can no longer open it (its answers stay).
  4. Send it from any WhatsApp conversation in the Inbox while the 24-hour window is open (the form button in the reply box).

Latest answers lists the last 50 submissions, each linking to its conversation.

WhatsApp campaigns

What it is — send an approved template to a segment. Create campaign opens New WhatsApp Campaign:

  • Name, Send from (the number) and Template (approved templates only).
  • Audience — anyone matching any of: Everyone who has messaged us on WhatsApp, WhatsApp conversations with chosen Inbox labels, CRM contacts in chosen categories, or CRM contacts with given tags.
  • A live count shows who'll receive it and who is left out (opted out, or numbers with no country code), with a few example names.
  • Send at (optional) — leave empty to save it as a draft you Start yourself. Each message is charged at WhatsApp's rate for the recipient's country.

Campaigns can be started, paused, resumed and cancelled from the list. Nothing is sent until you start it.

Message log, analytics & opt-outs

  • Message log — every WhatsApp send with its status (sent, delivered, read, failed).
  • WhatsApp analytics — delivery and read rates, plus two-way figures: conversation volume and how fast your team responds.
  • Opt-outs — a customer who replies STOP is recorded here and excluded from campaigns and template sends; START reverses it.

Worked example — WhatsApp from zero. Get a number → buy a local number → write a template under WhatsApp templates and wait for approval → build an Order request form under WhatsApp forms and publish it → customers' messages arrive in the Inbox, where you reply, send the form, and approve the order it produces.
