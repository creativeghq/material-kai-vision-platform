Public & Shared Pages

The screens your customers and prospects see — usually opened from a link, without signing in.

Many of the platform's outputs are designed to be shared with people who don't have an account. These pages are reached through links you generate (often token-protected and revocable), so recipients can view, approve, pay or respond without logging in.

How sharing works

  • Generated on demand — you create the link from the relevant screen (a quote, invoice, board, project, etc.).
  • Token-protected — links carry a unique token; they can be revoked or set to expire.
  • No account needed — recipients interact directly: view, accept, pay or comment.
  • Feedback returns to you — approvals and comments flow back into the originating record (e.g. a project's Client View).

Because these pages are public by design, only share the links with intended recipients, and revoke them when a deliverable is no longer current.
