Construction & Contracting

/projects/:id

A project can run a full construction / main-contracting workflow — drawing takeoff, drawings and RFIs, a programme with a real critical path, tenders that become subcontracts, valuations and retention, cost-value reconciliation, site diaries and snags. Choose Construction Contract as the project's kind and these tools appear in its sections; a fit-out job gets the site tools without the contract ones. This page is the map — what lives where, and where each deep guide is.

Where: everything is inside a project at /projects/:id, in its six stages (Overview · Design · Specification · Commercial · Delivery · Client). Start from drawings on the Projects list creates a Construction Contract project and opens the takeoff.

Which projects get the construction tools

The project's kind decides it:

  • Renovation & Fit-Out — Estimate, Changes, Site and Equipment & Warranties, on top of the design and commercial sections.
  • Construction Contract — all of that plus four construction features: Drawing Takeoff (in Estimate), Tender Packages (in Purchases), Valuations & Retention (in Invoicing) and Cost-Value Reconciliation (the Value and Margin columns in Costs).

Any of these can be switched on or off for one project under ⋯ → Sections; a section that already holds records always stays on.

The construction toolset — where each lives

Specification

  • Takeoff from drawings — Estimate → From Drawings: read the plan PDFs and the study, confirm the quantities on the sheet, add them to a quote.
  • Estimate from dimensions — a blueprint and a few measurements give a priced plan.

Commercial

See Commercial & cost control. Invoicing and Costs need the owner or a manager plus finance access.

  • Quotes — what we charge, with a warning when a newer drawing revision changed a quoted quantity.
  • Changes — the variations register, client and subcontractor, never netted.
  • Invoicing — invoices, sales orders and applications for payment with retention.
  • Purchases — made-to-order items, purchase orders, tenders → subcontracts, supplier bills and expenses.
  • Costs — job cost with labour, cost codes with budget, forecast and CVR, and stock held for the job.

Delivery

  • Programme — a Gantt with a real critical path, float and a baseline (the Schedule view of Tasks).
  • Site — the site diary, inspection checklists and the snag/defect list (with a dictate-a-walk shortcut).
  • Documents — a controlled drawing register with revisions, and markup → RFI.
  • Equipment & Warranties — what was installed and its warranties and service plans.

Client

  • Requests — the RFI & submittal register (numbered, due-dated, de-duplicated, tied to a drawing). An invited architect or subcontractor sees only the requests they raise or are assigned, never prices (see inviting guests).

What the assistant can do

The assistant can read the commercial picture from chat — the CVR, applications (claimed / certified / retention), variations, tender status and a bid analysis that ranks bids fairly (on a comparable total, flagging unpriced lines). These are read-only: raising a variation, certifying a valuation or awarding a tender stays a human decision. It can also run the drawing takeoff (status and analysis; confirming printed rows behind Approve). The other on-site tools (drawing register, RFIs, snags, programme) are UI-only.

Gating in one line: the construction tools come with the project kind in the Projects module; Invoicing and Costs additionally need the Sales & Finance module and finance.manage; AI Assessment is its own Pro add-on. There is no separate “construction” add-on to buy.
