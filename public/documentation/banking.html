Banking — Revolut Pro add-on · BYOK

/finance → Settings → Accounts /profile?tab=keys

Banking connects your workspace to Revolut Business so the platform can see your own bank account and act on it: a live transaction feed, automatic reconciliation of incoming transfers against your invoices and outgoing ones against supplier bills, money-out (pay suppliers, send payout links) with payee verification, and team cards whose spend flows into expense reports. It is bring-your-own-key (BYOK) and per-workspace — every tenant connects its own Revolut account; there is no shared platform bank. This page explains the two Revolut modules, how you connect, and how it's gated; the sub-pages go deep on each capability.

Two different Revolut modules — don't confuse them:

  • Revolut Business (this section) — your bank account. Feed, reconciliation, money-out, cards. Home: Finance → Settings → Accounts; connected in Profile → Keys.
  • Revolut Checkout — a way for your customers to pay you at checkout (cards, Revolut Pay, Apple/Google Pay, Pay by Bank), alongside Stripe and Viva. Home: Finance → Settings → Payments.

Connecting Revolut Business — the BYOK setup

You connect once, in Profile → Keys → Finance & Tax (the same card also appears under the module's admin settings). It's a strict four-step ladder — each step unlocks the next.

StepWhat you do
1. Signing keyClick Generate keys. The platform mints a key pair server-side — the private key never leaves the server. Copy the certificate (public key) and the redirect URI shown, and add them in your Revolut dashboard → Settings → API.
2. ApplicationPaste the Client ID Revolut gives you back, choose the environment (Sandbox / Production), and optionally your @revtag (Revolut username) — which then prints on your invoices for instant in-app payment. Save.
3. AuthoriseClick Authorise with Revolut — a standard OAuth consent that returns to the platform and completes the connection server-side (the webhook registers itself automatically). This is also where you later find Sync now, Re-register webhook, Sync labels and Disconnect.
4. Map accountsLoad Revolut accounts shows each currency pocket with its live balance; map each to a Finance bank account. Mapping also auto-fills that account's IBAN/BIC from Revolut when it's blank.

Your keys stay yours. The connection secrets live in a per-workspace store that has no read path to the browser — the private key and tokens can never be fetched by the app, only used server-side. Disconnecting drops the tokens and webhook but keeps your key pair and Client ID so you can reconnect without redoing Revolut's dashboard.

What you get once connected

🔁

Bank feed & reconciliation

A live transaction feed that auto-matches incoming transfers to your invoices (and outgoing ones to supplier bills), with a review queue for the uncertain ones.
💸

Money-out & payouts

Pay a supplier bill or a CRM counterparty, batch a bill run, or send a payout link — with Verification-of-Payee name checks before money moves.
💳

Team cards & expenses

Issue virtual cards, set spend limits, freeze/unfreeze, and import card spend (with receipts) straight into each person's expense report.

Invoice payment details fill themselves

Once connected, invoices and proformas need no manual bank block. Your Revolut pockets are auto-provisioned as bank accounts; the EUR one is marked to show on invoices and its IBAN prints automatically. If you set a @revtag, a “Revolut · @tag — instant in-app payment” line is added next to the IBANs. (Separately, if you use Viva, a Greek RF bank-transfer code can also print on the document and auto-reconcile — see Invoices.)

Verification of Payee (VoP) in CRM

When Revolut is connected, each CRM bank account gets a Verify holder button. It checks the account holder's name against the IBAN with Revolut and shows a plain verdict — matches, close match (with the bank's actual name), does not match, or couldn't be checked. This is the same check that gates outgoing payments (see Money-out), so verifying a supplier's details in CRM up front means paying them later is one click.

Gating & cost

  • Pro add-on, per workspace. Banking (banking-revolut) and Revolut Checkout (payments-revolut) are separate paid add-ons; enable them from the App Launcher / Finance → Settings → Payments. Only a workspace owner can activate a paid add-on; others can request it.
  • Finance-manager gated. All banking actions require the finance.manage capability and run under your workspace's own permissions; the connection secrets are writable only by finance managers and never readable by the browser.
  • No credit metering. Banking actions aren't credit-metered — they're gated by the add-on entitlement, not charged per action.
