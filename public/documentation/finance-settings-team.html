Settings · Team

/finance finance.manage

The Team section shows everyone who has access to this workspace, lets you invite new people with a role, change a member's role or remove them, and — for a time-limited Support login — decide when they may come back in. The role decides which portal a person lands on and what they can do; there are no passwords to set and no seats to buy.

Where it is

The same Team panel appears in three places, so you can reach it from wherever you are:

  • Finance → Settings → Team — here it sits above Approvals and Authority (the bands for credit holds, margin floors and till variances).
  • CRM → Users — every workspace's own members, roles and invites.
  • Profile → Team — shown to the workspace owner and admins.

What's on the screen

  • Invite a Team Member — a Role & portal dropdown (each option reads Role — portal, e.g. Accountant — Finance portal), an optional Email, the selected role's description, and two buttons: Send invitation and Generate link instead. Only people who can manage the team see this panel.
  • Awaiting Acceptance (N) — invitations not yet claimed, each with its role and expiry date, a copy-link button and Revoke invitation. Revoking stops the link working.
  • Support Access (N) — Support logins, their remaining time and any request to come back in (see below).
  • Members (N) — each person with their photo, name and email (and their status when it is not active), a role dropdown to change it, and Remove from workspace. The owner's row is locked.

The roles you can grant

The description under the dropdown is the one summarised here. Roles tied to a module are offered only when that module is on for the workspace.

None of the business roles includes workspace administration — giving someone HR or the warehouse does not hand over finance, pricing or the team.

How to invite someone — step by step

  1. Open the Team panel (for example Finance → Settings → Team).
  2. Pick a role in Role & portal and read the description beneath it.
  3. Either type their Email and press Send invitation — the invitation is tied to that address, so a forwarded link cannot be claimed by anyone else — or press Generate link instead and send the link yourself. A link is valid for 30 days.
  4. They open it, sign up or sign in, and join this workspace with that role. Until then they are listed under Awaiting Acceptance.

Support access — a login with a timer

What it is — the Support role is for someone helping you for a limited time (an outside consultant, an implementer). Why it matters — their access ends by itself; you never have to remember to remove them.

  1. Choose Support when inviting and set Access Lasts (1 hour, 4 hours, 8 hours, 1 day, 3 days or 7 days). The time starts when they accept the invitation.
  2. When the time is up their membership is suspended and they see “Your access has ended” with a Request Approval to Enter Workspace button. Any API key they hold for the workspace is switched off too.
  3. Their request appears under Support Access for an owner or admin, who can Deny it or Approve it — choosing the new access time and how long the PIN stays valid (10 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour or 1 day).
  4. Approving shows a 6-digit PIN. Hand it to the person yourself; they type it on their screen and press Enter workspace. Too many wrong tries close the request, and they can ask again.

End access on an active Support row stops it immediately. A Support member cannot approve themselves, invite anyone or extend their own time.

Worked example — adding a part-time bookkeeper. Pick Accountant — Finance portal, type their email and press Send invitation. When they sign up they can open invoices, record payments and submit to myDATA — the CRM, Pricing and Settings areas simply don't appear for them.

After they join

The new person appears under Members with their role. Change the role at any time from the dropdown on their row. For what each role reaches across the platform, see Getting Started → Roles & access.
