Account · Profile

/profile /u/:userId Public

The Profile tab is your public profile — and the place you set the things that follow you around the platform: your photo, your name, your email signature. Everything public you fill in here — your photo, name, role, business details and the services you offer — feeds a sharable public page at /u/:userId. This guide walks every section: what it is, why it matters, exactly how to use it, and what it changes elsewhere.

Where it is

Click your profile menu at the top-right of any page — it shows your photo and your name — or go straight to /profile. Profile is the first entry in the page's side rail (see Account & Billing for the rest of the rail).

The layout

  • The hero across the top — your photo, name, visibility, profile strength and headline numbers.
  • A wide column for your records: About you, Email signature, Business and Services.
  • A side column of short panels: Show property listings, Surplus Marketplace, Manufacturer identity, Skills & Expertise, the Brands you represent pointer, Supplier Verification, Featured Moodboard, Profile Analytics and, last, Appearance. Panels you don't qualify for simply aren't there. On a narrower screen the side panels go two-up under the records, and on a phone everything is one column.

The hero

What it is — one card that says who you are and how your public page is doing. Why it matters — it's the first thing you see, so it opens on you rather than on a settings switch, and it tells you exactly what is still missing.

  • Photo — click the large avatar to upload a new profile photo from your device.
  • Name and role — your name (or Add your name), your professional type, and a contact line with your company, location and website.
  • Public / Private — the switch that publishes your page. Public: “Anyone can find and view your public page.” Private: “Only you can see it.” Beside it, View public page and Copy link.
  • Profile strength — a meter over nine items: Photo, Name, Professional type, Phone, Location, Bio, A service, Skills and Email signature. Each missing item is a button that takes you to whatever fills it (the photo picker, the About you form, a new service or skill, or the signature designer). When everything is done it says “Everything is filled in.”
  • Headline numbers (when your profile is public) — Profile visits, Followers, Hire requests (with how many are unread) and Reviews (with the average rating).

Your photo appears everywhere you do

The photo you set here is the one the platform draws wherever it shows you: the account button in the top bar, your messages in the Agent Hub chat, Team members, the Inbox member pickers, comments and reviews, and a CRM contact's Linked account. Teammates in your workspace see it too.

Email signature

What it is — the signature added under the emails you send from the Inbox in this workspace: replies, new emails, scheduled emails and follow-ups. Why it matters — one designed signature — name, role, phone and company logo — on every email, without retyping it, and the same one you can paste into your own mail app. It's yours, and it's per workspace, so every member sets their own.

  • No signature yet? The section offers Design your signature.
  • The designer (“Design your email signature”) starts filled in from your profile and from the workspace's business identity — company name, website and logo — with a live Preview. An invalid email address or a logo that isn't an https:// image is left out, and the designer tells you so.
  • Once saved, the section shows a live preview, plus Copy for Gmail / Outlook (copies it ready to paste into your mail app's signature settings), Edit, and a bin to remove it.
  • An older plain-text signature is shown as text, with a prompt to Design one with your logo, links and layout.

Connected Gmail accounts use Gmail's own signature. The Inbox's reply settings no longer edit the signature — they show who you sign as and link here. See Inbox for how it's added to your emails.

Business

Your business details — company name, address, VAT/tax details and other registration info. For Greek companies these can be enriched from the ΑΑΔΕ business registry: enter your Greek VAT number and pull official details in one step rather than typing them by hand. The same registry data is reused for your tax documents and CRM records — see Business identity and CRM companies.

How to enrich your business from ΑΑΔΕ

  1. Open the Business section and click Edit.
  2. Enter your Greek ΑΦΜ (VAT number).
  3. Pull the official company details from the ΑΑΔΕ registry in one step.
  4. Save to publish.

Services

List the services you offer with Add service. They're the same services Finance invoices — a visitor can hire a priced one from your public page and pay online, and every one of them is in the invoice and quote pickers. See Finance → Settings → Services.

The side panels

  • Show property listings — when your profile is public and Real Estate is on, adds your live listings as a Listings tab on your public page.
  • Surplus Marketplace — apply to list your excess stock to other businesses and buy theirs; once approved by the operator you can pause or resume it. See Marketplace.
  • Manufacturer identity — are you the manufacturer? Claim your company to receive purchase orders in-app and publish your own product data.
  • Skills & Expertise — add skills with Add skill so visitors see your areas of expertise.
  • Brands you represent — a pointer to the Ambassador tab, where you declare the brands you work with.
  • Supplier Verification — shown when your professional type is Supplier.
  • Featured Moodboard — pick a board to showcase on your profile.
  • Profile Analytics (public profiles) — engagement beyond the hero's four numbers: board visits, appointments, quote requests, quotes and more.
  • Appearance — light/dark theme and the accent colour (e.g. Blue or Green). It's an app-wide preference, not part of your public profile, which is why it sits last.

Appointment availability lives on the Schedule tab, and your websites on the Websites tab.

How to complete your profile — step by step

  1. Open your profile menu (top-right) → Profile, or go to /profile.
  2. Click your avatar in the hero and upload a photo.
  3. Work through the Profile strength buttons — each one opens the field that fills it.
  4. Under Business, click Edit and, for a Greek company, pull your details from ΑΑΔΕ with your ΑΦΜ.
  5. Design your email signature, then Copy for Gmail / Outlook if you also want it in your own mail app.
  6. Turn the switch to Public and use Copy link to share /u/:userId.

Worked example — a consultant getting discovered. Open Profile → click the avatar and upload a photo → the strength meter lists Professional type, Bio and A service as missing; click each and fill it (Consultant, a two-line bio, a priced service) → Design your signature and save → switch to Public, Copy link and send the /u/:userId URL to a prospective client. They open it without an account and see your name, business, role and services, and the hero's Profile visits starts counting.

What it affects

  • When public, everything here is published to /u/:userId Public — viewable by anyone with the link, no account required.
  • Your photo and name are what teammates see next to your work across the platform.
  • Your email signature goes under the emails you send from the Inbox in this workspace.
  • Business details enriched from ΑΑΔΕ reuse the same registry data as your tax documents and CRM records.
  • Your Services are the same items Finance invoices and quotes.
