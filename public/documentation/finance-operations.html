Operations & Reports

/finance finance.manage

The operational and planning side of Finance — dispatch, warehouse, parties, scheduling, billable time, reporting, and settings.

How to open it: top navigation → Finance → the Dispatch board, Warehouse, Customers & Suppliers, Planning, Time & billing, Reports, Marketplace or Settings tab.

Dispatch board

A board view for managing goods going out — tracking orders/deliveries through their stages. Works hand-in-hand with Delivery notes under Documents.

Warehouse

The stock side of the business — manage inventory that backs your products and links into delivery/dispatch. Use it to keep on-hand quantities and movements aligned with what you sell and ship.

Customers & Suppliers

A consolidated view of the parties on your documents. It complements the CRM by focusing on the financial relationship — who you invoice and who you pay — and is the place those records surface inside Finance.

Planning

Forward-looking planning for the business — organising upcoming work and expected financial activity so the numbers aren't only backward-looking.

Time & billing

Track billable time and convert it into charges/invoices — useful for service work delivered alongside materials.

Reports

Financial summaries and exports across your data — revenue, expenses, receivables/payables and more — with export options (e.g. CSV) for your accountant or your own analysis.

Marketplace

Connects Finance to the platform's surplus/last-stock marketplace, tying marketplace activity into your financial view.

Settings

Workspace finance configuration lives here — company/issuer details, VAT, fiscal/e-invoicing connection, document defaults and keys. (/settings redirects into Finance because workspace settings are managed here.)

Several of these tabs are most useful for product/stock-based businesses. If a tab is empty, it simply means there's no data of that type yet — start by creating documents or stock entries.
