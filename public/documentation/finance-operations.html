Operations & Reports

/finance finance.manage

The operational and planning side of Finance — dispatch, warehouse, parties, scheduling, billable time, reporting, and settings.

How to open it: top navigation → Finance → the Dispatch board, Warehouse, Customers & Suppliers, Planning, Time & billing, Reports, Marketplace or Settings tab.

Dispatch board

A board view for managing goods going out — tracking orders/deliveries through their stages. Works hand-in-hand with Delivery notes and Warehouse.

  • Board of outgoing goods — a single place to see everything going out.
  • Add manual note — drop a manual note onto the board for items that aren't tied to a formal document.
  • Works with Delivery notes (under Documents) and the Warehouse stock side.

Warehouse

The stock side of the business — manage inventory that backs your products and links into delivery/dispatch. Use it to keep on-hand quantities and movements aligned with what you sell and ship.

  • Warehouse selector — switch between Main (default) and Warehouse.
  • Add item — register a new stock item.

Customers & Suppliers

A consolidated view of the parties on your documents. It complements the CRM by focusing on the financial relationship — who you invoice and who you pay — and is the place those records surface inside Finance.

  • Combined view of every CRM party with finance activity — customers and suppliers in one list.
  • Click a row for the full breakdown and to email a statement.
  • Filters — All (customers/suppliers) and All segments.
  • Search by Name or email.
  • Stats — Total customers owe (€) and We owe suppliers (€).

Planning

Forward-looking planning for the business — scheduled future payments and expected receipts so the numbers aren't only backward-looking.

  • Scheduled future payments + expected receipts in one forward view.
  • Add planned payment — schedule an upcoming outflow.
  • Marking Paid creates a real payment + allocation — a planned item becomes an actual Payment record when settled.
  • Filter — Upcoming (open).
  • Stats — Expected in (€), Planned out (€), Net planned (€).

Time & billing

Track billable time and convert it into invoices — useful for service work delivered alongside materials (e.g. "On-site measurement & consultation").

  • Log time — record billable time entries.
  • Views — By user and By contact.
  • Convert unbilled time to invoices — turn logged time into a Finance invoice.
  • Search by customer.

Reports

Financial summaries and exports across your data, with export options for your accountant or your own analysis.

  • Report types — Sales journal, Purchases journal, VAT summary, Sales per day.
  • Period selector — This month, Last month, or a custom range.
  • Export CSV — download the report for your accountant or spreadsheet.

Marketplace

Connects Finance to the platform's surplus/last-stock marketplace, tying marketplace activity into your financial view.

  • Surplus / last-stock marketplace tie-in — your marketplace activity surfaces alongside your financials.

Settings

Workspace finance configuration lives here — company/issuer details, VAT, fiscal/e-invoicing connection, document defaults and keys. (/settings redirects into Finance because workspace settings are managed here.)

Several of these tabs are most useful for product/stock-based businesses. If a tab is empty, it simply means there's no data of that type yet — start by creating documents or stock entries.
