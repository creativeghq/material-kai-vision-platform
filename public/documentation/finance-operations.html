Operations & Reports

/finance finance.manage

Once invoices and expenses are flowing, this is the side of Finance that helps you run the business: seeing who owes whom, planning cash that hasn't moved yet, billing time, preparing the VAT return, and reporting it all. Each area is its own detailed page below.

How to open it: top navigation → Finance → the Tools group of the section rail: Planning, Expense Cards, Time & Billing, Reports, VAT Return, Customers & Suppliers and more. The dispatch board and stock moved to the separate Warehouse module. (Settings has its own 13-section guide.)
