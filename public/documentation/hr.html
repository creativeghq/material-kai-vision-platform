HR Pro add-on

/hr

HR turns MaterialsHub into a full people-operations suite for your company: your employees (kept as CRM contacts tagged as employees), attendance with a clock-in kiosk and Work-Card check-in, time-off with leave balances, payroll with a Greek tax engine that posts straight into Finance, AI-assisted recruitment with a public careers page, document handling with AI OCR, and direct filing to the Greek labour authority (Ergani). It's the first paid add-on built on the module framework. This page is the map; each area has its own detailed guide below.

Where: enable HR from the App Launcher (Pro add-on), then open it at /hr. HR is owner/admin only — the sensitive people data is gated to workspace owners and admins. Employees get a separate, restricted self-service portal.

How it's organised

The HR page is a set of sections — Overview (headline analytics), Employees, Attendance, Time-off, Payroll, Recruitment, Documents and Ergani. Two things sit outside the admin page: the public-facing careers page and the two staff-facing surfaces (the clock-in kiosk and the employee self-service portal).

The restricted portal where staff clock in, request time off, and get payslips.

How the pieces connect

  • People live in CRM. An employee is a CRM contact tagged as an employee, with an HR record attached — so contact details stay in one place and HR adds the employment layer on top.
  • Attendance feeds payroll. Clock-in punches and time-off build the timesheets and day counts that payroll reads.
  • Payroll posts to Finance. An approved payroll run fans out into Finance as planned salary payments, so what you owe staff shows up alongside everything else you owe.
  • Ergani is the compliance layer. Hires, schedules, leave and punches can be filed to the Greek labour authority directly from HR — see Ergani.
  • AI is credit-metered. The AI steps (CV screening, accounting-document OCR) consume workspace credits, logged per operation.

Getting started: enable HR → add your employees → (optional) connect Ergani so filings go to the authority → open the clock-in kiosk for staff → run payroll at month-end.
