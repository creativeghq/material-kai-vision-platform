Projects · The stages & who sees what

/projects/:id

A project's detail page is organised into seven stages, each holding a few sections. This guide maps every section to its stage, explains who sees which (the owner, the project team, or an invited client), and shows how to bring a client in with an invite.

Where it is

Apps menu → Projects Hub → Projects → open a project to reach its detail page (/projects/:id). The stages run across the top of the page; the selected stage lists its sections in a rail beside the content (a strip above it on a narrow screen). The set you see depends on who you are on the project and on which modules are enabled.

What's on the screen — seven stages

The detail page groups its sections into seven stages on one row (you don't have to use them in order — they just gather related sections). A stage you can only see one section of shows as that section.

StageSections under it
OverviewThe cover with description, client, deadline, budget and tasks; Cost & margin, Rooms, Visits & meetings, task progress and milestones, and the Team. See Overview, costs, visits & team.
DesignRooms, Moodboards, Sheets (presentation & technical plans).
SpecificationProducts team, Plan (the estimating engine) team, Purchases (made-to-order joinery + supplier sheet) team.
CommercialQuotes, Contracts team, Billing manager, and Finance — which holds the construction money chain (BoQ, variations/CVR, valuations/retention, tenders, labour). manager · finance.manage
DeliveryTasks (List, Board, Calendar and the Schedule), Site (diary, inspections, snags), Documents (including Drawings).
ClientClient View team, Requests (RFIs & submittals), Handover (warranties) team.
ReviewAI Assessment team, Timeline team.

Several sections have their own guides: Rooms, the Plan & Estimating engine, Technical Plans, the Client View, and the whole Construction & Contracting toolset (Commercial, Delivery and Client stages). A team section is open to the owner and the project team but never to an invited client; manager means the owner and project managers only. Coming back to a stage reopens the section you left it on.

Who sees what

WhoWhat they get
OwnerEverything, including Delete and handing the project over.
Project managerEvery section, including Billing and (with finance access) Finance; edits the project, archives it, invites clients and manages the team.
Team memberEvery section except Billing and Finance — the working surface without the money.
Invited clientA read-only slice: Overview, Rooms, Moodboards, Sheets, Quotes, Tasks marked Client visible, Site, Documents and Requests. Never Products, Plan, Purchases, Contracts, Billing, Finance, Client View, Handover, Timeline or the AI Assessment.

Teammates are added on the Overview's Team panel. Clients are invited by email, below.

Inviting the client

What it is — a passwordless email invitation that lets your client look in on the project (/projects/invite/:token). Why it matters — the client follows progress, sees what you choose to share and can request a visit, without you handing over your workspace.

  1. In the project header, press Invite client (owner and managers). The dialog is “Invite Clients to View This Project”.
  2. Enter the client's Email address, an optional Personal message, and press Send invitation.
  3. The client opens the link, signs in with a one-time code sent to that email, and accepts. The dialog lists each invite as Pending, Active, Expired or Revoked; you can copy the link, re-send it or revoke access.

Worked example. Add your site supervisor to the team as a Member so they can run tasks and the site diary, then Invite client with the homeowner's email. The supervisor works in the project like you do (minus the money); the homeowner sees progress, client-visible tasks and documents, and can ask for a showroom visit — but never your costs, your plan or your internal task comments.
