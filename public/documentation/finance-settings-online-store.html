Settings · Online Store

/finance finance.manage

The Online Store section turns your priced catalogue into a public storefront at /store/:slug — a shareable page where anyone can browse and buy what you sell, with no login. You switch the store on, set the headline text shoppers see, and publish the priced products you want listed. Checkout creates a draft order and sends the buyer to the pay page; the order shows up in Invoices.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → Settings tab → Online Store (in the row of settings sub-tabs, after Team).

What's on the screen

A short form, top to bottom, followed by the Save button:

  • The on/off switch (top right of the card) — opens or closes the store. While it is on, the store's public URL is shown with copy and open buttons (if the workspace has no public slug yet, you are told to set one first).
  • Headline — the large title at the top of your public store page (for example, "Shop our collection").
  • Subheadline — the supporting line under the headline (for example, "Curated materials, shipped to you").
  • Save store settings — saves the switch and the text to the live store page.
  • Published products — every priced product with its price and a switch; the badge counts how many are live. Use Search products… to find one. Only switched-on products are listed.

Only priced, published products appear

The store is deliberately a price-driven surface: a product only shows up if it has a sell price and you have switched it on under Published products (new prices start unpublished, including those from a supplier XML import). If a product is missing from the public store, it almost always means it has no price yet. Set prices through your catalogue markup rules — see Settings → Pricing for category markups (cost → sell price), and Store → Public Pages for how the public storefront renders.

How to set up your store — step by step

  1. Go to Finance → Settings → Online Store.
  2. Type your Headline — keep it short and inviting, e.g. "Shop our collection".
  3. Type your Subheadline underneath it, e.g. "Curated materials, shipped to you".
  4. Turn the store's switch on and click Save store settings.
  5. Under Published products, switch on each product you want listed. Only products with a price appear here.
  6. Open /store/:slug in a browser to see exactly what a shopper sees.

Worked example — launching a small storefront. Open Online Store → set Headline to "Shop our collection" and Subheadline to "Curated materials, shipped to you" → search Published products and notice three items are missing. Hop to Settings → Pricing, add a category markup so those items get a sell price, come back, and they now appear — switch them on. Turn the store on, click Save store settings and share the /store/:slug link.

Checkout and payment

A shopper's checkout creates a draft order and takes them to the pay page at /pay/:token, which opens with "Thank you — we have received your order." and offers the methods you set up in Accounts & Payments — card through a provider, and Direct bank transfer with a receipt upload when an account is marked Show on invoice. Paid in full, the draft is issued as a real document. See Payment Providers.

Product feeds need an open store

Every product link in a product feed (Skroutz, BestPrice.gr, Google) points at this storefront, so a feed refuses to build while the store is closed, and a feed limited to published prices lists nothing until products are published here. Sales Channels warns you about both.

What it affects

Saving here updates your live public storefront at /store/:slug — the headline and subheadline text, and the set of products on display. The product list is governed entirely by which catalogue items have a price, so this section works hand-in-hand with Settings → Pricing. For the public-facing rendering and the rest of the storefront pages, see Store → Public Pages.
