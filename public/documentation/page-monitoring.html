Page Monitoring Add-on

/monitoring/pages

Some of the facts your business runs on live on somebody else's website: a supplier's payment terms or price list, a regulator's notice, a partner's API changelog, a competitor's spec sheet. Nobody tells you when they change. Page Monitoring watches those pages on a schedule you choose and shows you the exact lines that moved. You hear about changes only, never "still the same".

Where: the Page Monitoring tile in the App Launcher opens /monitoring/pages. It needs the page-monitoring module on the workspace; without it, the page shows the unlock screen instead (see Modules).

Which tool do you want?

Three features read web pages on a schedule. What separates them is whose page it is:

FeatureWhose pageWhat you get
Page MonitoringOne page someone else owns.A text diff of what changed.
WebsitesDomains you own, crawled whole for SEO.Articles, keywords, audits, Search Console.
Price MonitoringRetailers selling your products.A resolved product and a price to compare.

Watching a page

Click Watch a page and fill in:

FieldWhat to enter
What is itA plain-English name, e.g. Acme Tiles — payment terms. This is what the alert will be called.
Page URLOne public https page. A page behind a login cannot be watched.
KindSupplier Terms, Regulatory, Partner API Docs, Competitor or Other.
What counts as a change worth hearing about?Optional, e.g. Payment terms, minimum order quantity or lead times changing. Ignore typos and layout. Filling it in turns on the change judge, which labels each diff against what you asked for. Every change is still recorded and shown either way.
How oftenevery hour, every 6 hours, every day at 09:00, every Monday at 09:00 or the 1st of every month at 09:00.

A baseline snapshot is taken within a minute or two; every later check is compared against the last one.

The list

Each watch shows its name and URL, Kind, how often it Runs, its Last Check and its State: Awaiting First Check, No change, Changed (with how many to review), Check failed, Page Gone, Not scheduled or Paused. Search by name or URL and filter by Kind and State (Needs Review, Not running, Running, quiet, Paused). A watch pointed at a page that has disappeared reads Page Gone rather than looking healthy.

The row menu offers View changes, Check now (1 credit), Pause / Resume and Stop watching. Resume also relinks a watch that was saved but never started upstream.

Reading a change

View changes lists the changes, kept for 90 days, each as a coloured diff: added lines in green, removed lines in red. Mark reviewed clears one from the watch's review count.

Who hears about it

A detected change raises the Watched Page Changed automation, which notifies the workspace owner. Change who it reaches, or add an email, a task or a message, in Automations.

What it costs

  • One credit per page per check, plus one more when the change judge runs on a page that actually moved.
  • every hour is 24 checks a day; every day at 09:00 is one. Pick the slowest cadence you can live with.
  • A scheduled check that cannot be paid pauses the watch. A workspace can hold up to 25 active watches.
