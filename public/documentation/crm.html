CRM

/crm /crm/contacts/:id /crm/companies/:id crm.view

Manage the people and companies you do business with, alongside platform users.

What this page is for

The CRM is your relationship hub. A summary strip shows Total Users, Active Users, Total Contacts and Total Companies, and the work is organised into tabs.

Tabs

  • Users — platform users, their roles and subscriptions, with a search box.
  • Contacts — individual people you deal with.
  • Companies — organisations, suppliers and customers.
  • Categories — labels to segment contacts and companies.

Contact & company detail

Clicking a contact or company opens its detail page (/crm/contacts/:id · /crm/companies/:id) where you manage its information, categories and associated records.

Greek business lookup (ΑΑΔΕ)

For Greek companies, the platform can auto-fill the business profile from the official ΑΑΔΕ registry using the company's ΑΦΜ (VAT number): legal name, address, tax office, activity codes (ΚΑΔ) and status. This keeps customer/supplier records accurate and is used to validate VAT before issuing invoices. Results are cached, so repeat lookups of the same company are instant.

CRM is gated by the crm.view capability. The Admin sub-view (visible to operators) adds platform-wide user management beyond the standard CRM.
