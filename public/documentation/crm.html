CRM

/crm /crm/contacts/:id /crm/companies/:id crm.view

CRM is your relationship hub — the people and companies you do business with, the deals you are working, and the lists you file them under. It's organised into tabs, and each is documented in full (every column, every form field, how to add and edit records, and how it feeds invoicing and outreach) on its own page below.

How to open it: top navigation → CRM. Switch between the Pipeline, Contacts, Companies and Categories tabs. A summary strip across the top shows Total Contacts and Total Companies. Platform operators also see a Users tab and the Total Users / Active Users tiles — for everyone else they are hidden, and a ?tab=users link opens Contacts instead.

Adding a person or a business — one question first

What it is — every Add contact / Add company button (and the App Launcher's New Contact chip) opens the same dialog, “What Are You Adding?”, before any form. Why it matters — the answer decides which side of the trade the party is on and whether they are invoiced against a VAT number or given a retail receipt — a contact nobody classified is treated as a private consumer by every fiscal path. The cards are grouped:

GroupCardWhat happens
A personCustomer · Private PersonOpens the contact form as a customer with contact type Private (B2C) — retail receipt, no VAT number.
Other ContactOpens the contact form for a lead, partner or colleague you don't trade with yet.
A businessCustomer · BusinessA business you invoice against their ΑΦΜ / VAT number.
SupplierA business you buy from.

A business card goes to a second step: enter the name and VAT number and research it (for a Greek ΑΦΜ, ΑΑΔΕ then ΓΕΜΗ), check the identity it found, and press Continue to open the company form already filled in. If the ΑΦΜ turns out to belong to a sole trader, “This is a sole trader — add as a person instead” opens the contact form with the VAT number, country and trading name carried over.

“New” fills in where you are. When a list is filtered to one value — a single category, status, lifecycle stage, company, industry or profession — or you start from a record page, the new party opens with that value already set (a filtered category is joined on first save). From a customers-only or suppliers-only place, only the matching cards are offered, with Show all types for the rest. Nothing is locked; change any pre-filled value.

Finding people — search reaches your notes too

The search boxes on the Contacts and Companies lists, and the global Spotlight search (⌘K / Ctrl+K), match a party's name and trading name and also the text of the notes written on it — so “the one who wanted the oak samples” finds the company whose note says so.

Inviting people into the picture — members, customers & guests

From a CRM contact or company, the Invite button sends a sign-up link — “Nobody has an account created for them.” The kind of access you pick matters, because a workspace member can read your CRM, costs and pipeline, while an outsider sees only what's theirs:

Invite asWhat they get
Customer accountA login onto their own orders, invoices, receipts and balance — the Client Portal. Not a member; they see nothing else.
Trade portalA company-level B2B account (reorder, approvals, spend caps) — the Trade Portal, opened from the company's Trade portal tab.
Join your teamA real workspace member with a role. The dialog warns you off this for a buyer — “pick Customer account unless they actually work for you.”
Run their own workspaceMints a workspace they own (for a partner business you deal with).
Specific projects or propertiesA guest grant to named records only — see below.
  • A guest is “the outsider”. A guest has their own account and sees only Dashboard and “Shared with me” (/shared) — the projects, properties and moodboards someone granted them, nothing else.
  • One invitation, several records. A single guest invite can tick many records; one sign-up attaches them all (and links the CRM contact), and access ends when you revoke it.
  • Every invite is actually sent — and the dialog also shows a copyable link to hand over if the email doesn't arrive.

How it connects to the rest of the platform

  • Customers and suppliers you create here are who you pick when raising quotes and invoices/bills.
  • The billing details, VAT number, credit limit and segment on a record drive validation and limits at invoice time (see Finance → Settings → General).
  • Suppliers must be marked is_supplier here to appear in Payables.
  • Categories power targeted email outreach such as "Send to Customers" on catalogs.

CRM is gated by the crm.view capability. Platform user administration (account tiers, subscriptions, credits, Add user) belongs to the platform operator: the Users tab appears only for operators, and an ordinary member never lands on it.
