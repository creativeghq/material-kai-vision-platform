Factory Analytics

/factory-analytics Factory accounts

Market-demand intelligence for manufacturers — what's being searched, saved and specified, and where it's heading.

What this page is for

Aimed at factories/manufacturers, this dashboard turns platform activity into market intelligence: which materials and categories are in demand, which buyer types are driving it, and how those signals are trending week over week.

What you'll see

  • Headline signals — Active Demand Signals, Top Demanded Material, Most Saved Category, Total Category Saves, Top Buyer Type.
  • Where the Market is Going — week-over-week momentum: emerging materials, shifting design contexts and growing buyer segments.
  • Rising & Declining Materials — save-velocity change (recent 4 weeks vs the prior 4), flagging items as “Emerging”.
  • Filters — by platform scope, category and time window (e.g. 12 weeks), with Export CSV.

Before your account has enough recorded activity, the page shows clearly-labelled sample data, which is automatically replaced with live figures as activity accrues. Deeper geographic, designer-engagement and competitive sections are available on higher tiers.
