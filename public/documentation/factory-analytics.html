Factory Analytics

/factory-analytics Factory accounts

Market-demand intelligence for manufacturers — see what the platform is searching for, saving and specifying across catalogues, and which way the market is heading week over week.

How to open it: top navigation → Factory Analytics (available to factory accounts) — or open /factory-analytics.

What this page is for

Built for factory and manufacturer accounts, Factory Analytics turns real platform activity into market intelligence. As designers, architects and other professionals discover products, save them to moodboards and add them to quotes, those actions become demand signals. This page aggregates and trends them so you can answer questions like: which materials and categories are in demand right now? who is doing the buying? what is gaining momentum, and what is fading?

The intelligence is drawn from across the platform, not just from your own catalogue — giving you a true read on the market you compete in. You can also narrow the view to your own products where higher-tier comparisons are available.

Headline demand signals

A row of summary cards sits at the top, giving you the state of demand at a glance for the selected scope, category and time window:

SignalWhat it tells you
Active Demand SignalsHow many distinct materials are currently generating measurable interest — your overall measure of market activity.
Top Demanded MaterialThe single material attracting the most attention across the platform this period.
Most Saved CategoryThe product category accruing the most moodboard saves — where buyers are concentrating.
Total Category SavesThe total volume of saves in the current view, a measure of how much the market is actively collecting.
Top Buyer TypeThe professional segment driving the most demand (for example interior designers, architects, sourcing agents) so you know who to design and price for.

Where the market is going

Beyond the current snapshot, a dedicated section reads the direction of the market by comparing recent activity with the period before it. It surfaces:

  • Emerging materials — items whose interest is accelerating, including materials the market is starting to ask for that may not yet be widely catalogued.
  • Shifting design contexts — which room types and project settings are growing or cooling, so you can see where your materials will be needed next.
  • Growing buyer segments — which professional types are increasing their activity fastest, highlighting audiences worth courting.

Rising & declining materials

This view ranks materials by save-velocity change — comparing saves over the most recent four weeks against the four weeks before. Materials trending upward are flagged as rising; those losing ground are flagged as declining. Fast-accelerating items carry an “Emerging” badge to call out genuinely new demand. Reading this alongside the headline signals helps you decide what to promote, what to keep in stock, and where to expand your range.

Filters & export

Controls above the dashboard let you focus the analysis:

  • Platform scope — view the whole-platform market, or narrow to your own factory’s products where available.
  • Category — limit the view to a single product category (tiles, wood, lighting, sanitary and so on) or see all categories together.
  • Time window — choose how far back to look (for example the last 12 weeks).
  • Export CSV — download the current material-trend table, including growth and a short forward projection, to work with the numbers in your own tools.

Sample data before you have activity

Until enough real activity has been recorded for your scope and filters, the page displays clearly-labelled sample data so you can see what the dashboard will look like. A visible badge marks this state. As genuine activity accrues, the sample figures are replaced with your live numbers automatically — no action needed.

Higher-tier intelligence

The signals above are available to all factory accounts. Deeper analysis — including geographic demand, designer-engagement breakdowns and competitive positioning against the wider market — unlocks on higher subscription tiers.
