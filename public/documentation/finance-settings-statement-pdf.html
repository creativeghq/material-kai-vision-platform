Settings · Statement PDF

/finance finance.manage

The Statement PDF section brands the account-statement document you email to a customer or supplier. You upload a cover / page backdrop image — and, optionally, a footer image — and from then on every statement sent out carries your design instead of the plain default.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → Settings tab → Statement PDF (in the row of settings sub-tabs).

What's on the screen

A panel titled Statement PDF Design with two upload slots:

  • Cover / page backdrop — the image used as the background behind the statement's pages. Click Upload to choose a file.
  • Footer image (optional) — an image placed in the footer area of the statement. Also uploaded via its own Upload button; leave it empty if you don't need one.

How to brand your statement — step by step

  1. Go to Finance → Settings → Statement PDF.
  2. Under Cover / page backdrop, click Upload and select your background image.
  3. Optionally, under Footer image, click Upload and select a footer graphic. Skip this if you don't want a footer.
  4. The uploaded images are applied to the statement design straight away — no separate save step is needed beyond the uploads.
  5. Generate an account statement from Customers & Suppliers to confirm the branding looks right before sending.

Worked example — putting your brand on statements. Open Statement PDF → upload a branded page backdrop with your logo and colours as the Cover / page backdrop → upload a thin footer strip with your contact details as the Footer image. Now go to Customers & Suppliers, open a customer, and email their statement: the PDF carries your backdrop and footer automatically.

What it affects

These images are applied to the account-statement PDF that you email from Customers & Suppliers (the Finance operations area). They change only how that statement looks — its data is unchanged. Nothing here affects invoices or receipts, which carry their own invoice design template under Settings → Documents.
