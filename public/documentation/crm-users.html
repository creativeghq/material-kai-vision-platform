CRM · Users

/crm?tab=users /crm/users/:id crm.view

The Users tab shows the people who log in to your workspace — your members, their roles and the invitations still waiting to be accepted. Every workspace has it. A platform operator also sees, below the team, every account on the platform with its account tier, subscription and credits.

Where it is

Top navigation → CRM → the Users tab (between Leads and Contacts), or the App Launcher's CRM Users chip.

Your team

The top of the tab is the same Team panel as Finance → Settings → Team: Invite a Team Member, Awaiting Acceptance, Support Access and Members, where you change a member's role or remove them. People without the right to manage the team see the list read-only. See that page for every role you can grant and for time-limited Support access.

Platform Accounts (platform operators only)

Below the team, a platform operator sees Platform Accounts — “Every account on the platform and the business it runs.” Ordinary members never see this part.

Users vs. Contacts — a user has login access to your workspace; a contact is a record of a person you deal with who may or may not have a login. Most users arrive on their own once you invite them (see Team invites); this tab is where you see and manage them once they're in.

What's on the screen

A toolbar sits above a table of users:

  • Add user — a button that opens the new-user flow (see the step-by-step below).
  • Search — a search box (“Search by email…”) with filters, and sortable column headers.
  • Select rows — tick rows (or all) for bulk actions: Set account tier, Set status, Professional Type and Add to category.
  • The users table — one row per user, each row exposing the columns described below with per-row Actions on the right.

The columns in detail

Each row is one platform user. The columns read left to right:

ColumnWhat it shows
EmailThe user's login email address — this is also what the search box matches on.
Account TierThe user's account role — this is the access tier (see the box below). Editable inline from this column.
TypeThe account/professional type of the user.
SubscriptionThe user's subscription tier (free, pro, …).
CreditsThe user's current credit balance — what the account has left to spend.
StatusThe account status (e.g. active).
ActionsReset password (key icon) and Delete user (bin). Clicking a row opens the account's full record at /crm/users/:id.

Account tier vs. workspace role

An account's tier is platform-wide — it is true in every workspace the person belongs to — and is set inline in the Account Tier column. There are four:

TierWhat it means
UserThe baseline account; what they can do comes from their workspace role.
SupplierA supplier business node and its downstream.
ArchitectSells to end clients with their own downstream.
AdminPlatform administration.

Functional jobs — runs HR, the warehouse team, a sales manager, the accountant — are workspace roles, given per workspace from the Team panel above, never account tiers.

Filters. The toolbar has Any role, Any status, Any plan and Any type dropdowns to narrow the list, plus the email search box.

How to search

Type into the Search users by email… box at the top of the tab to filter the table down to matching users. It's the fastest way to jump to one person in a long list — search on their login email.

How to add a user

  1. Open CRM from the top navigation and go to the Users tab.
  2. Click Add user in the Platform Accounts header to open the new-user flow.
  3. Provide the user's details and choose their role — the role decides which areas of the platform they'll be able to reach.
  4. Save / create the user.
  5. The new user appears in the list, where you can read their role, subscription tier, credits and status at a glance, and manage them via the per-row Actions.

Deleting an account — what is refused

Deleting removes the login and the workspace it runs, so it is guarded. The delete is refused for your own account, for the owner of the platform's root workspace, and for any account that still has an active subscription or a credit balance. Bulk deletes run one account at a time, and the message names the reason any one of them was refused. Deleting a contact that invoices or supplier bills still point to is refused the same way, in plain words.

What this affects elsewhere

This tab decides who can log in and, through each user's role, what they can see and do platform-wide:

  • Role is the access tier. The role you set here is the primary driver of what a person can reach platform-wide — CRM, Finance, Sales, supplier/architect surfaces, and so on (per the six tiers above). It's changed inline, and takes effect for that user.
  • Invites land here. A colleague you invite appears under Members with the workspace role baked into the invitation. The workspace role (Team) decides what they can do in your workspace; the account tier (Platform Accounts) is platform-wide.

Worked example — onboarding a supplier. Once a supplier has signed up, find them in Platform Accounts with the email Search, confirm their Status is active, and set their Account Tier to Supplier from the inline dropdown — that grants their supplier business node immediately, no further list-keeping needed.

For the people and organisations you do business with, see CRM (Leads, Contacts, Companies and Categories). For what each role can do, see Getting Started → Roles & access.
