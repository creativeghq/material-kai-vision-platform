myDATA Book (ΑΑΔΕ)

/finance?tab=mydata_book finance.manage

The myDATA Book is AADE's own book for your business, exactly as myAADE reports it — as a Monthly Summary (Συνοπτικό Βιβλίο, income and expenses month by month) and as the per-document Income–Expense Book (Βιβλίο Εσόδων-Εξόδων). It is AADE's answer, never mixed with the platform's figures, so you can hold the two side by side and see where they disagree. It sits in the Finance rail under Tools, next to myDATA Transmissions: the Book is what AADE holds, Transmissions is what we sent to get it there.

Before you start — the Book is read with your business's own myDATA credentials (User ID + Subscription Key) from Finance → Settings → Documents. Without them the page says “No ΑΑΔΕ credentials for this workspace” and offers Open Settings → Documents.

The toolbar

  • View — Monthly Summary or Income–Expense Book.
  • Period — This year, Last 3 Months, Last Year or Custom Range (From / To).
  • Compare with ledger — opens the comparison of AADE's book with the platform's figures (below); a red tag on the button counts the months that differ.
  • Refresh from AADE — fetches the book again, including the documents you issued as AADE holds them and the E3 classifications your accountant filed for each document. If AADE rate-limits or fails, a band says “Last refresh failed” (with the retry time when AADE gave one) and the last figures AADE actually returned stay on screen.

Monthly Summary

What it is — one row per month and direction (Income / Expenses) in AADE's own columns: Net value, VAT, Withheld, Other taxes, Digital fee, Fees, Deductions, Third-party and Docs. The subtitle states the range, how many documents AADE holds, and when the figures were last confirmed against AADE (or “Never confirmed against AADE”). Export CSV downloads the same table, including the closing line.

  • A month with no figure says why — Nothing filed, Not fetched yet, Could not reach AADE or ΑΑΔΕ codes not set — instead of showing zero. If some months carry no figures, a note says the totals cover only part of the period.
  • An AADE document type the platform doesn't recognise is named in a warning and counted at face value.

The closing line — a total you can close a year on

Why it matters — AADE's expense book is purchases only. Payroll, depreciation and the year-end regularisations are filed as «Εγγραφές Οντότητας» (17.x) and AADE keeps them out of that total. So below Total expenses — ΑΑΔΕ book the table lists your own entries by kind — Payroll, Depreciation, Regularisation entries — and a final Expenses including your own entries line that adds them. It is the sum of the rows printed above it, so you can check it by hand.

If your own entries can't be read, the page says so: “the expense total below is ΑΑΔΕ's alone — payroll and depreciation are not in it”, rather than presenting AADE's figure as complete.

Income–Expense Book

What it is — every document AADE holds for the period, one row each, laid out the way the accountant's book is: date, document, counterparty, total, net, one column per category, VAT and withheld, with a subtotal per month and a Brought forward line. The category columns are the E3 categories the accountant filed in myDATA — goods, materials, services, expenses with and without VAT deduction, staff pay, fixed assets — plus intra-EU and non-EU acquisitions. A document nobody has classified yet lands in Not classified in myDATA, so every row still adds up.

  • Each month carries a check against AADE's own book — Matches AADE, or by how much it differs.
  • Print / PDF opens an A4-landscape page you can print or save as PDF; Excel downloads an .xlsx with one sheet per side.
  • A document AADE holds twice (two MARKs for one document) is booked once, as AADE itself counts it.

AADE's book against our ledger

What it is — a dialog, opened with Compare with ledger, comparing, month by month, AADE's net with what the platform holds under a MARK: income is your issued invoices less credit notes; expenses are the received myDATA documents. Columns: Month, Book (income / expenses), AADE net, Our Net, Difference, Verdict.

VerdictMeaning
MatchesAADE and the platform agree.
DiffersThey don't — the Difference column shows by how much. Investigate these.
Issued elsewhereAADE holds documents the platform never issued (e.g. from your previous software or another channel).
Not read yet / Not connected / Read failedThere is no AADE figure to compare, and the reason. Never read as a match.

If the comparison itself can't be loaded, the dialog says it is unknown, not matching.

Worked example — open Finance → myDATA Book (ΑΑΔΕ) → pick This year → Refresh from AADE. Click Compare with ledger: one month reads Differs on expenses. Open Expenses for that month and look for the received document that never made it into the books. When closing the year, take the Expenses including your own entries line, not AADE's purchases-only total.

Foreign-currency documents

A received document in a currency other than EUR is converted at the ECB reference rate for its date (the most recent working-day rate on or before it), fetched every working day — never at a guessed 1:1 — and that rate travels with it into the supplier bill.

What it affects

Nothing here writes to your books — it is a mirror and a comparison. Fix differences at their source: issue or correct the document in Documents, or handle the received document in Received supplier documents. For the VAT view of your own figures, see Reports → VAT.
