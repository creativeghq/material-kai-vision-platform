Stripe Pro add-on

/finance → Settings → Payments finance.manage · owner to activate

Stripe is the card-payment provider with the widest reach and the least setup. Unlike the other providers, you don't paste API keys — you connect through Stripe Connect, and payments for your invoices are routed to your connected Stripe account. Stripe also quietly powers the platform's own billing (your subscription, credit top-ups and add-ons), so it's worth understanding the two separate roles it plays.

Where: enable and configure it at Finance → Settings → Payments (a workspace owner activates the add-on). The Configure dialog handles the webhook and quick links; your Connect onboarding is a click-through with Stripe.

Two roles — don't confuse them

Stripe appears in two places on the platform, and they're deliberately separate:

  • Your invoice payments — when a customer pays one of your invoices by card, the money is routed to your connected Stripe account (via Stripe Connect). This is the payment-provider role documented here.
  • The platform's billing — your own MaterialsHub subscription, credit purchases and paid add-ons are also processed through Stripe, but on the operator's billing account. That's why some “pay” actions open a Stripe page even before you've connected anything — it's the platform charging you, not your customers paying you.

How you connect — Stripe Connect, not keys

What it is — rather than copying secret keys into the platform, you complete Stripe Connect onboarding: the platform creates a connected (Express) account for your workspace and hands you off to Stripe to verify your business and bank details. Why it matters — it means the platform never holds your Stripe keys, and Stripe pays you directly. Once onboarding reports your account as charges-enabled, invoice payments route to you automatically.

  1. A workspace owner enables the Stripe add-on in Settings → Payments.
  2. Complete Connect onboarding with Stripe (business details + payout bank). Until that's done, card payments still work but settle to the platform account rather than routing to you — so finish onboarding before you rely on it.
  3. In the Configure dialog, add the webhook (below) so completed payments reconcile.

The webhook

Paste the shown URL into Stripe Dashboard → Developers → Webhooks and subscribe to payment_intent.succeeded, checkout.session.completed, customer.subscription.* and invoice.payment_failed. The STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET is what verifies each event's signature — a payment is only booked after Stripe's signed confirmation, never on the customer's redirect alone.

What it powers

  • Invoice / pay-page card payments — the customer pays at /pay/:token via a Stripe Checkout session; on success the invoice is settled and routed to your connected account.
  • Platform subscriptions — your MaterialsHub plan (Pro / Enterprise) is billed through Stripe.
  • Credit-pack purchases — buying credits (personal or the workspace pool) goes through Stripe; €1 = 100 credits.
  • Add-on subscriptions — the paid modules (including the other payment providers) are Stripe subscriptions; enabling one may send you to a Stripe checkout.
  • Customer portal — manage your platform subscription and payment method through Stripe's hosted customer portal.

Methods & reach

Stripe offers card payments with no currency restriction (≈135 currencies), which is what makes it the broadest-reach option. It's offered first on the pay page when enabled.

Reconciliation, refunds & disputes

A completed payment arrives as a signed webhook, which settles the matching invoice through the shared payment ledger (so it books exactly like any other payment and never over-collects). A statement “pay balance” link settles the same way. Refunds and disputes are flagged to owners/admins — never auto-reversed: you resolve them deliberately with a credit note, so a chargeback can't silently unwind your books.

Recap: owner enables Stripe → complete Connect onboarding so payments route to you → add the webhook in the Configure dialog → email an invoice and confirm the card option appears at /pay/:token. See Payment Providers to compare with Viva and Revolut.
