Inbox

/inbox inbox.use

The Inbox is one place for every conversation your team relies on — team chats, customer threads, WhatsApp, email, social DMs and comments and lead-form submissions — organised, searchable, and assigned to the right person. Every member gets their own email address that files straight into the Inbox, each conversation can have an owner, and you can follow up automatically when nobody answers. An order that arrives as a message becomes a real order without re-typing, a supplier's PDF becomes a bill in two clicks, and an AI assistant can draft or send replies and hand the thread to your team when it should. This guide walks every part of it: what each control is, why it matters, how to use it, and what it changes elsewhere in the platform.

How to open it: top navigation → Inbox.

Recommended reading order: the sidebar & list → replying (the reply box, signature, Insert menu) → assigning & labels → Follow up → WhatsApp → the AI assistant. The first parts work the moment you have access; WhatsApp needs a connected number (see Profile → Social Accounts).

What this page is for

What it is — a single view that brings together the conversations your team relies on so they don't end up scattered across separate apps. Why it matters — when conversations live in one searchable, assigned place, nothing falls through the cracks and the right person always picks up the right thread. By default it shows the conversations of the workspace you're working in.

Layout — sidebar, list, conversation

What it is — three columns: the sidebar (what to show), the conversation list, and the open conversation. The customer's details open in a drawer from the Profile button in the conversation header. On a phone it's one column at a time, and the views become chips above the list.

The sidebar

  • Source tabs — All / Platform / WhatsApp — at the top. Platform is your email, team chats, social messages and customer threads; WhatsApp is only WhatsApp. Under Platform your own Inbox email address is shown with a copy button.
  • Compose — starts a new conversation (see below).
  • Views — Inbox (with a count), Follow up, Unread, Starred, Drafts, Sent, Scheduled (messages waiting to go out), Search messages and Archived. A count reading “120+” means more pages exist.
  • Assignment — Mine / Unassigned — conversations you own, and ones nobody owns yet.
  • Sources — the door each conversation came through: Public profile, Lead form, Email, WhatsApp, Social DM, Social comments, Customer, Dealer, Team, each with a count. Empty sources hide themselves.
  • Numbers — on the WhatsApp tab, when your workspace has more than one WhatsApp number: Every number or one number at a time. A Message templates link takes you to your approved WhatsApp templates.
  • Labels — your workspace's labels, each with the number of open conversations carrying it (see Labels).

The list

  • Status tabs — Open / Follow up / Done — work what's open, park what you're waiting on, close what's finished. Done is not one-way: a new message from the customer reopens it.
  • Search conversations — searches every conversation on the server (subject, preview, sender, phone and message text — never private notes), not just the page you've loaded.
  • Filter conversations — narrow by Source, WhatsApp number, Order (Waiting for approval / Approved / Dismissed), Type and labels (several at once).
  • Each row shows the source, the owner (or Unassigned), a subject line on email, a Files chip when it holds attachments, an AI tag when the assistant is on, and when a follow-up is due. The list loads more as you scroll (or Load older conversations).

What it affects: views, tabs, filters and search only change what you see — they don't move or hide anything for teammates.

All workspaces (top-right) is a platform-operator control that shows conversations across every workspace. Regular members always see the active workspace only — switch workspace to see another one's conversations.

Acting on several conversations at once

What it is — tick the checkbox on rows (or the header box to select everything shown) and a bar appears: Mark read, Done (or Reopen on the Done tab), Label, Assign to me, Follow up and Delete. In Archived the bar offers Restore and, for owners and admins, Delete forever. Why it matters — tidying fifty threads after a campaign is one action, not fifty. If some fail, the bar says how many (e.g. “10 of 12 — 2 failed”) and keeps the failed ones selected.

Delete vs Delete forever. Delete moves a conversation to Archived for 30 days, then it's removed. Delete forever (owners/admins, from Archived only, behind a confirmation) removes every message, note and attachment now. It only leaves this Inbox — the other person still has their copy.

Starting a conversation — Compose

Compose opens New Conversation with four tabs:

  • Team — a Topic and the teammates in it.
  • Customer — pick a CRM contact, an optional subject and first message. You can copy a private share link for them afterwards.
  • Email — sent from your own Inbox address: To (with contact suggestions), Cc / Bcc, Subject, the message (with formatting) and several attachments.
  • WhatsApp — a Phone number and optional name (plus a From picker when you have more than one number). This opens the conversation without sending anything; the first message must be an approved template.

Reading a conversation

  • It opens on the newest messages, at the bottom, 20 at a time. Scroll up (or Load earlier messages) for older ones.
  • Email reads like mail. The subject sits on top, earlier emails fold to one line you open on click, and the latest shows the sender, To and Cc as people chips, the body as the sender formatted it, and Attachments as cards. Remote images are held back until you press Show images or Always for that sender. WhatsApp, social and team chats stay as chat bubbles.
  • Timeline events — assignment, Done / reopen, labels and the assistant switching on or off appear as small event cards in the thread. When the assistant hands a conversation to the team, the card says so and why, and an unowned thread offers Assign to me.
  • The message menu — Reply, React, Copy text and, under More: Forward (into another conversation you can write to), Pin to top, Star, Add text to note and Remove from this inbox (removes our copy; WhatsApp has no unsend).
  • Links are links — a pasted URL is clickable and shows a preview of the page.

Replying — the reply box

What it is — one card at the bottom of the conversation. Why it matters — everything you need to answer well (the right recipients, a signature, a ready-made answer, a later send time, a follow-up) is in the same place, and the box says in words whether you're writing to the customer or to your team.

  • Reply / Reply all / Private note — the menu at the top-left. Reply all appears on email when others were copied in. A Private note turns the box amber and is seen only by your team.
  • Cc / Bcc and Track opens — on email replies (see Open tracking).
  • The toolbar — Suggest reply (the assistant drafts one for you to edit; 1 credit; you can tell it what the reply should do), Improve (Rewrite clearly / Make it shorter / Make it formal, keeping every fact, price and date), Insert, formatting, emoji, attach, and the product/service cart.
  • Formatting — email takes bold, italic, links, lists and quotes, with a preview; it's sent as HTML only when you use formatting.
  • Files — up to 10 files (20 MB) per message by the paperclip, drag-and-drop or paste. Social replies are text only.
  • Send is a split button: Send, Send and mark done (Ctrl/⌘+Shift+Enter), and Send later — Tomorrow morning, Tomorrow afternoon, Monday morning or Pick a date and time…. Scheduled messages are listed under Scheduled in the sidebar, where a waiting one can be cancelled.
  • Keyboard — Ctrl/⌘+Enter sends everywhere. On chat channels Enter sends; on email Enter is a new line.
  • Drafts — an unsent reply is saved as you type and comes back when you return (on any device); it's listed under Drafts. The discard button (with the bin icon) throws it away.
  • Writing score — on email, a score beside Send says how likely the message is to get a reply (length, questions, sentence length, a short subject) and what to change.
  • Follow up — the alarm-clock button beside Send sets a follow-up that starts once this message has gone (see Follow up).

Reply settings — signature & AI autocomplete

The settings icon in the reply box opens Reply settings:

  • Email signature — shows who you're signing as and links to Profile → Profile → Email signature to design or edit it. Under an email reply the signature is previewed with an Include my signature tick, so you can leave it off one message.
  • AI autocomplete — off by default. When on, it suggests how to finish your sentence when you pause; press Tab to accept. Each suggestion uses a small amount of credits.

Designing your signature. In Profile → Profile, the Email signature card → Design your signature: Name, Job title, Company, Phone, Email, Website, Location, Tagline, a Logo image URL (https) and an optional confidentiality note, with a live preview. It's added under every email you send from the Inbox — replies, new emails, scheduled emails and follow-ups. Copy for Gmail / Outlook copies the same design for your mail app. Inbox emails go out as a plain personal email with your signature, not wrapped in a branded newsletter layout.

The Insert menu

  • My booking link — your public profile's booking page. If online booking is off it tells you to turn it on in Profile → Availability.
  • Propose meeting times — three free slots in the next two weeks, inside the hours you set in Availability and clear of your bookings and CRM meetings.
  • A project task — pick an open task; its title, due date and link go into the message.
  • Snippets — reusable replies with {first_name}, {name} and {email} filled in for the recipient. Save this message as a snippet creates one; tick Share with my team to make it shared. Most-used come first.

Open tracking (opt-in)

What it is — a Track opens toggle on every email composer. A tracked email shows “Opened 3× · last …” or “No open recorded yet” on your copy — visible to your team only, never to the customer. Your choice is remembered in your browser. Why it matters — you know whether a quote was looked at before you chase it.

The count is approximate: some mail apps load images automatically (counting an open nobody made), others block them (hiding a real one).

Owners — assigning a conversation

What it is — each conversation can have one owner. The Assign button in the conversation header lists your team; pick someone, yourself, or Unassign. Why it matters — “who's answering this?” has one answer. The owner shows on the list row, Mine / Unassigned filter on it, and new mail to a person's own address is assigned to them automatically. Assigning someone gives them access to the conversation and is recorded in the timeline.

Labels

What it is — colour-coded tags for conversations, created per workspace by owners and admins. Tag a conversation from the Labels button in its header (or several at once from the bulk bar). Why it matters — labels filter the list, show live counts in the sidebar, can drive a Flow (a label added fires an automation), can target a WhatsApp campaign, and the assistant can read and set them.

  • Manage them in the sidebar. Hover a label for Rename, Colour and Delete (the label comes off every conversation; the conversations and messages stay). The Manage labels icon by the heading creates new ones.
  • Auto-apply. The ⚡ on a label opens Auto-apply: Keywords (comma-separated, matched anywhere in the customer's message ignoring case and accents — free) and/or Describe when it applies (AI) (a sentence the AI checks new messages against; uses credits), limited to chosen Channels or all. It only labels new incoming messages — nothing already in the Inbox is relabelled — and it only ever adds a label.

Search messages & export

What it is — Search messages in the sidebar turns the list into a message search: every matching message across the workspace, newest first, by words, channel (WhatsApp / Email / Social / Team), Received or Sent, date range (From / To), labels and With files. Click a row to jump to its conversation. Export CSV downloads the results (very large exports are capped — narrow the filters for the rest). Why it matters — “which customer asked about the oak decking last spring?” is one search, not an afternoon of scrolling.

Follow up — Boomerang and Automatic outreach

What it is — the alarm-clock button in the conversation header (and beside Send, and in the bulk bar) offers two kinds of follow-up. Why it matters — a quote you sent and never heard back about comes back to you, or gets chased for you, without a reminder in someone's head.

🪃

Boomerang

Takes the conversation out of the list and brings it back to the top, unread on the day you pick — Tomorrow, In 3 days, In a week, In 2 weeks or a date and time — with an optional note to yourself. Bring back now cancels it.

📨

Automatic outreach

Up to 5 follow-up messages sent as replies on the days you set (If no reply in…, then Sends N days after the one before). The moment they write back, the rest is cancelled — an out-of-office reply does not count. Each step can be written for you from the conversation.

  • Timing — tick Only on working days, 09:00–18:00, and Send at their best time (when this person usually writes to you) when there's enough history.
  • While sending — the Follow up button beside Send: Back on top if no reply in… (a Boomerang) or Send follow-ups if no reply…; it starts after the message goes out. Not available with Send later.
  • In bulk — select conversations → Follow up → a Boomerang day, or Send a follow-up to each…; each conversation stops on its own when that person answers.
  • The Follow up view (sidebar) lists everything waiting, split into Boomerang and Automatic outreach, ordered by when each fires, under a results line: sent · got a reply · answered before the nudge over the last 90 days. The same follow-ups show on the CRM contact and deal.
  • A follow-up that fails is reported in the conversation and brings it back on top — never silently dropped.

On WhatsApp a follow-up usually lands outside the 24-hour window, where free text is refused. Pick an approved template under “If WhatsApp's 24-hour window has closed, send this approved template instead” and it goes out as that template (billed like any template); otherwise the Inbox warns you when you schedule it.

WhatsApp conversations

What it is — customer messages on your connected WhatsApp numbers, read and answered here. Why it matters — customers expect to reach a business on WhatsApp, and handling those threads beside everything else means fast replies and nothing missed.

  • Folders or List. The WhatsApp tab opens in Folders — one folder per label plus No label (a conversation with two labels is in both). The folder with the newest message is on top, and one holding unread conversations turns green with the count and a preview. Switch to List for the plain list; your choice is remembered.
  • Several numbers. When the workspace has more than one number, the sidebar's Numbers section and the WhatsApp number filter separate them, and New conversation asks which number to send From. One customer writing to two of your numbers is two conversations.
  • Assigned on reply. When a customer messages, the conversation is created (or updated) and lands with the right owner.
  • Calls — a WhatsApp call that ended, was missed or failed, and a call-permission request, appear as events in that customer's conversation.
  • Send a WhatsApp form — the form button in the reply box sends one of your published WhatsApp forms. The customer fills in fields inside WhatsApp, and the answers arrive as a message in the conversation — an order form is read by order intake exactly like a typed order.

Templates and the 24-hour window

WhatsApp draws a line between answering a customer who contacted you and reaching out first:

  • Inside 24 hours of the customer's last message you can reply free-form.
  • Outside it (or before they've ever written), only a template approved in advance by Meta can be sent. The reply box says the window is closed and offers Send a template: pick an approved template, fill its variables, preview, send. Private notes are always allowed, and you can still message them from the WhatsApp Business app on your phone — it syncs back into the thread.

The 24-hour window is WhatsApp's rule, not ours. The Inbox follows it for you — and so does the AI assistant, which won't try to answer a conversation whose window has closed.

Email — your own address, in the Inbox

What it is — every member gets a personal email address that delivers straight into the Inbox. Why it matters — customer email is threaded, searchable and assignable beside your WhatsApp and team chats.

  • You get it by opening the Inbox — in the form firstname.lastname@mail.materialshub.gr, from your profile name (Greek names are transliterated). It's shown under the Platform tab and in the assistant settings popover (Your email address) with a copy button.
  • If your name's taken, you pick your own — the platform never appends a number.
  • Already have an address customers use? Forward that mailbox here.
  • Replying — goes to the customer's Reply-To if they set one; Reply all copies everyone else on their email. Cc/Bcc and attachments are sent as you'd expect, and a send that fails says so rather than silently dropping.

One-time operator setup. Receiving mail on mail.materialshub.gr relies on a mail worker your operator sets up once. After that every member's address just works.

The customer drawer — Profile, Mood and JARVIS

What it is — the Profile button (or the contact's name) in the conversation header opens Customer profile. Why it matters — you answer with the customer's history in view, and act on it without leaving the conversation.

  • Profile tab — the contact and company (with copy buttons on email and phone), status and owner, Customer value, Open invoices, Orders (with “owes …” when something's outstanding), Quotes, Projects, Deals, Coming up (meetings and appointments), Recent conversations with the same person, and participants.
  • Quick actions — New order (prefilled with this customer), Meeting (puts it on the calendar with a reminder, and can email the customer a calendar invite) and Task. Update a project changes a project's status in place, shows its open tasks, and adds a task linked back to this conversation.
  • Not in your CRM — an email sender you don't know can be added in one step (an existing contact with that address is reused first).
  • WhatsApp / Social tab — the service-window state, the WhatsApp profile, and adding them to the CRM.
  • Mood tab — Analyse conversation reads the conversation's sentiment and suggests a tone.
  • JARVIS tab — Summarise, Action items (owner and deadline per line), and a free question about the conversation. Billed to the person asking.

The Ask JARVIS button in the header opens the Agent Hub about this conversation instead.

Files in a conversation — orders, bills and payments

What it is — hover an attachment for its actions menu: Create an order (the New Order dialog, prefilled with the customer), Book as a supplier bill and Pay this invoice (the expense form opens with the file read and attached as the receipt; an unpaid bill then offers Pay via Revolut or Pay later), File in supplier documents, and Download. Members only. Why it matters — a supplier's emailed invoice becomes a booked bill — and a payment ready for approval — without downloading and re-uploading it.

Reading what customers send in — voice notes, documents & spreadsheets

On inbound WhatsApp and email the Inbox understands attachments on the way in (files land in a private bucket):

  • A voice note is transcribed. A Transcript block sits under the audio player; Transcribe runs it on demand if it hasn't.
  • A document is read and tagged. A PDF or photo gets a badge naming what it is (e.g. supplier invoice, “· unsure” when low-confidence), the facts printed on it, and a masked “bank details printed · IBAN ending NNNN” chip. A supplier invoice or receipt offers Add as expense; Read it covers anything not yet processed.
  • A spreadsheet can be asked a question through the assistant (metered; the person who asks pays).

Orders that arrive as a message → real orders

What it is — when a customer emails or WhatsApps what is effectively an order (or fills in a WhatsApp order form), the platform reads it into a proposed order on the thread, which a member turns into a real order. Why it matters — it takes the retyping out of turning an enquiry into a sale, with a human in charge.

  • A proposal, not an order yet. Nothing is written to your orders and no number is used until someone approves it. Threads with a proposal show an Order badge and match the Order filter.
  • Matched and priced from your catalogue (by text, and by a photo the customer sent), with your pricing rules. An item that can't be matched or priced is flagged for review, never guessed.
  • Review it. The Order panel shows the lines, a customer picker and needs-review markers; Edit lines changes quantities, repoints a line to another product, sets a price, removes or adds lines. Approve as pre-order creates a draft sales order (and can confirm to the customer on whichever channel is open); Dismiss drops it.

Who can approve & turning it on. Order intake is a workspace opt-in (it spends credits reading each message). Approve is limited to owner / admin / sales / sales-manager.

Find the business behind a conversation

When a thread isn't tied to a company, Find the business identifies who's behind it: a known number first (free), otherwise research that returns a verdict — a business / looks like an individual / not enough to tell / could not check — with a pre-filled draft. Nothing is filed until you say so; you create the business or link an existing match, and the number is remembered for next time.

Suggesting products & services — the /product and /service cards

  • Type / at the start of a line for /product or /service, or use the cart button. Queue up to 10 cards; you can send cards with no text.
  • Priced for this customer on send — the same engine as a quote line: net for a VAT-registered business, gross for a consumer, or “Price on request”.
  • Each channel gets its own shape — an interactive card per item on WhatsApp, an HTML table by email, text lines on social. The link goes to your online store when the item is published there, otherwise your public profile. Cards can't be scheduled with Send later.

The AI assistant in the Inbox

What it is — an AI assistant that can draft or send replies, grounded in that customer's own data (their statement, invoices, quotes and orders). Why it matters — it covers first response when nobody's at the desk, and steps aside the moment a human steps in.

Per conversation — the assistant button

The robot button in the conversation header cycles through three settings:

SettingWhat happens
OffThe assistant does nothing in this conversation.
Drafts repliesIt writes a reply for you to edit and send — you'll find it waiting in the reply box. Nothing goes out without you. A draft that answered an earlier message (because they wrote again) is set aside, not loaded.
Answers directlyReplies are sent to the customer without review.
  • A staff reply pauses it. When a team member sends a reply on a conversation the assistant was answering, it stops so it won't talk over them. Private notes don't pause it. Click the button to bring it back.
  • It hands off for real. When a question needs a person, the assistant pauses the conversation, records why, and notifies the team — shown as an event card with Assign to me.
  • It never sends a failure. If it can't answer (credits, a provider problem, a closed WhatsApp window), the customer gets nothing and your team gets a note in the thread.
  • It knows who it's talking to — supplier or customer, and who started the conversation — so it won't offer a supplier their “account balance”.
  • Public comments are never auto-answered, and account details are never shared under a public post.
  • Charged per turn, only when it actually replies.

Workspace settings owner / admin

The AI assistant settings button in the conversation header (any member can read; owners/admins change):

SettingWhat it does
Auto-respond to new chatsThe assistant answers first on every new customer conversation — WhatsApp, social DMs, and email to addresses that have Assistant answers email on. Off unless you turn it on.
Answer account & billingLets it share the customer's own statement, open invoices and pay links. On WhatsApp the customer is identified by phone number — decide accordingly.

“Assistant answers email” is per person. In the same popover, under Your email address, it answers new mail sent to your address without waiting for you, when the workspace's auto-respond is on. Off by default. Any single conversation can also be handed to it with the assistant button.

Gmail platform operator only

A Gmail source tab lets a connected Gmail account be worked live inside the Inbox — folders and labels, search, read, reply, compose, Boomerang, Automatic outreach, reminders, shared mailboxes with assignees, scheduled send, JARVIS summaries and drafts. It is currently shown only to the platform operator while Google's verification for Gmail access completes; it will open to workspaces after that.

A typical workflow

  1. Open /inbox, check Mine and Follow up, and use sources, labels or search to find what you need.
  2. Open a thread — the newest messages are at the bottom; open Profile for the customer's history.
  3. Reply (with a snippet, a product card or your booking link), Send and mark done, or send later.
  4. Waiting on them? Set a Boomerang or Automatic outreach.
  5. Book an emailed supplier invoice from its file menu; approve an order proposal from the Order panel.

Access & availability

The Inbox requires the inbox.use capability and the messaging module. WhatsApp conversations appear once a WhatsApp number is connected or bought (Profile → Social Accounts). If you don't see the Inbox, ask your workspace administrator.

Looking for the assistant instead of person-to-person chat? The AI workspace lives in the Agent Hub — which can also search, read and reply to your Inbox mail and book an emailed invoice as a bill, asking you to approve before it sends or books.
