Inbox

/inbox inbox.use

Team conversations, WhatsApp and customer chats — all in one place.

What this page is for

The Inbox unifies the conversations your team has — internal discussions, messages with customers, and WhatsApp threads — so nothing is scattered across separate tools. It's multi-tenant aware, so you can view a single workspace or, where permitted, all workspaces.

Layout & controls

  • Conversation list with a count and a Search conversations box.
  • Filters — All, Internal, WhatsApp.
  • All workspaces — switch between a single workspace and a combined view.
  • New conversation — start a thread.

WhatsApp

Connected WhatsApp numbers appear here: customer replies land as conversations, are assigned to the right owner, and can be answered from the Inbox. Outbound/marketing sends use approved message templates to respect WhatsApp's rules.

Customer / public threads

Some conversations can be shared with an outside party via a token link (/i/:token Public) so a customer can take part without an account. See Public & Shared Pages.

The Inbox is gated by the inbox.use capability and the messaging module being enabled for your workspace.
