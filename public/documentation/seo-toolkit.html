The SEO Toolkit — Edith Pro add-on

/agent-hub?capability=seo-research /agent-hub?capability=seo-article

The SEO Toolkit is an AI studio, not a form-driven screen. You work through Edith, the SEO assistant, in plain language — “research porcelain floor tiles”, “audit this URL”, “write me an article on underfloor heating”. Behind Edith sits a large set of search-marketing tools (SERP and keyword research, competitor and backlink analysis, site audits, traffic estimates) plus the article writer, which works from your own pages and links back to them (see SEO Article Writer). Every tool is website-aware: it defaults to your connected site and files its results under it.

Where: the SEO & Content tile in the App Launcher (Marketing hub) opens the studio at /agent-hub?capability=seo-research. The launcher also offers a direct “Write an article” entry (/agent-hub?capability=seo-article). It's a Pro add-on — the workspace needs the seo-toolkit module enabled.

Website-aware by default

The single most important thing about the toolkit is that it works against your connected website. When you've set a default connected site, every research run, audit and article automatically uses it as context and files the result under it (so it appears on that site's dashboard). The first-party tools that read Google Search Console require a connected, GSC-linked site and will point you to Profile → Websites if none exists.

What Edith can do — the tool families

You don't call these by name; you ask for the outcome and Edith picks the tool. Broadly, the toolkit covers:

FamilyWhat you can ask for
Your own site firstWhat should I work on? (one ranked list of quick wins, refreshes, consolidations and pages to add, each with its reason), My rankings (what your site ranks for, from the rank tracker and Search Console) and Site report (health, crawl, Search Console and AI visibility in one read). All of these come from your own website's data.

Page & site auditsAudit a URL (technical and on-page issues, with a Lighthouse pass), start a full-site crawl and check its status, and a plain-language site review.
Competitor & domain analysisDomain snapshots, ranked keywords, domain competitors, keyword gaps between you and a rival, and traffic estimates.
BacklinksBacklink summaries, anchor-text profiles and referring domains.
AI search visibilityThe AI Search Visibility toolkit: Where do AI answers put us?, Are we in the AI Overview?, Who do LLMs cite instead?, plus AI search volume (how often a term is asked of an assistant rather than typed). The full picture lives on the website's AI Citations section.
ExtrasGoogle Trends, local-pack checks, brand-search audits, YouTube and Reddit search, content sentiment and a site's detected technologies.

Each run renders as an inline card on the canvas and (where relevant) is saved to the site's Toolkit Runs / Keyword Research / Domain Audits tabs so you can come back to it.

These tools debit credits. The research and audit tools call paid data providers, so each run draws on your workspace credit pool. Reading the saved results later is free.

Writing an SEO article

Writing an article now has its own guide: SEO Article Writer. In short, there is one way to make an article: the Generate full article quick-start in the SEO Article Pipeline toolkit (/agent-hub?capability=seo-article), also reached from a website's Articles → New article. You give it a target keyword, an optional market, the website, notes for the writer, points it must cover and pages it must link. It researches the topic, reads your own pages on it, plans, writes, runs an editor review that checks every claim, and finishes with an analysis report. You watch it on the canvas as Research → Planning → Writing → Analyzing → Done. The finished article opens in the article viewer, where you refine it, keep it current and publish it to your WordPress site.

The toolkit's other quick-start, Just research, stops after the keyword-research stage.

Gating & cost recap

  • Pro add-on. The toolkit needs the seo-toolkit module enabled on the workspace (plus everyday agent.use). Without it, the launcher shows an upsell card.
  • Metered. Research and audit tools debit credits per run. An article bills what each stage actually uses (typically about 90 credits), and the total is shown on the article. Reading saved dashboards and re-opening articles is free.
  • Better with a connected site. Connecting a website (free to do; crawling spends credits) unlocks internal-link suggestions and first-party Search Console tools — see Connected Websites.
