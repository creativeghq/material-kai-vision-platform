The SEO Toolkit — Edith Pro add-on

/agent-hub?capability=seo-research /agent-hub?capability=seo-article

The SEO Toolkit is an AI studio, not a form-driven screen. You work through Edith, the SEO assistant, in plain language — “research porcelain floor tiles”, “audit this URL”, “write me an article on underfloor heating”. Behind Edith sits a large set of search-marketing tools (SERP and keyword research, competitor and backlink analysis, site audits, traffic estimates) plus a full article-writing pipeline that drafts, scores and inserts internal links to your own pages. Every tool is website-aware: it defaults to your connected site and files its results under it.

Where: the SEO & Content tile in the App Launcher (Marketing hub) opens the studio at /agent-hub?capability=seo-research. The launcher also offers a direct “Write an article” entry (/agent-hub?capability=seo-article). It's a Pro add-on — the workspace needs the seo-toolkit module enabled.

Website-aware by default

The single most important thing about the toolkit is that it works against your connected website. When you've set a default connected site, every research run, audit and article automatically uses it as context and files the result under it (so it appears on that site's dashboard). The first-party tools that read Google Search Console require a connected, GSC-linked site and will point you to Profile → Websites if none exists.

You don't call these by name; you ask for the outcome and Edith picks the tool. Broadly, the toolkit covers:

Each run renders as an inline card on the canvas and (where relevant) is saved to the site's Toolkit Runs / Keyword Research / Domain Audits tabs so you can come back to it.

These tools debit credits. The research and audit tools call paid data providers, so each run draws on your workspace credit pool. Reading the saved results later is free.

Writing an SEO article

The headline capability is the article pipeline. Ask Edith to write an article (from the studio, or the launcher's “Write an article” entry) and it runs an end-to-end job:

  1. Research — pulls the SERP, related searches and competitor angles for your target keyword.
  2. Plan — builds an outline / brief from that research.
  3. Write — drafts the full article (on Claude Opus).
  4. Analyze — scores the draft for SEO and readability, and can auto-fix weak spots.

The article is filed under your connected website and appears on the site dashboard's Articles tab. Opening it launches the article viewer, organised into tabs — Optimize, Brief, Gaps/Gains, Research and Inter-linking.

Inter-linking — internal links inserted into the draft

What it is — the reason connecting your site matters. On the Inter-linking tab, the article viewer shows “Suggested Internal Links from Your Site” — pages from your connected site's index that semantically match the article, each with a % match and a suggested anchor phrase. Why it matters — internal links are core SEO, and here they're real links to your pages, not invented ones.

  • Insert — finds the first unlinked occurrence of the anchor phrase in the draft and turns it into a Markdown link to your page, saving it into the article. The button then reflects whether it was inserted, already there, or the anchor wasn't found.
  • Copy MD link — grabs the Markdown link to paste wherever you like.
  • If you haven't connected a site, the tab shows a prompt to “Connect your website's sitemap” first — no connected pages means no suggestions.

Worked example: connect your site in Profile → Websites and let it crawl → open SEO & Content and ask Edith to “write an article targeting ‘bathroom tile ideas'” → when it's ready, open it from the site's Articles tab → on the Inter-linking tab, click Insert on the strongest matches to weave links to your own tile pages into the copy → export or publish the finished Markdown.

Gating & cost recap

  • Pro add-on. The toolkit needs the seo-toolkit module enabled on the workspace (plus everyday agent.use). Without it, the launcher shows an upsell card.
  • Metered. Research/audit tools debit credits per run; the article pipeline debits for the Claude Opus write. Reading saved dashboards and re-opening articles is free.
  • Better with a connected site. Connecting a website (free to do; crawling spends credits) unlocks internal-link suggestions and first-party Search Console tools — see Connected Websites.
