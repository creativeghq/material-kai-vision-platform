Documents · Invoices

/finance /finance/invoices/:id finance.manage

An invoice is a sales document you raise to bill a customer. The Invoices tab is the list of every sales invoice you've raised — where you start a new one, check what's still owed, re-open a past invoice, and (when enabled) transmit each document to the tax authority. This guide covers the list view, the full step-by-step of creating an invoice in the editor, the issue-time options, and what an issued invoice changes elsewhere on the platform.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → the Invoices tab. Click New to start one, or click any row to open its detail page at /finance/invoices/:id.

Before you issue your first invoice, set up Finance Settings — your business identity, a numbering series, your income categories, and (if you transmit to the tax authority) e-invoicing. Those settings supply the issuer details, the legal sequential number, and the category pickers you'll use here.

The Invoices list

What it is — the list of every sales invoice you've raised. When you'd use it — to bill a customer, to check what's still owed, or to find and re-open a past invoice. The toolbar and columns are built so you can read the state of each invoice at a glance.

Toolbar

  • New — start a new invoice in the editor (detailed below).
  • Sync from myDATA — pulls the documents the tax authority already has on file for your business, so your own already-filed invoices appear and reconcile without re-keying them. Clicking it asks which period to fetch first — a preset or your own From/To dates — so the pull stays bounded to the months you care about. It only works once you've entered your myDATA inbox credentials in Finance Settings → Documents.
  • Income category — opens the manager for the income category list used across the document lists, filters and reports. These same categories are also editable inline on a document header, so you can keep your books consistent without leaving the document you're working on. See Finance settings.
  • Filters — narrow the list, for example to one income category.
  • Search — by invoice number or customer when the history gets long.

The list is newest first — by issue day, then by legal number — and pages through long histories.

Columns

The columns let you read the state of each invoice at a glance — note the Due column (what's still outstanding) and mD (whether it has reached the tax authority):

The invoice detail page

/finance/invoices/:id

What it is — the record page of a single invoice, reached by clicking any row in the Invoices tab. The list shows you the summary; the detail page is where you act on the document. The back arrow returns to Finance → Invoices.

Header actions

  • Preview — the invoice as printed, on a paper-width sheet that scales down on a narrow screen.
  • Mark Issued — on a draft: assigns the legal number and issue date and, unless automatic sending is off, transmits it to AADE.
  • Record payment — on an issued invoice: allocate money received against it.
  • More actions (⋮) — the same menu as the list row: Edit invoice (while allowed, see below), Open customer view, Copy customer link, Send via WhatsApp, Send email, Download PDF, Download EN 16931 XML, Submit to myDATA, myDATA details, Copy MARK, Card payment on terminal, Issue credit note, Link to a project, Change category, Change description, Exclude from / include in P&L, Duplicate as draft, Save as template and History.

What the page shows

  • Details — status (with a With shipping tag when the invoice is also the delivery note), the customer and their VAT number, document type, series, issue and due dates, establishment, payment method and note, links to the source order or quote, the Project the invoice belongs to, and the Exclude from P&L switch.
  • Fiscal record — the MARK, UID and authentication code once AADE has accepted the document, with a send button while it has none.
  • Customer link — copy, open or send on WhatsApp the page the customer sees without an account. It is shown on drafts too, since a draft is the shareable pre-invoice.
  • Line Items — with cost and margin where the product cost is known, and a Net / VAT / Withholding / Total footer; then Payments, Credit Notes and the Linked records panel.

When AADE refuses an invoice a banner above the content gives AADE's reason. The invoice has no MARK, so fix the cause with Edit invoice and send it again under the same number. A banner also warns when a document has been awaiting transmission for more than 24 hours.

Editing an issued invoice

An invoice stays editable until myDATA returns a MARK — so a rejected or never-sent invoice can be corrected rather than voided. Editing keeps the legal number, type, series and issue date, and re-takes the stock the old lines moved. Edit is no longer offered once the invoice has a MARK, is awaiting a transmission, or has payments or credit notes against it; from then on a correction is a credit note.

Exclude from P&L

An invoice marked Exclude from P&L stays on record and still counts for VAT, myDATA, what the customer owes and cash — it is only left out of profit, revenue and expense totals (the P&L, sales reports and project P&L). The flag can be set after a MARK. The same switch exists on expenses, orders, quotes and other records that reach a P&L; credit notes follow the document they correct.

Payments and other actions

  • Card payment on terminal — in the actions menu of a transmitted invoice: sign for one of your registered terminals, charge the amount still due (Card or IRIS), enter the transaction id, and Terminal approved — file it.
  • Issue the 8.4 — on a wholesale invoice settled on a terminal, the detail page flags the missing 8.4 card receipt and issues it, linked to the invoice.
  • The QR code on the PDF opens the provider's filed copy of the document; the in-app verify links go to AADE.
  • The customer link opens the public invoice page, which shows the full invoice, its filed facts (MARK, UID, authentication code) and Download PDF — even after it is paid, cancelled or credited.
  • Download EN 16931 XML — the invoice as a European-standard UBL 2.1 e-invoice file, for a buyer whose system imports one.

How to create an invoice

How to open it: from Finance → Invoices, click New. The editor is one form laid out in rows, with a Live Preview of the printed document beside it on wide screens. You only need a handful of fields for a basic invoice (customer, a line, category); the rest cover the compliance and delivery options Greek invoicing requires. The sections below walk down the form in order.

Billed To

Search your companies and contacts, or use + Add new client (or the Add “…” as a new client suggestion under what you typed) to create one on the spot. On an invoice (1.x / 2.x) the search offers only businesses — companies, and contacts with a VAT number — because AADE rejects an invoice to a buyer with no VAT number; a private individual gets a receipt, where the search is unrestricted. A business customer's VAT can be checked with Validate via VIES.

In New client, a VAT number typed with a country prefix (GB…, DE…, US…) switches the country. For a country no registry covers, or when the ΑΑΔΕ / VIES lookup finds nothing, the billing address fields open so you can enter the client by hand.

Foreign buyers are invoiced at 0% VAT. When the buyer is abroad, every line is set to 0% with the regime's exemption cause (art. 29 for an export of goods, art. 33 for an intra-community supply of goods); on a service line you pick the cause. Intra-community treatment needs the buyer's VAT number. A rate you set by hand is kept.

Terms

Payment Terms (Days), Currency (with an Exchange Rate → EUR for a foreign currency), Default VAT %, the income Category, and Paid Upfront (Cash) — which takes the cash discount set in Settings → Pricing off every line and sets the terms to 0 days.

Shipping

Turn on Invoice with shipping when the invoice is also the delivery note (Τιμολόγιο – Δελτίο Αποστολής); it is filed with myDATA as one. Fill the Loading Place (Use HQ details) and Delivery Place (Customer delivery address) as street, number, postal code and city — an incomplete address is refused — plus the transport date and time, vehicle, responsible person and Purpose.

Items / Services

Each row has Description, Qty, Unit, Price, Discount €, VAT, and shows Net and Total incl. VAT. Type three or more letters in the description to find a product or service from your catalogue, with its image; picking one fills the price, the unit and the cost. For a product with variants, choose the colour and size on the row itself — changing the variant re-prices the line (unless you typed a price) and the row shows the free stock for that variant. A line discount larger than quantity × price is refused.

  • Prices include VAT — whether the prices you enter are VAT-inclusive.
  • Row defaults — a unit, VAT category, income type and income category applied to every row.
  • Add row — add a blank line.
  • Each line's details show its income classification — your choice, else the product's own codes, else the document type's default — so you see what will be filed.

A catalogue line's sell price comes from the resale pricing engine — the category markup, then the customer's pricing level discount — so a catalogue cost becomes the customer's sell price automatically.

Cash (paid-upfront) discount. When an invoice is created from an accepted quote that carried a paid-upfront (cash) discount, the invoice inherits that cash-discount line — it shows as its own line in the totals so the customer sees the upfront-payment saving on the invoice too.

Withholding & Fees, Charges & Levies

Withholding Tax (offered on sales invoices) and the Digital Transaction Fee; then Charges & Levies (Document Level) for taxes stated once on the document rather than per line. Leave these blank unless they apply.

When Issued

  • Send to AADE on Issue — on by default, following the workspace setting Send to AADE automatically when an invoice is created in Settings → e-Invoicing. A failed send raises a notification.
  • Send by Email on Create.
  • Include in MYF Report.
  • Move Stock on Issue — decrement the warehouse.
  • Exclude from P&L — see above.

Public Sector (B2G)

For sales to a public body (not shown on retail documents): Contract Reference (ΑΔΑΜ), Buyer Reference, Buyer Legal Registration Id, Buyer Identifier (ΚΗΜΔΗΣ/KAE), Budget Identifier, Payment Due Date, a CPV code (used for lines whose product has none) and an optional Purchase Order Reference. Your IBAN and the delivery details are sent with it.

When everything's right, click Preview to check the PDF, then Create invoice — or Save draft to keep it without a legal number and issue it later from its page.

The legal number. Each document type has its own numbering range, created automatically on the first document if you haven't set one up, and an invoice from a channel with its own range (e.g. a Skroutz order) uses that range. The number printed on the PDF is the one sent to myDATA. Set ranges in Settings → Documents; see Greek e-invoicing.

Draft vs. issued

Save draft keeps the invoice without a legal number — it can be edited freely and shared as a pre-invoice. Create invoice (or Mark Issued on a draft) assigns the legal number and date and, by default, sends it to AADE. An issued invoice can still be corrected until AADE returns a MARK; after that it is a legal record.

Every issued document is emailed

An issued invoice or receipt is emailed to the customer automatically, carrying its online link. Credit notes and delivery notes are emailed once AADE assigns the MARK (so the PDF carries it), and sales and purchase orders when they are confirmed. Each document is sent once; a party with no email address is skipped. The Sent column on the list shows whether it arrived.

Payment details on the document — IBAN, @revtag & the RF code

An issued invoice (and a proforma) carries a payment block so the customer can pay by bank transfer even if they never open the online pay link. What appears there fills itself from your payment setup:

  • IBAN — any bank account you've marked show on invoice prints here. An account added from Revolut Business prints only once you switch on show on invoice for it.
  • Revolut @revtag — if set, a “Revolut · @tag — instant in-app payment” line appears next to the IBANs.
  • RF bank-transfer code — if you use Viva with the RF method, a structured RF reference is printed next to the IBANs.

Why the RF code matters — it reconciles itself. The RF reference is minted per invoice for the amount due, and it's the same code on the PDF, the email and the online pay page. When the customer's bank transfer arrives quoting it, the invoice is settled automatically — no manual matching. It's amount-aware (if you edit a proforma's total before issuing, the code refreshes) and it only appears when there's a balance to collect and Viva is connected for EUR; otherwise the document simply omits it. See Settings → Payments to enable it.

Worked example: create & issue an invoice

The full happy path, end to end, from opening the tab to handing the customer a way to pay:

  1. Open Finance from the top navigation, then select the Invoices tab.
  2. Click New.
  3. Pick the customer. The VAT number is validated; for Greek customers the details can be enriched automatically from ΑΑΔΕ — this also helps avoid a rejected submission later.
  4. Add line items — type three letters to pick a catalogue product or service, or write a custom line — each with a quantity, unit, price and VAT rate.
  5. Assign an income category from the document header (categories are editable and can be managed inline) so the invoice lands in the right report bucket.
  6. Review the totals (net, VAT and gross) to confirm the figures.
  7. Click Create invoice. On issue it is transmitted to ΑΑΔΕ / myDATA and appears in Receivables.
  8. The customer receives it by email with its online link; you can also copy the customer link from the invoice page.

What it affects elsewhere

Issued invoices flow into Receivables and the Dashboard automatically, and (when enabled) transmit to ΑΑΔΕ / myDATA on issue. Nothing here is a dead end — every issued invoice moves a balance or a report.
