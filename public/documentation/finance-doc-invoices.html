Documents · Invoices

/finance /finance/invoices/:id finance.manage

An invoice is a sales document you raise to bill a customer. The Invoices tab is the list of every sales invoice you've raised — where you start a new one, check what's still owed, re-open a past invoice, and (when enabled) transmit each document to the tax authority. This guide covers the list view, the full step-by-step of creating an invoice in the editor, the issue-time options, and what an issued invoice changes elsewhere on the platform.

Where it is

Top navigation → Finance → the Invoices tab. Click New to start one, or click any row to open its detail page at /finance/invoices/:id.

Before you issue your first invoice, set up Finance Settings — your business identity, a numbering series, your income categories, and (if you transmit to the tax authority) e-invoicing. Those settings supply the issuer details, the legal sequential number, and the category pickers you'll use here.

The Invoices list

What it is — the list of every sales invoice you've raised. When you'd use it — to bill a customer, to check what's still owed, or to find and re-open a past invoice. The toolbar and columns are built so you can read the state of each invoice at a glance.

Toolbar

  • New — start a new invoice in the editor (detailed below).
  • Sync from myDATA — pulls the documents the tax authority already has on file for your business, so your own already-filed invoices appear and reconcile without re-keying them. Clicking it asks which period to fetch first — a preset or your own From/To dates — so the pull stays bounded to the months you care about. It only works once you've entered your myDATA inbox credentials in Finance Settings → Documents.
  • Income category — opens the manager for the income category list used across the document lists, filters and reports. These same categories are also editable inline on a document header, so you can keep your books consistent without leaving the document you're working on. See Finance settings.
  • All categories filter — narrow the list to one income category.
  • Search — by invoice number or customer when the history gets long.

Columns

The columns let you read the state of each invoice at a glance — note the Due column (what's still outstanding) and mD (whether it has reached the tax authority):

The invoice detail page

/finance/invoices/:id

What it is — the full view of a single invoice, reached by clicking any row in the Invoices tab. Why it matters — the list shows you the summary; the detail page is where you act on the document. What you can do here: see the customer, line items, taxes and totals, and the status; issue/transmit it, generate the PDF, and share a payment link so the customer can pay online.

How to create an invoice

How to open it: from Finance → Invoices, click New. The same editor builds several document types — Sales invoice, Service invoice, Receipt, Delivery note — so pick the type at the top first; the rest of the form adapts to it. The sections below walk down the form in order. You only need a handful of fields for a basic invoice (Client, a line item, Category); the rest cover the compliance and delivery options Greek invoicing requires.

Billed to

Why it's first — the document needs a recipient before anything else. If the customer isn't in your contacts yet, you can create them without leaving the form.

Items / services

The heart of the document — the lines that make up what you're billing. Add a blank row and type it in, or pull a priced line straight from your catalogue so you don't re-key product details. The Prices include VAT toggle tells the editor how to read the figures you enter, which changes how net/VAT/gross are calculated.

FieldWhat it does
Description, Qty, Price, Disc., Unit, VAT categoryThe per-row fields for each item or service line.
Prices include VATToggle for whether the entered prices are VAT-inclusive.
Add row / Pick from productsAdd a blank line, or pull a line from the catalogue.

Pick from products proposes a sell price using the resale pricing engine — the category markup, then the customer's pricing level discount — so a catalogue cost becomes the customer's sell price automatically.

Cash (paid-upfront) discount. When an invoice is created from an accepted quote that carried a paid-upfront (cash) discount, the invoice inherits that cash-discount line — it shows as its own line in the totals so the customer sees the upfront-payment saving on the invoice too.

Document & delivery options (issue-time toggles)

What these do — these are the toggles that decide what happens the moment you create the document: whether it transmits, whether it moves stock, whether it's emailed, and — most importantly — whether it's issued as a legal record or saved as a draft.

  • Submit to myDATA on issue — transmit electronically when issued.
  • Print online code / QR.
  • Include in MYF report.
  • Move stock on issue — decrement warehouse.
  • Print terms & comments.
  • Send by email on create / Send by SMS on create.
  • Info box (printed, ≤250 chars) — extra printed note (warranty/delivery terms).
  • Issue now — assigns the legal number + date (otherwise saved as draft).

When everything's right, click Preview to check the PDF, then Create invoice.

"Issue now" vs. draft

Draft vs. issued: "Issue now" assigns the official myDATA number — drafts can be edited freely; issued documents are legal records. Leave it off while you're still working on the document, and turn it on only when the document is final.

Worked example: create & issue an invoice

The full happy path, end to end, from opening the tab to handing the customer a way to pay:

  1. Open Finance from the top navigation, then select the Invoices tab.
  2. Start a new invoice.
  3. Pick the customer. The VAT number is validated; for Greek customers the details can be enriched automatically from ΑΑΔΕ — this also helps avoid a rejected submission later.
  4. Add line items — choose catalogue products or add custom lines — each with a quantity, price and VAT rate.
  5. Assign an income category from the document header (categories are editable and can be managed inline) so the invoice lands in the right report bucket.
  6. Review the totals (net, VAT and gross) to confirm the figures.
  7. Issue the invoice. On issue it is transmitted to ΑΑΔΕ / myDATA and appears in Receivables.
  8. Generate the PDF and share a payment link with the customer.

What it affects elsewhere

Issued invoices flow into Receivables and the Dashboard automatically, and (when enabled) transmit to ΑΑΔΕ / myDATA on issue. Nothing here is a dead end — every issued invoice moves a balance or a report.
