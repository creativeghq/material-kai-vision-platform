About This Product

VALENOVA by SG NY is the new Signature collaboration from Harmony. Stacy Garcia, a New York-based designer, draws inspiration from the elegance of folded paper, redefining the aesthetics of wall covering. Each tile captures the unique essence of textures, reliefs, and shapes that only paper can offer, creating a tactile and visual experience.

A small-format 11.8 × 11.8 cm white-body ceramic wall tile with a three-dimensional faceted "LAYER TECH" relief, offered in four colourways — White, Sand, Taupe and Clay — across 12 distinct patterns per box.

Run Context

Job cb137e70 is the first fully-green single-product E2E after resolving the PaddleOCR-VL cold-start hang. Root cause was two-fold: PaddleOCR-VL's default use_queues=True spawns a 3-thread queue pipeline whose _worker_vlm thread deadlocks at container startup; and even on the synchronous path the first VLM generation on a cold GPU intermittently wedges. Fix: force use_queues=False everywhere + a bounded warmup in @modal.enter that raises on a wedge so Modal recycles the container (only a warmed container reports healthy). GPU moved L4→A10G; max_new_tokens capped at 2048.
